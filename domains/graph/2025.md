# Graph Learning — 2025

- **GOFA: A Generative One-For-All Model for Joint Graph Language Modeling** — ICLR 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09709)] [[Code](https://github.com/JiaruiFeng/GOFA)]
  Interleaves GNN layers into a frozen LLM and pre-trains on graph-level next-word prediction and structural tasks, enabling zero-shot graph reasoning across diverse domains.

- **LayerDAG: A Layerwise Autoregressive Diffusion Model for Directed Acyclic Graph Generation** — ICLR 2025 Spotlight [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02322)] [[Code](https://github.com/Graph-COM/LayerDAG)]
  Decomposes DAGs into layered bipartite graphs and applies autoregressive diffusion for scalable and expressive DAG generation up to 400 nodes.

- **Joint Graph Rewiring and Feature Denoising via Spectral Resonance** — ICLR 2025 Oral [`Theory`] [`GNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07191)] [[Code](https://github.com/jlinki/JDR)]
  Jointly denoises node features and rewires graph topology by aligning the leading spectral spaces of graph and feature matrices to improve downstream GNN performance.

- **Homomorphism Expressivity of Spectral Invariant Graph Neural Networks** — ICLR 2025 [`Theory`] [`GNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00485)]
  Provides a comprehensive quantitative analysis of spectral invariant GNNs through the lens of homomorphism expressivity, settling open questions on their subgraph-counting power.

- **DeFoG: Discrete Flow Matching for Graph Generation** — ICML 2025 Oral [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04263)] [[Code](https://github.com/manuelmlmadeira/DeFoG)]
  Applies discrete flow matching to graph generation with a disentangled training-sampling formulation, achieving state-of-the-art results with 5-10% of the sampling steps of diffusion baselines.

- **HGOT: Self-supervised Heterogeneous Graph Neural Network with Optimal Transport** — ICML 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`GNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02619)]
  Uses optimal transport to align branch meta-path views with an aggregated central view for augmentation-free self-supervised learning on heterogeneous graphs.

- **RAG4GFM: Bridging Knowledge Gaps in Graph Foundation Models through Graph Retrieval Augmented Generation** — NeurIPS 2025 Oral [`Foundation Model`] [`GNN`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tirl2l9oKg)] [[Code](https://github.com/Matrixmax/RAG4GFM)]
  Introduces the RAG paradigm for graph foundation models with hierarchical graph indexing, task-aware retrieval, and graph fusion enhancement for efficient knowledge updating.

- **GFM-RAG: Graph Foundation Model for Retrieval Augmented Generation** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01113)] [[Code](https://github.com/RManLuo/gfm-rag)]
  Builds a knowledge graph index from documents and uses a pre-trained 8M-parameter graph foundation model to reason over graph structure for retrieval-augmented question answering.
