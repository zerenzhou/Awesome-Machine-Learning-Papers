- **G-reasoner: Foundation Models for Unified Reasoning over Graph-structured Knowledge** — ICLR 2026 [`Foundation Model`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24276)] [[Code](https://github.com/RManLuo/gfm-rag)]

- **GFM-RAG: Graph Foundation Model for Retrieval Augmented Generation** — ICLR 2026 [`Foundation Model`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01113)] [[Code](https://github.com/RManLuo/gfm-rag)]
  Trains a graph neural network-based retriever on 60 knowledge graphs with 14M triples to enable zero-shot retrieval-augmented generation on unseen datasets without fine-tuning.

- **BoGrape: Bayesian Optimization over Graphs with Shortest-Path Encodings** — ICLR 2026 [`Molecular`] [`GNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05642)]
  Formulates Bayesian optimization acquisition functions for shortest-path graph kernels as mixed-integer programs, enabling principled global exploration of graph-structured search spaces for molecular design.
