# Deep Learning for Signal Processing — 2024

- **Large Brain Model for Learning Generic Representations with Tremendous EEG Data in BCI** — ICLR 2024 Spotlight [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18765)] [[Code](https://github.com/935963004/LaBraM)]
  First large-scale EEG foundation model (LaBraM) pre-trained on ~2,500 hours of EEG from ~20 datasets via vector-quantized neural spectrum prediction, achieving SOTA across diverse BCI tasks.

- **Large-scale Training of Foundation Models for Wearable Biosignals** — ICLR 2024 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.05409)]
  First foundation model for PPG and ECG biosignals pre-trained via self-supervised contrastive learning on 141K participants, demonstrating strong transfer to cardiovascular and activity tasks.

- **SpectralGPT: Spectral Remote Sensing Foundation Model** — IEEE TPAMI 2024 [`Radar & Remote Sensing`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07113)] [[Code](https://github.com/danfenghong/IEEE_TPAMI_SpectralGPT)]
  First foundation model for spectral remote sensing using a 3D generative pre-trained transformer on 1M+ spectral images with progressive training across varying resolutions and regions.

- **SkySense: A Multi-Modal Remote Sensing Foundation Model Towards Universal Interpretation for Earth Observation Imagery** — CVPR 2024 [`Radar & Remote Sensing`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10115)] [[Code](https://github.com/Jack-bo1220/SkySense)]
  Largest multi-modal RS foundation model pre-trained on 21.5M temporal sequences of optical and SAR data, surpassing 18 prior RSFMs across 16 datasets and 7 tasks.

- **Solving Inverse Problems with Latent Diffusion Models via Hard Data Consistency** — ICLR 2024 Spotlight [`Inverse Problems`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08123)] [[Code](https://github.com/soominkwon/resample)]
  Proposed ReSample, solving general inverse problems using pre-trained latent diffusion models by incorporating hard data consistency with a resampling scheme to maintain the noisy data manifold.
