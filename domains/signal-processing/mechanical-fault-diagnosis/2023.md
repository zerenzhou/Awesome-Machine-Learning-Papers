# Mechanical Fault Diagnosis — 2023

- **Physics-Informed Interpretable Wavelet Weight Initialization and Balanced Dynamic Adaptive Threshold for Intelligent Fault Diagnosis of Rolling Bearings (EWSNet)** — Journal of Manufacturing Systems 2023 [`Mechanical Fault Diagnosis`] [`Physics-Informed`]
  [[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmsy.2023.08.014)] [[Code](https://github.com/liguge/EWSNet)]
  Injected wavelet priors into CNN kernel initialization and combined with a balanced dynamic adaptive soft-threshold module, yielding interpretable and robust bearing diagnosis under variable speed.

- **Deep Causal Factorization Network: A Novel Domain Generalization Method for Cross-Machine Bearing Fault Diagnosis (DCFN)** — MSSP 2023 [`Mechanical Fault Diagnosis`] [`Domain Generalization`]
  [[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2023.110228)]
  Used a structural causal model to disentangle causal (fault-relevant) from non-causal (machine-specific) factors, delivering strong cross-machine generalization on CWRU↔Paderborn protocols.

- **FaultFormer: Pretraining Transformers for Adaptable Bearing Fault Classification** — arXiv 2023 / IEEE Access 2024 [`Mechanical Fault Diagnosis`] [`Self-Supervised`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02380)] [[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3399670)]
  Applied masked-signal pretraining to a transformer backbone on vibration data, demonstrating few-shot and cross-dataset transfer and becoming an early major SSL-transformer work for bearing fault diagnosis.
