# Mechanical Fault Diagnosis — 2024

- **ClassBD: Classifier-Guided Neural Blind Deconvolution for Bearing Fault Diagnosis under Noisy Conditions** — MSSP 2024 [`Mechanical Fault Diagnosis`] [`Physics-Informed`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15341)] [[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2024.111750)] [[Code](https://github.com/asdvfghg/ClassBD)]
  Coupled a differentiable quadratic-convolution blind-deconvolution filter with physics-informed losses (kurtosis + l2/l4), achieving SOTA on heavy-noise CWRU (−4 dB SNR), Paderborn, and JNU while remaining interpretable.

- **BearLLM: A Prior-Knowledge-Enhanced Bearing Health Management Framework with Unified Vibration Signal Representation** — AAAI 2025 (arXiv Aug 2024) [`Mechanical Fault Diagnosis`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11281)] [[Code](https://github.com/SIA-IDE/BearLLM)]
  Unified vibration embeddings with LLM reasoning, using a single pretrained model to reach reported SOTA across 9 public bearing benchmarks — the first AAAI-accepted LLM-for-bearing framework.

- **RmGPT: A Foundation Model with Generative Pre-trained Transformer for Fault Diagnosis and Prognosis in Rotating Machinery** — arXiv 2024 [`Mechanical Fault Diagnosis`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17604)] [[Code](https://github.com/Pandalin98/RmGPT)]
  Proposed a unified tokenizer (Signal / Prompt / Task / Fault tokens) for generative pretraining across joint diagnosis and prognosis, reporting strong one-shot 16-class classification — one of the first generative foundation models for rotating-machinery PHM.

- **FD-LLM: Large Language Model for Fault Diagnosis of Machines** — arXiv Dec 2024 / Advanced Engineering Informatics 2025 [`Mechanical Fault Diagnosis`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01218)] [[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aei.2025.103208)]
  Fine-tuned Llama-3 with string-token and statistical encodings of vibration signals, matching or beating CNN baselines across CWRU and HUST benchmarks and validating LLM-as-classifier for PHM.
