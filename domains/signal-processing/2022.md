# Deep Learning for Signal Processing — 2022

- **Solving Inverse Problems in Medical Imaging with Score-Based Generative Models** — ICLR 2022 [`Inverse Problems`] [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.08005)] [[Code](https://github.com/yang-song/score_inverse_problems)]
  Used score-based generative models as learned priors to solve CT and MRI inverse problems without task-specific training, matching or exceeding supervised baselines.

- **SatMAE: Pre-training Transformers for Temporal and Multi-Spectral Satellite Imagery** — NeurIPS 2022 [`Radar & Remote Sensing`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08051)] [[Code](https://github.com/sustainlab-group/SatMAE)]
  Adapted masked autoencoder pre-training to multi-spectral and temporal satellite imagery with spectral positional encodings, improving downstream RS tasks by up to 14%.

- **Nonlinear Transform Source-Channel Coding for Semantic Communications** — IEEE JSAC 2022 [`Communications`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10961)] [[Code](https://github.com/wsxtyrdd/NTSCC_JSAC22)]
  Introduced a learned nonlinear analysis transform as a semantic prior for joint source-channel coding, enabling content-aware rate-distortion optimization beyond vanilla DeepJSCC.

- **Deep Unfolding with Normalizing Flow Priors for Inverse Problems** — IEEE TSP 2022 [`Algorithm Unrolling`] [`Inverse Problems`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.02848)]
  Embedded normalizing flow generative models as learned proximal operators within an unrolled proximal gradient algorithm for compressed sensing and image reconstruction.
