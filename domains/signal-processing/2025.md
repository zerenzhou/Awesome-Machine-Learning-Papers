# Deep Learning for Signal Processing — 2025

- **NeuroLM: A Universal Multi-task Foundation Model for Bridging the Gap between Language and EEG Signals** — ICLR 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00101)] [[Code](https://github.com/935963004/NeuroLM)]
  First multi-task EEG foundation model treating EEG as a foreign language for LLMs, with the 1.7B-parameter NeuroLM-XL pre-trained on ~25,000 hours of EEG achieving SOTA across six BCI tasks.

- **CBraMod: A Criss-Cross Brain Foundation Model for EEG Decoding** — ICLR 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07236)] [[Code](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod)]
  Proposed a criss-cross transformer separately modeling spatial and temporal EEG dependencies, pre-trained via patch-based masked reconstruction, achieving SOTA across 10 BCI tasks on 12 datasets.

- **Diffusion State-Guided Projected Gradient for Inverse Problems** — ICLR 2025 [`Inverse Problems`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03463)] [[Code](https://github.com/Anima-Lab/DiffStateGrad)]
  Projects measurement guidance gradients onto a low-rank subspace from intermediate diffusion states, acting as a plug-in for existing diffusion-based inverse solvers with improved robustness.

- **Invertible Diffusion Models for Compressed Sensing** — IEEE TPAMI 2025 [`Compressed Sensing`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17006)]
  Repurposed the diffusion sampling process as an end-to-end CS reconstruction model via a two-level invertible design, reducing GPU memory by up to 93.8% during training.

- **SARATR-X: Toward Building A Foundation Model for SAR Target Recognition** — IEEE TIP 2025 [`Radar & Remote Sensing`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09365)] [[Code](https://github.com/waterdisappear/SARATR-X)]
  First foundation model for SAR ATR trained on 0.18M unlabelled SAR samples via self-supervised learning with multi-scale gradient features, matching supervised methods in few-shot settings.

- **Sensitivity-Constrained Fourier Neural Operators for Forward and Inverse Problems** — ICLR 2025 [`Neural Operator`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08740)] [[Code](https://github.com/AMBehroozi/SC_Neural_Operators)]
  Introduced sensitivity-based regularization into FNO training, improving forward prediction, parameter inversion, and robustness across up to 82-dimensional parameter spaces.
