# Biomedical Signals — 2026

- **Are EEG Foundation Models Worth It? Comparative Evaluation with Traditional Decoders in Diverse BCI Tasks** — ICLR 2026 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10011450)] [[Code](https://github.com/LiuyinYang1101/STEEGFormer)]
  Head-to-head benchmark of EEG foundation models against traditional supervised decoders (EEGNet and variants) across diverse BCI tasks, exposing a pretraining-to-fine-tuning gap and calling for better large-scale EEG evaluation protocols.

- **Foundation Models for Electrocardiogram Interpretation: Clinical Implications (DeepECG-SSL)** — European Heart Journal 2026 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehaf1119)]
  Two-stage self-supervised ECG foundation model combining Wav2Vec local-feature learning with Contrastive Multi-Segment Coding for global representations, pre-trained on ~2M unlabeled ECGs and reaching micro-AUROC 0.990 internal / 0.981–0.983 external across 77 cardiac conditions, with notable gains on limited-data biomarker tasks (e.g., long-QT genotype AUROC 0.931 vs. 0.850 supervised).

- **Looking Beyond Accuracy: A Holistic Benchmark of ECG Foundation Models** — arXiv 2026 [`Biomedical`] [`Benchmark`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21830)]
  Holistic evaluation protocol for ECG foundation models that augments accuracy with representation-level analysis (SHAP and UMAP) across datasets of varying scale, exposing generalization and embedding-quality differences that aggregate metrics hide — the ECG counterpart to the 2026 EEG-FM benchmark.
