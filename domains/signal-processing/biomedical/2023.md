# Biomedical Signals — 2023

- **BrainBERT: Self-supervised Representation Learning for Intracranial Recordings** — ICLR 2023 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.14367)] [[Code](https://github.com/czlwang/BrainBERT)]
  Applied BERT-style masked pre-training to intracranial EEG spectrograms, producing reusable representations that generalize across subjects and reduce labeled data requirements by up to 85%.

- **EEG Conformer: Convolutional Transformer for EEG Decoding and Visualization** — IEEE TNSRE 2023 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9991178)] [[Code](https://github.com/eeyhsong/EEG-Conformer)]
  Unified local convolutional encoding and global self-attention in a single EEG decoder, delivering competitive results on BCI Competition IV-2a / 2b and SEED with visualization of learned spectral-temporal patterns.

- **BIOT: Biosignal Transformer for Cross-data Learning in the Wild** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10351)] [[Code](https://github.com/ycq091044/BIOT)]
  Tokenized heterogeneous biosignals (varying channels / sampling rates / lengths) into unified "biosignal sentences" to pre-train a Transformer across datasets, reaching ~0.879 AUROC on TUAB and establishing the biosignal foundation-model recipe adopted by LaBraM and successors.
