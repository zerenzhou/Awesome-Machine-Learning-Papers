# Biomedical Signals — 2024

- **Large Brain Model for Learning Generic Representations with Tremendous EEG Data in BCI** — ICLR 2024 Spotlight [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18765)] [[Code](https://github.com/935963004/LaBraM)]
  First large-scale EEG foundation model (LaBraM) pre-trained on ~2,500 hours of EEG from ~20 datasets via vector-quantized neural spectrum prediction, achieving SOTA across diverse BCI tasks (AUROC ~0.902 on TUAB, BA ~0.662 on TUEV).

- **Large-scale Training of Foundation Models for Wearable Biosignals** — ICLR 2024 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.05409)]
  First foundation model for PPG and ECG biosignals pre-trained via self-supervised contrastive learning on 141K participants, demonstrating strong transfer to cardiovascular and activity tasks.

- **EEGPT: Pretrained Transformer for Universal and Reliable Representation of EEG Signals** — NeurIPS 2024 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07721)] [[Code](https://github.com/BINE022/EEGPT)]
  Pre-trained a dual self-supervised masked-reconstruction + alignment Transformer on large-scale EEG, producing universal representations that transfer competitively across motor-imagery, sleep, and event-related tasks.

- **ECG-FM: An Open Electrocardiogram Foundation Model** — arXiv 2024 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05178)] [[Code](https://github.com/bowang-lab/ECG-FM)]
  Open-source 12-lead ECG foundation model pre-trained with masked-signal modeling on 1.5M+ ECGs, reaching macro-AUC ~0.94 on PTB-XL superclasses and competitive results on CinC 2021, with full weights and training code released.
