# Biomedical Signals — 2020

- **Automatic Diagnosis of the 12-Lead ECG Using a Deep Neural Network** — Nature Communications 2020 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01949)] [[Code](https://github.com/antonior92/automatic-ecg-diagnosis)]
  Trained a DNN on over 2 million 12-lead ECGs to diagnose 6 cardiac abnormalities, outperforming cardiology residents with F1 scores above 80% and specificity over 99%.

- **Deep Learning for ECG Analysis: Benchmarks and Insights from PTB-XL** — IEEE JBHI 2020 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13701)] [[Code](https://github.com/helme/ecg_ptbxl_benchmarking)]
  Established the standard deep-learning benchmark on PTB-XL (21k clinical 12-lead ECGs, multi-label), with xResNet1d101 reaching ~0.93 macro-AUC on the superclass task — the reference baseline for subsequent ECG foundation models.
