# Biomedical Signals — Classic Papers

Foundational and pioneering works in deep learning for biomedical signals.

- ⭐ **EEGNet: A Compact Convolutional Neural Network for EEG-Based Brain-Computer Interfaces** — Journal of Neural Engineering 2018 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08024)] [[Code](https://github.com/vlawhern/arl-eegmodels)]
  Introduced a compact CNN using depthwise and separable convolutions for EEG-based BCI, generalizing across four BCI paradigms with minimal parameters.
