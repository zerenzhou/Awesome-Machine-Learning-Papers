# Biomedical Signals — 2025

- **NeuroLM: A Universal Multi-task Foundation Model for Bridging the Gap between Language and EEG Signals** — ICLR 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00101)] [[Code](https://github.com/935963004/NeuroLM)]
  First multi-task EEG foundation model treating EEG as a foreign language for LLMs, with the 1.7B-parameter NeuroLM-XL pre-trained on ~25,000 hours of EEG achieving SOTA across six BCI tasks.

- **CBraMod: A Criss-Cross Brain Foundation Model for EEG Decoding** — ICLR 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07236)] [[Code](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod)]
  Proposed a criss-cross transformer separately modeling spatial and temporal EEG dependencies, pre-trained via patch-based masked reconstruction, achieving SOTA across 10 BCI tasks on 12 datasets.

- **BrainOmni: A Brain Foundation Model for Unified EEG and MEG Signals** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18185)] [[Code](https://github.com/OpenTSLab/BrainOmni)]
  First foundation model unifying EEG and MEG via a device-conditioned BrainTokenizer plus a Sensor Encoder, pre-trained on ~2,653 hours of standardized EEG/MEG and generalizing zero-shot to unseen devices.

- **NeurIPT: Foundation Model for Neural Interfaces** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=D4hcJPkJ3y)]
  EEG foundation model combining Amplitude-Aware Masked Pretraining (AAMP), a progressive mixture-of-experts backbone, electrode-coordinate spatial conditioning, and intra-/inter-lobe pooling, reaching SOTA across 9 BCI datasets.

- **REVE: A Foundation Model for EEG — Adapting to Any Setup with Large-Scale Pretraining on 25,000 Subjects** — arXiv 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21585)] [[Project](https://brain-bzh.github.io/reve/)]
  Masked-autoencoder EEG foundation model with a 4D positional encoding that handles arbitrary electrode montages, pre-trained on 60,000+ hours across 92 datasets from 25,000 subjects — the largest-subject-count EEG pretraining to date — reaching SOTA on 10 downstream BCI tasks.

- **ALFEE: Adaptive Large Foundation Model for EEG Representation** — arXiv 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06291)] [[Code](https://github.com/xw1216/ALFEE)]
  Hybrid-attention EEG foundation model with a channel encoder handling variable channel counts plus a temporal encoder and dual-domain reconstruction, pre-trained on ~25,000 hours of EEG with explicit demonstration of EEG scaling laws.

- **ECGFounder: An Electrocardiogram Foundation Model Built on over 10 Million Recordings** — NEJM AI 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://ai.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/AIoa2401033)] [[Code](https://github.com/PKUDigitalHealth/ECGFounder)]
  ECG foundation model pre-trained on 10.77M ECGs from 1.8M subjects (Harvard–Emory database) covering 150 diagnostic labels, reaching AUROC >0.95 on 80 diagnoses — ~7× the data scale of ECG-FM — targeting clinical and wearable deployment.

- **Pulse-PPG: An Open-Source Field-Trained PPG Foundation Model** — arXiv 2025 [`Biomedical`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01108)]
  First PPG foundation model trained on real-world field data (120 participants over 100 days) with relative contrastive learning, improving lab → field → clinical generalization over prior lab-only PPG models.

- **LibriBrain & the PNPL 2025 Speech Detection / Phoneme Classification Competition** — arXiv 2025 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10165)]
  Released LibriBrain, the largest within-subject MEG corpus to date, together with the `pnpl` Python library and two non-invasive speech-decoding benchmarks, aiming for an "ImageNet moment" for neural speech decoding.
