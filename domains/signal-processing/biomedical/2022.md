# Biomedical Signals — 2022

- **Physics-Informed Attention Temporal Convolutional Network for EEG-Based Motor Imagery Classification (ATCNet)** — IEEE TII 2022 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9852687)] [[Code](https://github.com/Altaheri/EEG-ATCNet)]
  Proposed ATCNet combining convolutional sliding-window encoding with multi-head self-attention and temporal convolutional decoding, reaching ~85.4% subject-dependent accuracy on BCI Competition IV-2a and becoming a leading MI-BCI baseline.

- **XSleepNet: Multi-View Sequential Model for Automatic Sleep Staging** — IEEE TPAMI 2022 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05492)] [[Code](https://github.com/pquochuy/xsleepnet)]
  Jointly learned from raw EEG signals and time-frequency images via a multi-view sequential architecture, achieving ~87.6% accuracy / κ≈0.82 on SHHS and strong Sleep-EDF-78 results.
