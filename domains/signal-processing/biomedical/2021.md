# Biomedical Signals — 2021

- **An Attention-based Deep Learning Approach for Sleep Stage Classification with Single-Channel EEG (AttnSleep)** — IEEE TNSRE 2021 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.11043)] [[Code](https://github.com/emadeldeen24/AttnSleep)]
  Combined multi-resolution CNNs with an attention-based sequence model for automatic sleep staging from single-channel EEG, achieving ~84.4% accuracy / 78.1 MF1 on Sleep-EDF-20 and becoming a standard sleep-staging baseline.
