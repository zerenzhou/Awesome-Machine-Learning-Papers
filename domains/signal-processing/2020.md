# Deep Learning for Signal Processing — 2020

- **Model-Driven Deep Learning for MIMO Detection** — IEEE TSP 2020 [`Algorithm Unrolling`] [`Communications`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.09439)] [[Code](https://github.com/hehengtao/OAMP-Net)]
  Proposed OAMP-Net by unfolding orthogonal AMP into a trainable network for MIMO detection and joint channel estimation, with far fewer parameters than data-driven approaches.

- **DeepMUSIC: Multiple Signal Classification via Deep Learning** — IEEE Sensors Letters 2020 [`Radar & Remote Sensing`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04357)]
  Designed multiple CNNs each dedicated to angular subregions for multi-source DOA estimation, achieving superior accuracy and lower complexity than both classical and DL-based methods.

- **Automatic Diagnosis of the 12-Lead ECG Using a Deep Neural Network** — Nature Communications 2020 [`Biomedical`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01949)] [[Code](https://github.com/antonior92/automatic-ecg-diagnosis)]
  Trained a DNN on over 2 million 12-lead ECGs to diagnose 6 cardiac abnormalities, outperforming cardiology residents with F1 scores above 80% and specificity over 99%.
