# Natural Language Processing — 2026

- **Empirical Analysis of Decoding Biases in Masked Diffusion Models** — ACL 2026 (Oral) [`LLM`] [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.311/)] [[Code](https://github.com/NEUIR/Uncode)]
  Analyzed rigid boundary and trivial token biases in masked diffusion decoding and introduced training-free calibration with positional-trajectory and content-informativeness priors.

- **Chunks as Arms: Multi-Armed Bandit-Guided Sampling for Long-Context LLM Preference Optimization** — ACL 2026 (Oral) [`LLM`] [`Alignment`]
  [[Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.161/)] [[Code](https://github.com/NEUIR/LongMab-PO)]
  Used multi-armed-bandit feedback to sample context chunks and construct diverse preference pairs for long-context DPO.

- **Does Reinforcement Learning Really Incentivize Reasoning Capacity in LLMs Beyond the Base Model?** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Reasoning`] [`Alignment`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13837)]
  Showed that RLVR improves sampling efficiency but does not elicit fundamentally new reasoning patterns beyond those already in the base model's distribution.

- **SPIRAL: Self-Play on Zero-Sum Games Incentivizes Reasoning via Multi-Agent Multi-Turn Reinforcement Learning** — ICLR 2026 [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.24119)] [[Code](https://github.com/spiral-rl/spiral)]
  Trained reasoning through competitive self-play on zero-sum games, transferring to up to 10% gains on 8 reasoning benchmarks across Qwen and Llama families without human supervision.

- **Curriculum Reinforcement Learning from Easy to Hard Tasks Improves LLM Reasoning** — ICLR 2026 [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06632)]
  Demonstrated that scheduling RL tasks from easy to hard enables small LLMs (1.5B-3B) to build reasoning skills that vanilla RL alone fails to achieve.

- **R1-Code-Interpreter: LLMs Reason with Code via Supervised and Multi-stage Reinforcement Learning** — ICLR 2026 [`Reasoning`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21668)] [[Code](https://github.com/yongchao98/R1-Code-Interpreter)]
  Combined SFT with multi-stage GRPO and curriculum learning for code-augmented reasoning, outperforming GPT-4o on 144 diverse tasks with a 14B model.

- **AgentFlow: In-the-Flow Agentic System Optimization for Effective Planning and Tool Use** — ICLR 2026 [`LLM`] [`RAG`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05592)] [[Code](https://github.com/lupantech/AgentFlow)]
  Coordinated planner-executor-verifier-generator modules with Flow-GRPO optimization, outperforming GPT-4o across search, agentic, and math tasks with a 7B backbone.

- **OSCAR: Online Soft Compression And Reranking** — ICLR 2026 [`RAG`] [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07109)]
  Proposed query-dependent online soft compression for RAG that achieves 2-5x inference speedup with minimal accuracy loss across 1B to 24B models.
