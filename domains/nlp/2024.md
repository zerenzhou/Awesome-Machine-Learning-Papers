# Natural Language Processing — 2024

- **The LLaMA 3 Herd of Models** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783)] [[Code](https://github.com/meta-llama/llama3)]
  Scaled the LLaMA family to 405B parameters with multilingual support and 128K context, matching GPT-4 class performance as a fully open model.

- **Mixtral of Experts** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`] [`MoE`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04088)]
  Combined Mistral's architecture with sparse mixture-of-experts (8x7B), matching or outperforming LLaMA 2 70B while using only 13B active parameters per token.

- **Gemma: Open Models Based on Gemini Research and Technology** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08295)] [[Code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/gemma)]
  Released lightweight 2B and 7B open models from Google DeepMind, built with Gemini technology and achieving strong performance relative to size.

- **Phi-3 Technical Report: A Highly Capable Language Model Locally on Your Phone** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`] [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14219)]
  Demonstrated that a 3.8B model trained on carefully curated data can rival much larger models, enabling on-device deployment.

- **Qwen2 Technical Report** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10671)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen2)]
  Presented Qwen2 models from 0.5B to 72B with strong multilingual and coding capabilities, competitive with leading open and closed models.

- **Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces** — COLM 2024 [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752)] [[Code](https://github.com/state-spaces/mamba)]
  Introduced input-dependent selective state-space models that match Transformer quality on language modeling at linear-time complexity.

- **Eagle and Finch: RWKV with Matrix-Valued States and Dynamic Recurrence** — arXiv 2024 [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05892)] [[Code](https://github.com/RWKV/RWKV-LM)]
  Extended RWKV to v5/v6 with matrix-valued states and data-dependent recurrence, scaling to 7.5B parameters with competitive Transformer-level performance.

- **Griffin: Mixing Gated Linear Recurrences with Local Attention for Efficient Language Models** — arXiv 2024 [`SSM`] [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19427)]
  Proposed a hybrid architecture mixing gated linear recurrences (Hawk) with local attention, matching Transformer quality while achieving higher throughput on long sequences.

- **ORPO: Monolithic Preference Optimization without Reference Model** — EMNLP 2024 [`Alignment`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07691)] [[Code](https://github.com/xfactlab/orpo)]
  Unified supervised fine-tuning and preference alignment in a single training stage by adding an odds-ratio penalty, eliminating the need for a reference model.

- **SimPO: Simple Preference Optimization with a Reference-Free Reward** — NeurIPS 2024 [`Alignment`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14734)] [[Code](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/SimPO)]
  Simplified DPO by using average log-probability as an implicit reward with a target margin, outperforming DPO and its variants without a reference model.

- **Ring Attention with Blockwise Transformers for Near-Infinite Context** — ICLR 2024 [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01889)]
  Distributed self-attention across multiple devices in a ring topology, enabling training on sequences millions of tokens long without approximation.
