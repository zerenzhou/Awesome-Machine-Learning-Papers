# Natural Language Processing — 2025

- **DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`] [`MoE`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19437)] [[Code](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V3)]
  Introduced a 671B MoE model (37B active) with auxiliary-loss-free load balancing and multi-token prediction, trained on 14.8T tokens using only 2.788M H800 GPU hours.

- **DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning** — arXiv 2025 [`LLM`] [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948)] [[Code](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1)]
  Demonstrated that reasoning abilities emerge from pure reinforcement learning without supervised fine-tuning, achieving performance comparable to OpenAI-o1 on math and code benchmarks.

- **Qwen2.5 Technical Report** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15115)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen2.5)]
  Scaled pre-training to 18T tokens with 128K context and 29 languages, offering open-weight models from 0.5B to 72B competitive with Llama-3-405B-Instruct.

- **Qwen3 Technical Report** — arXiv 2025 [`LLM`] [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388)]
  Unified thinking and non-thinking modes in a single framework across dense and MoE models (0.6B to 235B), expanded multilingual support to 119 languages under Apache 2.0.

- **Gemma 2: Improving Open Language Models at a Practical Size** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00118)] [[Code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/gemma)]
  Released 2B/9B/27B open models using interleaved local-global attention and knowledge distillation, offering competitive alternatives to models 2-3x larger.

- **OLMo 2: The Best Fully Open Language Model to Date** — arXiv 2025 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00656)] [[Code](https://github.com/allenai/OLMo)]
  Fully open 7B/13B/32B models (data, code, weights, logs) from AI2 that match or exceed Llama 3.1 and Qwen 2.5 at equivalent compute.

- **Towards System 2 Reasoning in LLMs: Learning How to Think With Meta Chain-of-Thought** — arXiv 2025 [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04682)]
  Proposed Meta Chain-of-Thought to explicitly model the underlying reasoning process behind chain-of-thought, incorporating search traces and reinforcement learning post-training.

- **s1: Simple Test-Time Scaling** — arXiv 2025 [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19393)] [[Code](https://github.com/simplescaling/s1)]
  Showed that fine-tuning on just 1K curated reasoning traces with budget forcing exceeds o1-preview on competition math by up to 27%.

- **Kimi k1.5: Scaling Reinforcement Learning with LLMs** — arXiv 2025 [`LLM`] [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12599)]
  Scaled reinforcement learning with long-CoT supervised fine-tuning and partial rollout optimization, enabling state-of-the-art multi-modal reasoning from Moonshot AI.

- **Native Sparse Attention: Hardware-Aligned and Natively Trainable Sparse Attention** — ACL 2025 [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11089)]
  Combined coarse-grained token compression with fine-grained token selection for hardware-aligned sparse attention, achieving substantial speedups on 64K sequences without sacrificing quality.

- **Byte Latent Transformer: Patches Scale Better Than Tokens** — ACL 2025 [`LLM`] [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09871)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/blt)]
  Replaced fixed tokenization with entropy-based dynamic byte patches, matching Llama 3 performance while using up to 50% fewer inference FLOPs.

- **Gated Attention for Large Language Models: Non-linearity, Sparsity, and Attention-Sink-Free** — NeurIPS 2025 [`LLM`] [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06708)] [[Code](https://github.com/qiuzh20/gated_attention)]
  Applied a learnable sigmoid gate after scaled dot-product attention, eliminating attention sinks and improving long-context extrapolation, adopted in Qwen3-Next.
