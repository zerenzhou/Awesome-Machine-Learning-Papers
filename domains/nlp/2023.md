# Natural Language Processing — 2023

- **LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models** — arXiv 2023 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13971)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/llama)]
  Showed that smaller models trained on more data can match or exceed larger models, democratizing LLM research.

- **LLaMA 2: Open Foundation and Fine-Tuned Chat Models** — arXiv 2023 [`LLM`] [`Alignment`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09288)] [[Code](https://github.com/meta-llama/llama)]
  Scaled LLaMA to 70B parameters with RLHF-based chat fine-tuning, released openly and competitive with closed-source models.

- **Mistral 7B** — arXiv 2023 [`LLM`] [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06825)] [[Code](https://github.com/mistralai/mistral-src)]
  Introduced a 7B model using grouped-query attention and sliding window attention that outperformed LLaMA 2 13B on all benchmarks.

- **The Falcon Series of Open Language Models** — arXiv 2023 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16867)]
  Presented Falcon 7B/40B/180B models trained on the high-quality RefinedWeb dataset, achieving top results on open LLM leaderboards.

- **Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Alignment`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290)]
  Eliminated the need for a separate reward model in RLHF by reparameterizing the objective into a simple classification loss on preference pairs.

- **Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback** — arXiv 2023 [`Alignment`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073)]
  Proposed training a harmless AI assistant using AI-generated self-critiques guided by a set of constitutional principles, reducing reliance on human red-teaming.

- **Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Reasoning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601)] [[Code](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/tree-of-thought-llm)]
  Generalized chain-of-thought prompting into a tree-search framework, enabling LLMs to explore and backtrack across multiple reasoning paths.

- **QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized Language Models** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314)] [[Code](https://github.com/artidoro/qlora)]
  Enabled fine-tuning of 65B-parameter models on a single 48GB GPU by combining 4-bit quantization with low-rank adapters.

- **FlashAttention-2: Faster Attention with Better Parallelism and Work Partitioning** — ICLR 2024 [`Efficiency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691)] [[Code](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention)]
  Improved FlashAttention with better GPU work partitioning, achieving 2x speedup and reaching up to 73% of theoretical peak FLOPS on A100.

- **Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools** — NeurIPS 2023 [`RAG`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761)]
  Trained a language model to autonomously decide when and how to call external tools (calculator, search, etc.) by self-generating API-call annotations.
