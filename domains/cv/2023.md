# Computer Vision — 2023

- **Segment Anything** — ICCV 2023 [`Foundation Model`] [`Segmentation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02643)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything)]
  Introduced SAM, a promptable foundation model for image segmentation trained on over 1 billion masks.

- **DINOv2: Learning Robust Visual Features without Supervision** — arXiv 2023 [`Foundation Model`] [`Self-Supervised`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07193)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2)]
  Trained general-purpose visual features via self-supervised learning at scale that match or exceed task-specific models across diverse benchmarks.

- **Adding Conditional Control to Text-to-Image Diffusion Models** — ICCV 2023 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05543)] [[Code](https://github.com/lllyasviel/ControlNet)]
  Proposed ControlNet, enabling spatial conditioning (edges, poses, depth) for fine-grained control over diffusion generation.

- **DreamBooth: Fine Tuning Text-to-Image Diffusion Models for Subject-Driven Generation** — CVPR 2023 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.12242)] [[Code](https://github.com/google/dreambooth)]
  Personalized text-to-image models by fine-tuning on a few subject images, enabling novel scene synthesis for specific concepts.

- **DINO: DETR with Improved DeNoising Anchor Boxes for End-to-End Object Detection** — ICLR 2023 [`Detection`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03605)] [[Code](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/DINO)]
  Improved DETR-style detection with denoising anchor boxes, achieving state-of-the-art on COCO without extra data.

- **ConvNeXt V2: Co-designing and Scaling ConvNets with Masked Autoencoders** — CVPR 2023 [`CNN`] [`Self-Supervised`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00808)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ConvNeXt-V2)]
  Co-designed a fully convolutional masked autoencoder with Global Response Normalization for improved ConvNet self-supervised learning.

- **InternImage: Exploring Large-Scale Vision Foundation Models with Deformable Convolutions** — CVPR 2023 [`CNN`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.05778)] [[Code](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternImage)]
  Scaled deformable convolution-based models to 3 billion parameters, achieving competitive results against large vision transformers.

- **3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering** — SIGGRAPH 2023 [`3D`] [`Rendering`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04079)] [[Code](https://github.com/graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting)]
  Represented scenes with 3D Gaussians and a differentiable splatting rasterizer, enabling real-time novel view synthesis at high quality.

- **Zip-NeRF: Anti-Aliased Grid-Based Neural Radiance Fields** — ICCV 2023 [`3D`] [`NeRF`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06706)]
  Combined mip-NeRF 360 with grid-based representations for fast training and state-of-the-art anti-aliased neural rendering.

- **DALL-E 3** — arXiv 2023 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`] [`Vision-Language`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17400)]
  Improved text-to-image generation by training on highly descriptive synthetic captions, greatly enhancing prompt adherence and compositional understanding.

- **EfficientViT: Lightweight Multi-Scale Attention for High-Resolution Dense Prediction** — ICCV 2023 [`Transformer`] [`Efficient`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14756)] [[Code](https://github.com/mit-han-lab/efficientvit)]
  Designed lightweight multi-scale linear attention enabling efficient vision transformers for high-resolution dense prediction.

- **FastViT: A Fast Hybrid Vision Transformer using Structural Reparameterization** — ICCV 2023 [`Transformer`] [`Efficient`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.14189)] [[Code](https://github.com/apple/ml-fastvit)]
  Proposed RepMixer with structural reparameterization, achieving state-of-the-art latency-accuracy trade-offs on mobile devices.

- **InstructPix2Pix: Learning to Follow Image Editing Instructions** — CVPR 2023 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09800)] [[Code](https://github.com/timothybrooks/instruct-pix2pix)]
  Trained a conditional diffusion model to edit images from human instructions by generating a paired training dataset using GPT-3 and Stable Diffusion.

- **YOLOv7: Trainable Bag-of-Freebies Sets New State-of-the-Art for Real-Time Object Detectors** — CVPR 2023 [`Detection`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02696)] [[Code](https://github.com/WongKinYiu/yolov7)]
  Introduced Extended-ELAN and trainable bag-of-freebies with planned re-parameterized convolution and coarse-to-fine auxiliary head training.

- **DETRs with Collaborative Hybrid Assignments Training** — ICCV 2023 [`Detection`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.12860)] [[Code](https://github.com/Sense-X/Co-DETR)]
  Proposed Co-DETR, combining one-to-one and one-to-many label assignments in a collaborative training scheme to improve DETR convergence and accuracy.

- **Sigmoid Loss for Language Image Pre-Training** — ICCV 2023 [`Foundation Model`] [`Vision-Language`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15343)]
  Replaced softmax-based contrastive loss with pairwise sigmoid loss (SiGLIP) for vision-language pre-training, simplifying distributed training and improving efficiency.

- **Stable Video Diffusion: Scaling Latent Video Diffusion Models to Large Datasets** — arXiv 2023 [`Diffusion`] [`Video Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15127)]
  Systematically trained latent video diffusion models on large curated video datasets, enabling state-of-the-art image-to-video generation.
