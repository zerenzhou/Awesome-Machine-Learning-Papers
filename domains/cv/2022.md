# Computer Vision — 2022

- **Masked Autoencoders Are Scalable Vision Learners** — CVPR 2022 [`Self-Supervised`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06377)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/mae)]
  Proposed MAE, masking 75% of image patches and reconstructing them for highly scalable self-supervised pre-training of Vision Transformers.

- **High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models** — CVPR 2022 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752)] [[Code](https://github.com/CompVis/latent-diffusion)]
  Introduced Latent Diffusion for efficient high-resolution image generation in compressed latent space, enabling the Stable Diffusion ecosystem.

- **A ConvNet for the 2020s** — CVPR 2022 [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.03545)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ConvNeXt)]
  Modernized ResNet with Transformer-inspired design choices (ConvNeXt), demonstrating pure ConvNets can match Swin Transformer performance.

- **Hierarchical Text-Conditional Image Generation with CLIP Latents** — arXiv 2022 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.06125)]
  Proposed DALL-E 2 (unCLIP), generating images from text via a CLIP text-to-image prior followed by a diffusion decoder.

- **Photorealistic Text-to-Image Diffusion Models with Deep Language Understanding** — NeurIPS 2022 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11487)]
  Introduced Imagen, leveraging a large frozen T5 language encoder with cascaded diffusion models for unprecedented text-to-image fidelity.

- **BEiT: BERT Pre-Training of Image Transformers** — ICLR 2022 [`Self-Supervised`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.08254)] [[Code](https://github.com/microsoft/unilm/tree/master/beit)]
  Applied masked image modeling with discrete visual tokens for self-supervised ViT pre-training, bridging BERT-style pre-training to vision.

- **Masked-attention Mask Transformer for Universal Image Segmentation** — CVPR 2022 [`Segmentation`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.01527)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Mask2Former)]
  Proposed Mask2Former with masked attention for universal segmentation (panoptic, instance, semantic) within a single Transformer architecture.
