# Computer Vision — 2015

- **Very Deep Convolutional Networks for Large-Scale Image Recognition** — ICLR 2015 [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1556)]
  Demonstrated that increasing network depth with small 3×3 filters significantly improves classification, establishing VGGNet as a widely adopted feature extractor.

- **Going Deeper with Convolutions** — CVPR 2015 [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.4842)]
  Proposed the Inception module with parallel multi-scale convolutions, winning ILSVRC 2014 with GoogLeNet while maintaining computational efficiency.

- **Fast R-CNN** — ICCV 2015 [`Detection`] [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.08083)] [[Code](https://github.com/rbgirshick/fast-rcnn)]
  Unified region-based detection into a single-stage training pipeline with RoI pooling, achieving 9× faster training and 213× faster inference over R-CNN.

- **Fully Convolutional Networks for Semantic Segmentation** — CVPR 2015 [`Segmentation`] [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.4038)]
  Adapted classification CNNs into fully convolutional architectures for dense pixel-wise prediction, founding modern semantic segmentation.

- **U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation** — MICCAI 2015 [`Segmentation`] [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04597)] [[Code](https://github.com/milesial/Pytorch-UNet)]
  Proposed an encoder-decoder with skip connections for precise segmentation from few training images, becoming the standard for medical image analysis.

- **Batch Normalization: Accelerating Deep Network Training by Reducing Internal Covariate Shift** — ICML 2015 [`CNN`] [`Training`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167)]
  Introduced batch normalization to stabilize and accelerate deep network training, enabling higher learning rates and reducing initialization sensitivity.
