# Computer Vision — 2025

- **VGGT: Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer** — CVPR 2025 [`3D`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11651)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vggt)]
  Feed-forward transformer that directly infers all key 3D attributes (camera poses, point maps, depth) from images in under one second.

- **AIMv2: Multimodal Autoregressive Pre-training of Large Vision Encoders** — CVPR 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`Self-Supervised`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14402)] [[Code](https://github.com/apple/ml-aim)]
  Pre-trained vision encoders via a multimodal autoregressive objective, outperforming state-of-the-art with fewer pre-training samples.

- **Video Depth Anything: Consistent Depth Estimation for Super-Long Videos** — CVPR 2025 [`Depth Estimation`] [`Video`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12375)] [[Code](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Video-Depth-Anything)]
  Extended Depth Anything V2 to video with a spatial-temporal head for consistent depth estimation in videos over five minutes.

- **Depth Anything V2: A More Capable Foundation Model for Monocular Depth Estimation** — NeurIPS 2024 [`Foundation Model`] [`Depth Estimation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09414)] [[Code](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2)]
  Improved monocular depth foundation model using synthetic data and large teacher models for finer details and robustness.

- **SigLIP 2: Multilingual Vision-Language Encoders with Improved Semantic Understanding, Localization, and Dense Features** — ArXiv 2025 [`Vision-Language`] [`Contrastive`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14786)] [[Code](https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2)]
  Enhanced SigLIP with improved localization, dense features, and multilingual capabilities for stronger vision-language alignment.

- **FLUX.1: A Flow-Matching Diffusion Transformer for Text-to-Image Generation** — ArXiv 2025 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux)] [[Code](https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux)]
  Rectified flow transformer from Black Forest Labs achieving state-of-the-art text-to-image generation with efficient inference.

- **GEN3C: 3D-Informed World-Consistent Video Generation with Precise Camera Control** — CVPR 2025 [`3D`] [`Video Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03751)] [[Code](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/toronto-ai/gen3c/)]
  Combined 3D geometry awareness with video diffusion models for camera-controllable, world-consistent video generation.

- **Molmo and PixMo: Open Weights and Open Data for State-of-the-Art Vision-Language Models** — CVPR 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`Vision-Language`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17146)] [[Code](https://github.com/allenai/molmo)]
  Fully open VLM (weights and data) whose 7.2B model surpasses proprietary models on multiple open benchmarks.
