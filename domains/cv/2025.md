# Computer Vision — 2025

- **SAM 2: Segment Anything in Images and Videos** — ICLR 2025 Oral [`Foundation Model`] [`Segmentation`] [`Video`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00714)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2)]
  Extended Segment Anything to video with a streaming memory architecture for real-time promptable segmentation across frames.

- **VGGT: Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer** — CVPR 2025 Best Paper [`3D`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11651)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vggt)]
  Feed-forward transformer that directly infers camera poses, depth, and point maps from images in under one second.

- **AIMv2: Multimodal Autoregressive Pre-training of Large Vision Encoders** — CVPR 2025 Highlight [`Foundation Model`] [`Self-Supervised`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14402)] [[Code](https://github.com/apple/ml-aim)]
  Pre-trained vision encoders via a multimodal autoregressive objective, outperforming CLIP/SigLIP with fewer pre-training samples.

- **Molmo and PixMo: Open Weights and Open Data for State-of-the-Art Vision-Language Models** — CVPR 2025 Best Paper Honorable Mention [`Foundation Model`] [`Vision-Language`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17146)] [[Code](https://github.com/allenai/molmo)]
  Fully open VLM (weights and data) whose 7.2B model surpasses proprietary models on multiple open benchmarks.

- **Video Depth Anything: Consistent Depth Estimation for Super-Long Videos** — CVPR 2025 Highlight [`Foundation Model`] [`Depth Estimation`] [`Video`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12375)] [[Code](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Video-Depth-Anything)]
  Extended Depth Anything V2 to video with a spatial-temporal head for consistent depth estimation in videos over five minutes.

- **SigLIP 2: Multilingual Vision-Language Encoders with Improved Semantic Understanding, Localization, and Dense Features** — CVPR 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`Vision-Language`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14786)] [[Code](https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2)]
  Enhanced SigLIP with captioning-based pretraining and self-supervised losses for improved localization and dense feature quality.

- **GEN3C: 3D-Informed World-Consistent Video Generation with Precise Camera Control** — CVPR 2025 Highlight [`3D`] [`Video Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03751)] [[Code](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/GEN3C)]
  Combined 3D geometry awareness with video diffusion models for camera-controllable, world-consistent video generation.

- **MegaSaM: Accurate, Fast and Robust Structure and Motion from Casual Dynamic Videos** — CVPR 2025 Best Paper Honorable Mention [`3D`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04463)] [[Code](https://github.com/mega-sam/mega-sam)]
  Full pipeline for accurate camera tracking and depth estimation from in-the-wild monocular videos of complex dynamic scenes.

- **MambaVision: A Hybrid Mamba-Transformer Vision Backbone** — CVPR 2025 [`SSM`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08083)] [[Code](https://github.com/NVlabs/MambaVision)]
  First hybrid Mamba-Transformer backbone for vision, achieving state-of-the-art ImageNet accuracy with superior throughput.

- **Your ViT is Secretly an Image Segmentation Model** — CVPR 2025 Highlight [`Transformer`] [`Segmentation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19108)] [[Code](https://github.com/tue-mps/EoMT)]
  Showed that a plain ViT with mask annealing training (EoMT) matches specialized segmentation architectures while being up to 4× faster.

- **FlowEdit: Inversion-Free Text-Based Editing Using Pre-Trained Flow Models** — ICCV 2025 Best Paper Honorable Mention [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08629)] [[Code](https://github.com/fallenshock/FlowEdit)]
  Text-based image editing via direct source-to-target distribution mapping with no inversion or optimization, working on FLUX and other flow models.

- **Normalizing Flows are Capable Generative Models** — ICML 2025 Oral [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06329)] [[Code](https://github.com/apple/ml-tarflow)]
  Proposed TarFlow, a Transformer-based normalizing flow achieving diffusion-level image generation quality while maintaining exact likelihood estimation.

- **FoundationStereo: Zero-Shot Stereo Matching** — CVPR 2025 Oral [`Foundation Model`] [`Depth Estimation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09898)] [[Code](https://github.com/NVlabs/FoundationStereo)]
  Foundation model for stereo depth trained on 1M synthetic pairs, achieving strong zero-shot generalization via monocular depth prior side-tuning.

- **YOLOv12: Attention-Centric Real-Time Object Detectors** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Detection`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12524)] [[Code](https://github.com/sunsmarterjie/yolov12)]
  First attention-centric YOLO framework introducing Area Attention and R-ELAN to match CNN-based YOLO speed while leveraging attention benefits.

- **YOLOE: Real-Time Seeing Anything** — ICCV 2025 [`Detection`] [`Segmentation`] [`Vision-Language`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07465)] [[Code](https://github.com/THU-MIG/yoloe)]
  Unified open-vocabulary detection and segmentation supporting text and visual prompts with zero overhead compared to closed-set YOLOs.

- **YOLOv13: Real-Time Object Detection with Hypergraph-Enhanced Adaptive Visual Perception** — arXiv 2025 [`Detection`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17733)] [[Code](https://github.com/iMoonLab/yolov13)]
  Proposed HyperACE mechanism capturing high-order correlations beyond pairwise attention and FullPAD for cross-scale feature fusion.
