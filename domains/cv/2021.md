# Computer Vision — 2021

- **Swin Transformer: Hierarchical Vision Transformer using Shifted Windows** — ICCV 2021 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14030)] [[Code](https://github.com/microsoft/Swin-Transformer)]
  Introduced shifted window-based self-attention for efficient hierarchical feature extraction, becoming a general-purpose vision backbone.

- **Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision** — ICML 2021 [`Foundation Model`] [`Vision-Language`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020)] [[Code](https://github.com/openai/CLIP)]
  Proposed CLIP, training on 400M image-text pairs with contrastive learning, enabling powerful zero-shot visual recognition across diverse tasks.

- **Emerging Properties in Self-Supervised Vision Transformers** — ICCV 2021 [`Self-Supervised`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.14294)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dino)]
  Discovered that self-supervised ViTs (DINO) learn features containing explicit scene layout and object boundary information without any labels.

- **Training data-efficient image transformers & distillation through attention** — ICML 2021 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.12877)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/deit)]
  Proposed DeiT, training ViTs efficiently on ImageNet-1K alone with knowledge distillation and strong data augmentation, democratizing Vision Transformer research.

- **MLP-Mixer: An all-MLP Architecture for Vision** — NeurIPS 2021 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.01601)] [[Code](https://github.com/google-research/vision_transformer)]
  Showed that a pure MLP architecture with token-mixing and channel-mixing layers achieves competitive image classification without convolutions or self-attention.

- **Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations** — ICLR 2021 [`Diffusion`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.13456)] [[Code](https://github.com/yang-song/score_sde)]
  Unified score matching and diffusion models under a continuous-time SDE framework, enabling controllable generation and exact likelihood computation.
