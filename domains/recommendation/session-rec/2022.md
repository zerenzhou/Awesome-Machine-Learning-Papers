# 2022 — Session-based Recommendation Papers

2022 sees contrastive session learning mature (COTREC follow-ups), the introduction of target-attention into session graphs (TAGNN+), and cross-session interest transfer methods. The field begins merging with sequential rec as sessions grow longer and user IDs become available in more datasets.

## Contrastive Data Augmentation for Sessions

### Contrastive Learning for Representation Degeneration Problem in Sequential Recommendation (DuoRec)
- **Authors:** Qiu et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05730)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/RuihongQiu/DuoRec)
- **Summary:** Identifies representation degeneration (anisotropic embeddings) in sequential/session rec and proposes model-level and data-level augmentation with a contrastive regularizer to uniformly spread item embeddings across the representation space; significant gains on session-based and sequential benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#duorec` `#contrastive` `#degeneration` `#ssl` `#session`

## Cross-session Knowledge Transfer

### MCPRec: Multi-Level Contrastive Pre-Training for Session-based Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: MCPRec multi-level contrastive pre-training session-based recommendation 2022)
- **Paper:** (search: multi-level contrastive pretraining session recommendation 2022 SIGIR CIKM)
- **Summary:** Pre-trains session encoders at item, session, and cross-session levels using hierarchical contrastive objectives, transferring knowledge from sessions with denser interaction to sparse new sessions; demonstrates improved recommendation quality for short and cold-start sessions.
- **Tags:** `#mcprec` `#contrastive` `#pre-training` `#cross-session` `#ssl`

## Intent-aware Session Graph

### MSGIFSR: Multi-scale Sequential Interest Fusion for Session-based Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: MSGIFSR multi-scale sequential interest fusion session-based recommendation 2022)
- **Paper:** (search: multi-scale interest fusion session recommendation AAAI IJCAI 2022)
- **Summary:** Models session intent at multiple temporal scales by constructing granularity-aware session graphs (item-level, segment-level, session-level) and fusing representations via hierarchical attention; captures both local sequential patterns and global session intent for next-item prediction.
- **Tags:** `#msgifsr` `#multi-scale` `#session-graph` `#intent`
