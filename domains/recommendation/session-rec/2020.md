# 2020 — Session-based Recommendation Papers

2020 expands session graphs to global cross-session transitions: GCE-GNN adds global graph edges from items co-occurring across sessions, and TAGNN introduces target-aware graph attention for session modeling.

## Global Graph Augmentation

### Global Context Enhanced Graph Neural Networks for Session-based Recommendation (GCE-GNN)
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2020
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3397271.3401142)
- **Summary:** Constructs a global item transition graph spanning all sessions and a local session graph; applies GNN over both and combines representations via a cross-graph attention mechanism, capturing long-range sequential patterns invisible within a single session.
- **Tags:** `#gcegnn` `#global-graph` `#session`

### TAGNN: Target Attentive Graph Neural Networks for Session-based Recommendation
- **Authors:** Yu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2020
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3397271.3401319)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/CRIPAC-DIG/TAGNN)
- **Summary:** Extends SR-GNN with target-aware attention: item embeddings are re-weighted based on their relevance to each candidate target item, rather than using a fixed session vector; enables different session representations per candidate for more accurate ranking.
- **Tags:** `#tagnn` `#target-aware` `#graph-attention` `#session`

## Self-supervised / Contrastive Pre-session

### Handling Information Loss of Graph Neural Networks for Session-based Recommendation (LESSR)
- **Authors:** Chen & Wong
- **Venue:** KDD 2020
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3394486.3403170)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/twchen/lessr)
- **Summary:** Identifies that GNN aggregation on session graphs loses item ordering and edge multiplicity information; proposes lossless encoding via a combination of shortcut graph attention and ECC (edge-type-conditioned convolution) to preserve exact transition patterns.
- **Tags:** `#lessr` `#lossless` `#gnn` `#session`

## Attribute-rich Sessions

### Exploiting Cross-Session Information for Session-based Recommendation with Graph Neural Networks
- **Authors:** Pan et al.
- **Venue:** ACM TOIS 2020
- **Paper:** (search: Exploiting Cross-Session Information Session-based Recommendation Graph Neural Networks Pan TOIS 2020)
- **Summary:** Builds a cross-session item-relation graph that connects items from different sessions via shared attributes, enabling neighborhood information flow across session boundaries while maintaining local session graph structure.
- **Tags:** `#cross-session` `#attributes` `#gnn`

## Data Augmentation

### Augmenting Sequential Recommendation with Pseudo-Prior Items via Reversely Pre-training Transformer
- **Authors:** Liu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.00522)
- **Summary:** Pre-trains a Transformer in reverse temporal order to generate pseudo prior items that augment short/sparse sessions, addressing the cold-start and session-sparsity problem in session-based recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#data-augmentation` `#transformer` `#cold-start` `#session`
