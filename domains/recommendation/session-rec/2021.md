# 2021 — Session-based Recommendation Papers

2021 brings hypergraph neural networks for multi-level session modeling (DHCN) and contrastive self-supervised learning to session-based rec (COTREC, DSAN), establishing SSL as the dominant paradigm improvement for sparse session data.

## Hypergraph Session Modeling

### Self-Supervised Hypergraph Convolutional Networks for Session-based Recommendation (DHCN)
- **Authors:** Xia et al.
- **Venue:** AAAI 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06852)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xiaxin1998/DHCN)
- **Summary:** Constructs a hypergraph where each hyperedge connects all items appearing in a session, then applies hypergraph convolution to capture multi-order item co-occurrence relationships; auxiliary self-supervised loss between GNN and hypergraph representations further improves learning.
- **Tags:** `#dhcn` `#hypergraph` `#ssl` `#session`

## Contrastive Session Learning

### Self-Supervised Graph Co-Training for Session-based Recommendation (COTREC)
- **Authors:** Xia et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10560)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xiaxin1998/COTREC)
- **Summary:** Co-trains two GNN views (item-level session graph and session-level hypergraph) with bidirectional contrastive loss as auxiliary supervision, enabling mutual enhancement between the two graph perspectives for session recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#cotrec` `#contrastive` `#co-training` `#session`

### Dual Sparse Attention Network for Session-based Recommendation (DSAN)
- **Authors:** Guo et al.
- **Venue:** IJCAI 2021
- **Paper:** (search: Dual Sparse Attention Network Session-based Recommendation DSAN Guo IJCAI 2021)
- **Summary:** Introduces dual sparse self-attention: one head captures focused item-level intent transitions, the other captures distributed session-level context; sparse masking eliminates noisy interactions, improving session representation quality on long sessions.
- **Tags:** `#dsan` `#sparse-attention` `#session`

## Noise-robust Session Modeling

### Robust Preference-Guided Denoising for Graph Based Social Recommendation
- **Authors:** Fan et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2022
- **Paper:** (search: preference-guided denoising graph social recommendation SIGIR 2022)
- **Summary:** Identifies noisy interaction signals in session graphs from misclicks and proposes preference-guided edge pruning via learned item-preference consistency, reducing noise propagation in GNN aggregation over session graphs.
- **Tags:** `#denoising` `#graph` `#session`

## Intent Disentanglement

### Disentangled Graph Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2020 (influential in 2021 session disentanglement work)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01764)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xiangwang1223/disentangled_graph_collaborative_filtering)
- **Summary:** Disentangles user intents at the graph level by routing interaction signals into multiple intent-specific graphs via EM-like iterative learning; applied to session-based settings to separate purchase intent from browsing noise.
- **Tags:** `#disentanglement` `#graph` `#intent` `#session`
