# 2024 — LLM for Recommendation Papers

2024 matures the LLM4Rec paradigm into scalable production systems: ReLLa introduces retrieval-augmented recommendation for LLMs, PALR enables personalized abstractive recommendation generation, and the community debates collaborative vs. semantic ID representations at scale.

## Retrieval-augmented LLM Rec

### ReLLa: Retrieval-enhanced Large Language Models for Lifelong Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Lin et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11131)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/LaVieEnRose365/ReLLa)
- **Summary:** Introduces semantic retrieval of the most relevant historical behaviors at inference time to overcome the context length limit of LLMs in sequential recommendation; retrieved behaviors are formatted as LLM prompts, achieving near-full-history performance with only top-K items.
- **Tags:** `#rella` `#retrieval-augmented` `#lifelong` `#sequential`

### PALR: Personalization Aware LLMs for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Chen et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07622)
- **Summary:** Fine-tunes LLaMA with personalized preference descriptions as instruction prompts; retrieves candidate items via a traditional retriever, then generates abstractive personalized recommendation responses with preference-grounded explanations.
- **Tags:** `#palr` `#personalization` `#abstractive` `#llama`

## Alignment / Collaborative + Semantic Fusion

### BIGRec: A Paradigm for Recommendation via Language Modeling on Interaction-Oriented Data
- **Authors:** Bao et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024 (extension of 2023 work)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12028)
- **Summary:** Extended evaluation of BIGRec's grounding mechanism across diverse benchmarks, showing that the generated-item grounding step (matching LM output to actual catalog items) is the critical component for bridging open-ended language generation with closed-set recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#bigrec` `#grounding` `#language-generation` `#extended`

### CoLLM: Integrating Collaborative Embeddings into Large Language Models for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Zhang et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19488)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/zyang1580/CoLLM)
- **Summary:** Proposes a collaborative token mechanism that maps collaborative filter embeddings to special tokens in the LLM vocabulary, allowing the LLM to jointly attend to text tokens and collaborative signal tokens within the standard attention mechanism.
- **Tags:** `#collm` `#collaborative-token` `#alignment` `#hybrid`

## Agent / Planning

### Recommender AI Agent: Integrating Large Language Models for Interactive Recommendations
- **Authors:** Huang et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16505)
- **Summary:** InteRecAgent implements a recommendation agent that uses an LLM as the brain, with specialized tool calls for candidate retrieval (traditional rec model), query rewriting, and multi-turn conversational refinement; achieves strong results on conversational recommendation benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#agent` `#conversational` `#tool-use` `#interactive`

## Evaluation / Benchmark

### LLM-CF: Harnessing the Power of Large Language Models for Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** (search: LLM-CF Large Language Models Collaborative Filtering 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: harnessing LLM collaborative filtering embedding alignment recommendation 2024)
- **Summary:** Systematically benchmarks different strategies for injecting LLM knowledge into collaborative filtering: direct embedding initialization, contrastive alignment, and generative augmentation; finds contrastive alignment most effective for cross-domain transfer.
- **Tags:** `#llm-cf` `#benchmark` `#alignment` `#cf`
