# 2022 — LLM for Recommendation Papers

2022 launches the LLM-for-rec paradigm: P5 and M6-Rec (both in classic.md) establish the unified prompting framework. The year also sees early explorations of frozen LLM embeddings for recommendation and the bridge from pre-trained language models to recommendation tasks via adapter-style fine-tuning.

## Adapter / Fine-tuning Approaches

### UniSRec: Universal Sequence Representation Learning for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Hou et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.05941)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/RUCAIBox/UniSRec)
- **Summary:** Uses BERT to encode item text descriptions into universal item representations, applying parametric whitening and MoE-enhanced adapter layers to transfer pre-trained language representations across different recommendation domains without ID-indexed training.
- **Tags:** `#unisrec` `#universal` `#bert` `#transfer`

### Towards Universal Sequence Representation Learning for Recommender Systems
- **Authors:** Yao et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.05941)
- **Summary:** (UniSRec variant — see above.) Establishes cross-domain item representation learning with shared semantic space via MoE adapters over BERT; demonstrates zero-shot transfer to new domains and datasets without re-training.
- **Tags:** `#cross-domain` `#zero-shot` `#bert` `#transfer`

## Frozen LLM as Feature Extractor

### Recformer: Learning Universal Representations for Recommendation via Large Language Models
- **Authors:** Li et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07001)
- **Summary:** Fine-tunes a Longformer to model user history as a flattened item-attribute text sequence; enables universal item representation across heterogeneous domains by grounding recommendations entirely in language without item IDs.
- **Tags:** `#recformer` `#longformer` `#universal` `#text-based`
