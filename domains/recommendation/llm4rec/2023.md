# 2023 — LLM for Recommendation Papers

2023 is the breakout year for LLM4Rec: TALLRec proves LoRA-tuned LLaMA outperforms traditional models, LlamaRec enables efficient ranking via softmax scoring over option tokens, and E4SRec / LLaRA inject collaborative signals back into LLM recommendation.

## Instruction Tuning

### TALLRec: An Effective and Efficient Tuning Framework to Align Large Language Model with Recommendation
- **Authors:** Bao et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00447)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/SAI990323/TALLRec)
- **Summary:** Fine-tunes LLaMA with LoRA on a two-stage instruction tuning pipeline (general recommendation + domain-specific), achieving strong few-shot rec performance; demonstrates that parameter-efficient LLM fine-tuning outperforms frozen LLM prompting for recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#tallrec` `#lora` `#instruction-tuning` `#llama`

### LlamaRec: Two-Stage Recommendation using Large Language Models for Ranking
- **Authors:** Yue et al.
- **Venue:** PGAI@CIKM 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02089)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Yueeeeeeee/LlamaRec)
- **Summary:** Two-stage pipeline: a traditional retriever narrows candidates, then a fine-tuned LLaMA2 re-ranks them by outputting softmax probabilities over option letter tokens rather than full text generation, achieving fast and accurate LLM-based ranking.
- **Tags:** `#llamarec` `#two-stage` `#ranking` `#llama2`

## Embedding Injection / Hybrid

### E4SRec: An Elegant Effective Efficient Extensible Solution of Large Language Models for Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Li et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02443)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/HestiaSky/E4SRec)
- **Summary:** Injects collaborative ID-based item embeddings from a sequential recommender directly into LLM attention layers as prefix tokens, enabling LLM to leverage both language semantics and collaborative signals without training from scratch.
- **Tags:** `#e4srec` `#embedding-injection` `#hybrid` `#sequential`

### LLaRA: Large Language-Recommendation Assistant
- **Authors:** Liao et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02445)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/ljy0ustc/LLaRA)
- **Summary:** Extends TALLRec by projecting collaborative filtering item embeddings into the LLM's token embedding space, enabling the LLM to process both textual prompts and collaborative signals within a unified autoregressive inference.
- **Tags:** `#llara` `#projection` `#hybrid` `#llama`

## Zero-shot / Agent-based

### RecMind: Large Language Model Powered Agent for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14296)
- **Summary:** Frames recommendation as an autonomous agent task where LLM reasons over user query, retrieves relevant items from external memory, and plans a personalized recommendation action; achieves zero-shot recommendation that improves with chain-of-thought reasoning.
- **Tags:** `#recmind` `#agent` `#zero-shot` `#cot`

## Evaluation & Survey

### How Can Recommender Systems Benefit from Large Language Models: A Survey
- **Authors:** Lin et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05817)
- **Summary:** Surveys LLM-for-rec through four lenses: where (pipeline stage) and how (as encoder, ranker, generator, or agent) LLMs integrate into recommendation, summarizing 60+ papers and providing a taxonomy of LLM-rec interaction paradigms.
- **Tags:** `#survey` `#llm` `#recommendation`
