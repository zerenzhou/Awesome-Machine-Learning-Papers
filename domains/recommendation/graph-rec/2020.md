# 2020 — Graph-based Recommendation Papers

LightGCN (in classic.md) radically simplifies NGCF; simultaneously, 2020 sees MixGCF hard negative mining, and relational knowledge graphs expand into KGIN-precursor explorations of intent modeling. Disentangled graph models begin separating user intents from interaction noise.

## Scalable / Simplified GCN CF

### LightGCN: Simplifying and Powering Graph Convolution Network for Recommendation
- **Authors:** He et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.02126)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/gusye1234/LightGCN-PyTorch)
- **Summary:** (Also in classic.md — key 2020 milestone.) Removes feature transformation and nonlinear activation from NGCF, retaining only neighborhood aggregation and weighted layer combination; achieves better accuracy with far fewer parameters, establishing LightGCN as the standard GCN-CF baseline.
- **Tags:** `#lightgcn` `#simplified-gcn` `#cf` `#seminal`

## Negative Sampling for Graph CF

### MixGCF: An Improved Training Method for Graph Neural Network-based Recommender Systems
- **Authors:** Huang et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.08101)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/huangtinglin/MixGCF)
- **Summary:** Generates hard synthetic negatives by mixing positive and negative item embeddings along the embedding space, providing stronger contrastive signal for GNN-based CF training without requiring additional graph construction.
- **Tags:** `#mixgcf` `#hard-negative` `#data-augmentation` `#gnn`

## Intent Disentanglement

### Disentangled Graph Collaborative Filtering (DGCF)
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01764)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xiangwang1223/disentangled_graph_collaborative_filtering)
- **Summary:** Decomposes user-item interaction into multiple intent-specific sub-graphs via iterative routing, learning disentangled user preference representations that separate distinct motivations behind user behaviors.
- **Tags:** `#dgcf` `#disentanglement` `#intent` `#cf`

## Social-aware Graph Rec

### DiffNet++: A Neural Influence and Interest Diffusion Network for Social Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** IEEE TKDE 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00844)
- **Summary:** Extends DiffNet with an influence diffusion module (social graph) and an interest diffusion module (user-item graph), iteratively updating user representations by propagating both social trust and item preference signals.
- **Tags:** `#diffnet++` `#social` `#influence`

## Knowledge-Intent Graph

### CKAN: Collaborative Knowledge-aware Attentive Network for Recommender Systems
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2020
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3397271.3401141)
- **Summary:** Propagates collaborative signals alongside knowledge graph paths using a dual-aggregation strategy that distinguishes collaborative neighbors from knowledge neighbors, achieving better knowledge-CF fusion than KGAT on several benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#ckan` `#knowledge-graph` `#collaborative` `#attention`
