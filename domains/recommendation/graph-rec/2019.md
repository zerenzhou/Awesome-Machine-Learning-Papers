# 2019 — Graph-based Recommendation Papers

2019 is the year GNN-based CF matures: NGCF (in classic.md) starts the era; simultaneously knowledge-graph-aware models (KGAT, KGIN precursors) demonstrate multi-hop KG reasoning for recommendation. Social graph methods augment user representations with trust networks.

## Knowledge-Graph Aware Recommendation

### KGAT: Knowledge Graph Attention Network for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2019
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07854)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xiangwang1223/knowledge_graph_attention_network)
- **Summary:** Jointly models user-item interaction graph and knowledge graph via attention-based propagation; each hop in the KG is weighted by relation-aware attention, capturing collaborative signals enriched with semantic item attributes.
- **Tags:** `#kgat` `#knowledge-graph` `#attention` `#seminal`

### Ripple Network: Propagating User Preferences on the Knowledge Graph for Recommender Systems
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2018
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.03467)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/hwwang55/RippleNet)
- **Summary:** Propagates user preference outward from seed items through knowledge graph hops (like water ripples), aggregating entity embeddings weighted by preference similarity; an early approach to multi-hop KG propagation for recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#ripplenet` `#knowledge-graph` `#propagation`

## Social Graph Recommendation

### A Neural Influence Diffusion Model for Social Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2019
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10322)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/PeiJieSun/diffnet)
- **Summary:** DiffNet models social influence propagation iteratively over the social graph, using GNN to diffuse friend embeddings and update user representations; combines social and item influence for recommendation via alternating propagation steps.
- **Tags:** `#diffnet` `#social` `#influence-propagation`

## Spectral / Bipartite GCN

### Spectral Graph Convolution Approximations for Large-scale Graph-based Recommendation
- **Authors:** Peng et al.
- **Venue:** (search: spectral graph convolution approximation large-scale recommendation Peng 2019)
- **Paper:** (search: spectral GCN approximation recommendation bipartite graph 2019)
- **Summary:** Develops Chebyshev polynomial approximations of spectral GCN for scalable application to large user-item bipartite graphs, enabling spectral filtering at million-scale without full eigendecomposition.
- **Tags:** `#spectral-gcn` `#scalable` `#bipartite`

## Heterogeneous Graph

### Heterogeneous Graph Attention Network
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2019
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.07293)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Jhy1993/HAN)
- **Summary:** HAN applies hierarchical attention (node-level and semantic-level) over heterogeneous graph meta-paths for node classification and link prediction; widely adapted for heterogeneous user-item-attribute recommendation graphs.
- **Tags:** `#han` `#heterogeneous-graph` `#meta-path` `#attention`
