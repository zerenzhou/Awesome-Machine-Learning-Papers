# 2023 — Graph-based Recommendation Papers

2023 deepens the graph-contrastive paradigm with LightGCL's SVD-based global graph view and XSimGCL's cross-layer contrastive refinement. Scalability remains a priority: SEPT and SimGCL variants scale to billion-node graphs while LLM-augmented graph rec begins emerging.

## Efficient Graph Contrastive Learning

### LightGCL: Simple yet Effective Graph Contrastive Learning for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Cai et al.
- **Venue:** ICLR 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08191)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/HKUDS/LightGCL)
- **Summary:** Replaces stochastic structural augmentation with SVD-based global graph view construction, providing globally consistent contrastive views without randomness; achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on multiple benchmarks with simpler training than SGL and SimGCL.
- **Tags:** `#lightgcl` `#svd` `#contrastive` `#ssl`

### XSimGCL: Towards Extremely Simple Graph Contrastive Learning for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Yu et al.
- **Venue:** IEEE TKDE 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02544)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Coder-Yu/QRec)
- **Summary:** Applies noise perturbation uniformly across all GCN layers and introduces cross-layer contrastive loss between representations from different propagation depths; surpasses SimGCL with minimal code changes, confirming the sufficiency of layer-wise feature perturbation.
- **Tags:** `#xsimgcl` `#cross-layer` `#contrastive`

## LLM-augmented Graph Rec

### LLMRec: Large Language Models with Graph Augmentation for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wei et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00423)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/HKUDS/LLMRec)
- **Summary:** Uses LLM-generated text descriptions to augment the user-item interaction graph with semantic edges, enabling LightGCN-style propagation over a richer semantic graph; achieves significant cold-start improvement by grounding graph structure in language semantics.
- **Tags:** `#llm-augmented` `#graph` `#cold-start` `#cf`

## Scalable Graph Rec

### Automated Graph Self-Supervised Learning via Multi-View Representation (AutoGCL)
- **Authors:** Yin et al.
- **Venue:** AAAI 2022
- **Paper:** (search: AutoGCL Automated Graph Self-Supervised Learning Multi-View Representation Yin 2022)
- **Summary:** Learns to automatically select graph augmentation strategies for contrastive learning via a learnable view generator, replacing manual augmentation design in SGL-family methods and finding dataset-optimal augmentation policies.
- **Tags:** `#autogcl` `#nas` `#contrastive` `#graph`

## Sequential + Graph Fusion

### Graph-Flashback: Leveraging Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks for Location Recommendation
- **Authors:** Rao et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2022
- **Paper:** (search: Graph-Flashback Spatio-Temporal GNN Location Recommendation Rao KDD 2022)
- **Summary:** Builds a user-location-time heterogeneous graph and applies GNN propagation guided by temporal decay, outperforming LSTM/Transformer baselines for POI (point-of-interest) recommendation by modeling periodic mobility patterns.
- **Tags:** `#spatio-temporal` `#poi` `#gnn` `#location`

## Robustness / Fairness in Graph Rec

### Improving Fairness and Accuracy of Graph Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Jiang et al.
- **Venue:** (search: fairness accuracy graph collaborative filtering recommendation Jiang 2023)
- **Paper:** (search: fairness-aware graph CF recommendation 2023)
- **Summary:** Addresses popularity bias in GCN-based CF by rebalancing neighborhood aggregation weights inversely proportional to node degree, reducing over-amplification of popular items while maintaining recommendation accuracy.
- **Tags:** `#fairness` `#popularity-bias` `#graph-cf`
