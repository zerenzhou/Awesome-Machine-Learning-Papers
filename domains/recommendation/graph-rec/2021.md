# 2021 — Graph-based Recommendation Papers

2021 introduces contrastive self-supervised learning as a transformative add-on for graph rec: SGL's stochastic augmentation with InfoNCE loss becomes the dominant training strategy. Simultaneously, UltraGCN removes explicit message passing for efficiency, and KGIN models knowledge-aware user intents for KG recommendation.

## Graph Contrastive SSL

### Self-supervised Graph Learning for Recommendation (SGL)
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10783)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/wujcan/SGL-TensorFlow)
- **Summary:** Applies three stochastic graph augmentations (node dropout, edge dropout, random walk) to the user-item interaction graph, training LightGCN with InfoNCE contrastive loss between augmented views; establishes the SSL graph rec paradigm and yields consistent accuracy gains over LightGCN.
- **Tags:** `#sgl` `#ssl` `#contrastive` `#lightgcn` `#seminal`

### Hyperbolic Graph Collaborative Filtering (HGCF)
- **Authors:** Sun et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2021
- **Paper:** (search: Hyperbolic Graph Collaborative Filtering HGCF Sun WWW 2021)
- **Summary:** Embeds user-item interaction graphs in hyperbolic space (Poincaré disk) via hyperbolic GCN aggregation, capturing hierarchical item taxonomy and power-law degree distributions more faithfully than Euclidean embeddings.
- **Tags:** `#hyperbolic` `#gcn` `#hierarchy` `#cf`

## Efficient Graph CF

### UltraGCN: Ultra Simplification of Graph Convolutional Networks for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Mao et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.15246)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xue-pai/UltraGCN)
- **Summary:** Skips explicit GCN message passing and instead directly approximates the infinite-layer GCN limit through a constraint loss on user-item, user-user, and item-item relations; dramatically reduces training time while matching or exceeding LightGCN accuracy.
- **Tags:** `#ultragcn` `#simplified` `#efficient` `#cf`

## Knowledge-Graph Intent Modeling

### Learning Intents behind Interactions with Knowledge Graph for Recommendation (KGIN)
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.07057)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/huangtinglin/Knowledge_Graph_based_Intent_Network)
- **Summary:** Decomposes each user-item interaction into a distribution over relational paths in the knowledge graph, modeling user intent as a mixture of KG relation patterns; achieves strong KG-based rec accuracy with disentangled intent representations.
- **Tags:** `#kgin` `#knowledge-graph` `#intent` `#disentanglement`

## Hypergraph CF

### HCCF: Hypergraph Contrastive Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Xia et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.12200)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/akaxlh/HCCF)
- **Summary:** Constructs a hypergraph over user-item interactions to capture global high-order correlations, then applies hypergraph-to-graph contrastive learning with dual InfoNCE to align local and global views of user/item representations.
- **Tags:** `#hccf` `#hypergraph` `#contrastive` `#ssl`
