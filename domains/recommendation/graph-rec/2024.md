# 2024 — Graph-based Recommendation Papers

2024 witnesses graph rec merging with LLM capabilities: graph structure encodes collaborative signals while LLM provides semantic item understanding, forming hybrid architectures. XSimGCL variants scale further and diffusion-graph hybrids appear for generation-augmented recommendation.

## LLM + Graph Hybrid

### RLMRec: Representation Learning with LLMs for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Ren et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15950)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/HKUDS/RLMRec)
- **Summary:** Aligns LLM-derived semantic user/item representations with collaborative GNN embeddings via mutual information maximization, enabling each side to enrich the other without requiring joint fine-tuning; achieves state-of-the-art on cold-start and warm-start benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#llm` `#graph-rec` `#alignment` `#mutual-information`

### A Survey of Graph Neural Networks for Recommender Systems: Challenges, Methods, and Directions
- **Authors:** Chen et al.
- **Venue:** ACM TOIS 2023/2024
- **Paper:** (search: Survey Graph Neural Networks Recommender Systems Challenges Methods Directions Chen TOIS 2024)
- **Summary:** Comprehensive survey covering bipartite CF-GNN, knowledge-graph GNN, session-graph, and social-graph recommendation; organizes over 100 papers into a unified taxonomy and identifies open challenges including scalability, cold-start, and fairness.
- **Tags:** `#survey` `#gnn` `#recommendation`

## Diffusion-augmented Graph

### Graph Collaborative Signals Denoising and Augmentation for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Fan et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2023
- **Paper:** (search: graph collaborative signals denoising augmentation recommendation SIGIR 2023)
- **Summary:** Uses a denoising diffusion process over the user-item interaction graph to distinguish true preference signals from noise, augmenting training with denoised positive samples and achieving robust GCN-CF training under sparse and noisy interaction data.
- **Tags:** `#denoising` `#diffusion` `#graph-cf` `#augmentation`

## Scalable / Efficient Graph Rec

### IDEA: Interest Decoupled Attentive Graph Neural Network for Session-based Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: IDEA Interest Decoupled Attentive Graph Neural Network Session-based Recommendation 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: interest decoupling attentive graph session recommendation 2024)
- **Summary:** Decouples user short-term and long-term interests at the graph level using separate GNN branches, then applies cross-interest attention to fuse temporal interest signals, achieving strong session-graph recommendation with interpretable intent separation.
- **Tags:** `#interest-decoupling` `#session` `#gnn` `#attention`

## Knowledge-Graph 2024

### KGCF+: Robust Knowledge Graph Contrastive Filtering for Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: robust knowledge graph contrastive filtering recommendation 2024)

## Multi-behavior 2024

### Multi-Relational Graph Neural Network for Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: multi-relational graph neural network recommendation behavior 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: multi-relational GNN recommendation multi-behavior 2024)
- **Summary:** Models heterogeneous user behaviors (click, cart, purchase, review) as typed edges in a multi-relational graph, using relation-specific message passing and cross-relation attention aggregation to transfer knowledge from auxiliary behaviors to target behaviors.
