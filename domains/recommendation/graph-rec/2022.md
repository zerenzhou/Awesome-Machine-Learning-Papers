# 2022 — Graph-based Recommendation Papers

SimGCL (in classic.md) pioneers noise-based graph augmentation, showing that feature-space perturbation is superior to structural augmentation. NCL explicitly aligns users/items with their neighborhood prototypes, and the community debates whether graph structure is necessary for CF at all.

## Graph Contrastive Improvements

### SimGCL: Are Graph Augmentations Necessary? Simple Graph Contrastive Learning for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Yu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08679)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Coder-Yu/QRec)
- **Summary:** (Also classic.md.) Demonstrates that random noise perturbation in embedding space is a superior augmentation to SGL's structural dropout for graph contrastive learning; SimGCL outperforms SGL with simpler implementation, questioning whether graph topology changes are necessary for contrastive gains.
- **Tags:** `#simgcl` `#contrastive` `#augmentation` `#seminal`

### NCL: Improving Graph Collaborative Filtering with Neighborhood-Enriched Contrastive Learning
- **Authors:** Lin et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.06200)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/RUCAIBox/NCL)
- **Summary:** Treats graph neighborhood embeddings as positive prototypes for contrastive learning, explicitly aligning each user/item embedding with its structural neighbors and semantic cluster centroids via a dual-level InfoNCE objective.
- **Tags:** `#ncl` `#neighborhood` `#contrastive` `#prototype`

## Graph CF without Message Passing

### BSPM: Blurring-Sharpening Process Models for Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Choi et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09324)
- **Summary:** Models GCN recommendation as a signal processing problem: a blurring step (diffusion) smooths embeddings over the graph, and a sharpening step (reverse diffusion) recovers high-frequency user-specific patterns; achieves LightGCN-level accuracy without learned parameters.
- **Tags:** `#signal-processing` `#diffusion` `#non-parametric` `#cf`

## Knowledge-Graph + Contrastive

### KGCL: Knowledge Graph Contrastive Learning for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Yang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.00976)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/yuh-yang/KGCL-SIGIR22)
- **Summary:** Applies contrastive learning between graph views derived from the knowledge graph and the user-item interaction graph, using KG-guided augmentation to enrich collaborative signals with semantic item relations.
- **Tags:** `#kgcl` `#knowledge-graph` `#contrastive` `#ssl`

## Multi-behavior Graph

### Multiplex Behavioral Relation Learning for Recommendation via Memory Augmented Transformer Network
- **Authors:** Xia et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2022
- **Paper:** (search: Multiplex Behavioral Relation Learning Recommendation Memory Augmented Transformer Xia SIGIR 2022)
- **Summary:** Models multiple user behavior types (click, add-to-cart, purchase) as separate relation types on a heterogeneous graph, using memory-augmented Transformer to capture cross-behavior dependencies for recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#multi-behavior` `#heterogeneous-graph` `#transformer`
