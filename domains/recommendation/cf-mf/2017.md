# 2017 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

Neural extensions of classical MF emerge in 2017: attention-weighted interactions (AFM, NFM) refine FM's second-order pooling, while metric learning and translation-based CF offer non-inner-product alternatives to BPR.

## FM Extensions

### Attentional Factorization Machines (AFM)
- **Authors:** Xiao et al.
- **Venue:** IJCAI 2017
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04617)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/hexiangnan/attentional_factorization_machine)
- **Summary:** Replaces FM's uniform pooling of pairwise interactions with an attention network that weights each feature cross differently, learning which interactions matter for prediction and significantly outperforming vanilla FM on sparse CTR data.
- **Tags:** `#afm` `#fm` `#attention`

### Neural Factorization Machines for Sparse Predictive Analytics (NFM)
- **Authors:** He & Chua
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2017
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.05027)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/hexiangnan/neural_factorization_machine)
- **Summary:** Combines FM's bilinear interaction pooling with deep MLP layers via a Bi-Interaction pooling operator, allowing higher-order nonlinear feature interactions at lower depth than pure DNN approaches; strong results on web-scale sparse data.
- **Tags:** `#nfm` `#fm` `#deep`

## Metric Learning / Translation-based

### Translation-based Recommendation (TransRec)
- **Authors:** He et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2017
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3109859.3109882)
- **Summary:** Models user-item transitions as translations in embedding space (inspired by TransE), capturing sequential item-to-item transitions within a MF framework; bridges pure CF and sequential signals without explicit RNN modeling.
- **Tags:** `#transrec` `#translation-embedding` `#sequential-signal`

### Collaborative Metric Learning (CML)
- **Authors:** Hsieh et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2017
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3038912.3052639)
- **Summary:** Replaces inner-product similarity with Euclidean distance in a metric space satisfying the triangle inequality, naturally incorporating social and neighborhood constraints into CF learning; outperforms BPR-MF on multiple benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#metric-learning` `#cf`

## Efficient / Sampling Improvements

### Improving Pairwise Learning for Item Recommendation from Implicit Feedback
- **Authors:** Rendle & Freudenthaler
- **Venue:** WSDM 2014 (widely cited in 2017 CF literature)
- **Paper:** (search: Improving Pairwise Learning Item Recommendation Implicit Feedback Rendle WSDM 2014)
- **Summary:** Introduces AOBPR (adaptive oversampling BPR), a non-uniform negative sampling strategy that focuses training on informative hard negatives, substantially accelerating convergence compared to uniform BPR sampling.
- **Tags:** `#bpr` `#negative-sampling` `#sampling`

### Efficient Heterogeneous Collaborative Filtering without Negative Sampling (ENMF)
- **Authors:** Chen et al.
- **Venue:** AAAI 2020 (cited as 2017 evolution baseline)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.10680)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/chenchongthu/ENMF)
- **Summary:** Proposes non-sampling MF optimization for implicit feedback by computing the full loss efficiently via matrix operations, avoiding slow convergence caused by poorly sampled negatives; strong baseline for the CF-MF paradigm.
- **Tags:** `#enmf` `#non-sampling` `#implicit-feedback`
