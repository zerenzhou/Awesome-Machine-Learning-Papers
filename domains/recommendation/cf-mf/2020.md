# 2020 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

2020 brings variational autoencoder-based generative CF (RecVAE), efficient negative-free matrix factorization (ENMF), and growing interest in disentangling user intent from exposure bias in pure latent-factor models.

## Variational Autoencoder CF

### RecVAE: A New Variational Autoencoder for Top-N Recommendations with Implicit Feedback
- **Authors:** Shenbin et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2021 (preprint 2020)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.11160)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/ilya-shenbin/RecVAE)
- **Summary:** Improves MultVAE by introducing a composite prior combining standard Gaussian with the encoder posterior, and alternating training between encoder and decoder components; demonstrates consistent improvements over MultVAE and EASE on sparse implicit-feedback datasets.
- **Tags:** `#recvae` `#vae` `#generative` `#cf`

## Efficient Negative-Free MF

### Efficient Non-Sampling Factorization Machines for Optimal Context-Aware Recommendation (ENMF)
- **Authors:** Chen et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2020
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3366423.3380148)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/chenchongthu/ENMF)
- **Summary:** Derives a closed-form gradient computation for BPR-style MF that avoids explicit negative sampling by analytically marginalizing over all unobserved items, achieving full-data training efficiency; significantly faster than BPR sampling while matching or exceeding its accuracy.
- **Tags:** `#enmf` `#non-sampling` `#mf` `#efficiency`

## Benchmark / Reproducibility

### Neural Collaborative Filtering vs. Matrix Factorization Revisited
- **Authors:** Rendle et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09683)
- **Summary:** Shows that dot-product MF (iALS) consistently outperforms NCF's MLP-based interaction model when both are properly tuned, arguing that expressiveness of neural interaction functions is less important than the training objective and sampling strategy for implicit CF.
- **Tags:** `#benchmark` `#ncf` `#mf` `#reproducibility`
