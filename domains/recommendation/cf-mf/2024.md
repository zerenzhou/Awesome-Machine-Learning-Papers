# 2024 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

2024 is a quiet year for pure CF/MF as a standalone paradigm — most interaction-based rec work is absorbed into LLM4Rec or graph-rec. Remaining dedicated CF work concentrates on scalable linear methods and principled negative sampling theory.

## Scalable Linear CF

### SLIM and EASE at Scale: Practical Exact and Approximate Linear Autoencoders
