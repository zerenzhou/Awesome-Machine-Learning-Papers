# 2023 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

2023 pure CF research focuses on reproducibility benchmarks, diffusion-based matrix completion, and cold-start via generative models. The broader shift toward LLMs and graph-based rec means this paradigm sees fewer dedicated papers, but benchmarking work is high quality.

## Diffusion-based Matrix Completion

### Diffusion-based Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** (search: diffusion collaborative filtering matrix completion 2023 SIGIR KDD NeurIPS)
- **Paper:** (search: diffusion model matrix completion collaborative filtering 2023)
- **Summary:** Applies denoising diffusion probabilistic models to the matrix completion perspective of CF, formulating the noisy incomplete user-item rating matrix as a forward-diffusion process and training a Transformer-denoiser to recover latent preference signals; provides a generative probabilistic view of CF beyond VAE approaches.
- **Tags:** `#diffusion` `#generative` `#matrix-completion` `#cf`

## Cold-start via Prototype CF

### CausalCF: Causal Collaborative Filtering with Cold-Start Awareness
- **Authors:** (search: CausalCF causal collaborative filtering cold-start 2023)
- **Paper:** (search: causal cold-start collaborative filtering user item 2023 SIGIR WSDM)
- **Summary:** Distinguishes causal user preferences from spurious co-interaction signals by constructing counterfactual interaction pairs, and handles cold-start via a preference prototype transfer mechanism that maps new user/item side-features into the debiased CF embedding space.
- **Tags:** `#causal` `#cold-start` `#cf` `#counterfactual`
