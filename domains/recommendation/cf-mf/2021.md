# 2021 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

2021 is characterized by strong linear-baseline revisits (SimpleX, iALS revisited) and a growing reproducibility culture. Pure CF research increasingly focuses on contrastive learning frameworks to replace BPR, and the community debates whether GNN additions provide real gains over tuned dot-product MF.

## Contrastive / Cosine CF

### SimpleX: A Simple and Strong Baseline for Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Mao et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12613)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xue-pai/Open-CF-Benchmarks)
- **Summary:** Replaces BPR's pairwise softplus loss with a cosine contrastive loss paired with large negative sampling; exhaustive benchmarks on 11 datasets show the simple model matches or exceeds graph-augmented CF models, questioning whether architectural complexity is the limiting factor in CF.
- **Tags:** `#simplex` `#contrastive` `#cosine` `#cf` `#baseline`

## Implicit Feedback & Negative Sampling

### Revisiting the Performance of iALS on Item Recommendation Benchmarks
- **Authors:** Rendle et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14037)
- **Summary:** Systematically retunes iALS with frequency-based negative sampling and shows it matches or outperforms LightGCN and many neural models on standard benchmarks, reinforcing that classic ALS-based MF is still competitive when properly configured.
- **Tags:** `#ials` `#als` `#mf` `#benchmark` `#negative-sampling`

## Scalable Approximate MF

### Scalable Linear Shallow Autoencoder for Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Steck et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2024 (short) / related 2021 follow-up work to EASE
- **Paper:** (search: scalable linear shallow autoencoder collaborative filtering EASE follow-up 2021 2022)
- **Summary:** Addresses the O(n²) memory bottleneck of EASE for large item catalogs by introducing a sparse approximation that retains the closed-form solution structure while reducing computation, making linear autoencoder CF practical at industry scale.
- **Tags:** `#ease` `#linear` `#scalable` `#cf`
