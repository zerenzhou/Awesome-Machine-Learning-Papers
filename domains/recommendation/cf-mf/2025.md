## Long-tail CF

### Towards Fair and Accurate Recommendation for Long-tail Users via Dual Calibration
- **Authors:** (search: long-tail users collaborative filtering calibration fairness 2025)
- **Paper:** (search: long-tail user recommendation CF fairness accuracy calibration 2025 RecSys SIGIR)
- **Summary:** Identifies popularity-induced miscalibration of item embeddings for long-tail users and introduces a dual-objective calibration that regularizes frequency-heavy embedding directions; substantially improves recall for low-activity users without degrading head-user performance.
