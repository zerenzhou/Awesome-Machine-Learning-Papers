# 2022 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

2022 in pure CF is characterized by contrastive data-augmentation (SGL-style applied to MF), cold-start via disentanglement, and invariant learning approaches to remove spurious CF signals. The paradigm is relatively quiet as research shifts to GNN-CF and LLM-augmented retrieval, but solid foundational work continues.

## Contrastive CF with Data Augmentation

### Are Graph Augmentations Necessary? Simple Graph Contrastive Learning for Recommendation (SimGCL)
- **Authors:** Yu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08679)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Coder-Yu/QRec)
- **Summary:** Shows that graph augmentation (edge dropout, node dropout) in contrastive CF is unnecessary — adding uniform noise to embedding space achieves the same uniformity effect; the resulting SimGCL is simpler and stronger than SGL while clarifying what contrastive loss actually contributes to CF performance.
- **Tags:** `#simgcl` `#contrastive` `#ssl` `#cf` `#augmentation`

## Cold-start CF

### Improving Item Cold-start Recommendation via Model-Agnostic Conditional Variational Autoencoder
- **Authors:** (search: cold-start recommendation conditional variational autoencoder model-agnostic 2022 SIGIR CIKM)
- **Paper:** (search: cold-start collaborative filtering CVAE item content 2022)
- **Summary:** Conditions a variational autoencoder on item content features to generate pseudo-interaction embeddings for cold-start items, enabling them to be scored by any existing collaborative filtering model without retraining; achieves significant improvement on zero- and few-shot item scenarios.
- **Tags:** `#cold-start` `#vae` `#cf` `#content`
