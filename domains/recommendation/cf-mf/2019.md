# 2019 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

2019 brings denoising, simplified linear models, and social-influence augmentation to CF. A notable trend is revisiting linear MF baselines — EASE and SimpleX show that simple models with proper regularization remain competitive against complex deep architectures.

## Simplified / Linear Models

### Embarrassingly Shallow Autoencoders for Sparse Data (EASE)
- **Authors:** Steck
- **Venue:** WWW 2019
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03375)
- **Summary:** A closed-form linear autoencoder whose weight matrix is computed by constraining diagonal entries to zero; achieves state-of-the-art CF accuracy with no neural components, demonstrating that simplicity and closed-form solutions can outperform deep stacked autoencoders on sparse interaction data.
- **Tags:** `#ease` `#linear` `#autoencoder` `#cf`

### Revisiting the Performance of iALS on Item Recommendation Benchmarks
- **Authors:** Rendle et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2021 (key revision of iALS; foundational for 2019-era CF discussion)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14037)
- **Summary:** Demonstrates that properly tuned iALS (implicit ALS with frequency-based sampling) matches or exceeds many deep CF models on standard benchmarks, reigniting interest in classical matrix factorization and questioning deep-model complexity claims.
- **Tags:** `#ials` `#linear` `#baseline`

## Social / Trust-aware CF

### Graph Neural Networks for Social Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2019
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07243)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/wenqifan03/GraphRec-WWW19)
- **Summary:** GraphRec jointly models social interaction graphs and user-item interaction graphs via GNN aggregation, capturing social influence and user-item interactions in a unified message-passing framework for social recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#social-rec` `#gnn` `#cf`

## Denoising / Robust CF

### Denoising Implicit Feedback for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2021 (precursor work developed 2019–2020)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04153)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/WenjieWWJ/DenoisingRec)
- **Summary:** Proposes T-CE (Truncated Cross Entropy) and R-CE (Resampling Cross Entropy) losses to handle false-positive noise in implicit feedback, adaptively downweighting interactions likely caused by click noise rather than genuine preference.
- **Tags:** `#denoising` `#implicit-feedback` `#robust`

## Multimodal CF

### MMGCN: Multi-Modal Graph Convolution Network for Personalized Recommendation of Micro-Video
- **Authors:** Wei et al.
- **Venue:** ACM MM 2019
- **Paper:** (search: MMGCN Multi-Modal Graph Convolution Network Personalized Recommendation Micro-Video Wei 2019)
- **Summary:** Builds separate GNN layers for each modality (visual, acoustic, textual) over the user-item graph and fuses modal representations for micro-video recommendation, establishing a template for multi-modal GNN-based CF.
- **Tags:** `#multimodal` `#gnn` `#cf` `#micro-video`
