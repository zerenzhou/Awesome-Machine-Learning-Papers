# 2018 — Collaborative Filtering & Matrix Factorization Papers

2018 sees variational and adversarial generative models applied to CF: VAEs produce calibrated uncertainty for recommendation while GANs provide robust negative sampling. Multi-modal side information is woven into MF through visual and social features.

## Variational / Generative CF

### Variational Autoencoders for Collaborative Filtering (Mult-VAE)
- **Authors:** Liang et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2018
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.05814)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/dawenl/vae_cf)
- **Summary:** Applies a multinomial likelihood VAE to model bag-of-items user consumption vectors, introducing KL-annealing and a Bayesian view of CF that outperforms linear and MF baselines on large-scale implicit data; became a standard deep CF baseline.
- **Tags:** `#vae` `#generative` `#cf`

### CFGAN: A Generic Collaborative Filtering Framework Based on Generative Adversarial Networks
- **Authors:** Chae et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2018
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3269206.3271743)
- **Summary:** Generator produces plausible user interaction vectors; discriminator distinguishes real from synthetic; the adversarial game yields high-quality implicit negative examples that improve MF training without manual negative sampling heuristics.
- **Tags:** `#gan` `#cf` `#negative-sampling`

## Multi-modal / Side Information

### Spectral Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Zheng et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2018
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3240323.3240343)
- **Summary:** Encodes the user-item bipartite graph structure via spectral graph convolution into latent factors, augmenting MF with structural information and anticipating GNN-based CF methods developed in subsequent years.
- **Tags:** `#spectral` `#graph` `#cf`

### Outer Product-based Neural Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** He et al.
- **Venue:** IJCAI 2018
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.03912)
- **Summary:** ONCF uses an outer product of user and item embeddings to form an interaction map, then applies CNN over the map to capture high-order correlations; demonstrates that 2D convolutional feature interaction outperforms pointwise MLP NCF.
- **Tags:** `#oncf` `#cnn` `#cf`

## Ranking / Loss Improvements

### LambdaFM: Learning Optimal Ranking with Factorization Machines Using Lambda Surrogates
- **Authors:** Yuan et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2016 (influential in 2018 MF ranking research)
- **Paper:** (search: LambdaFM Learning Optimal Ranking Factorization Machines Lambda Surrogates Yuan CIKM 2016)
- **Summary:** Adapts LambdaRank's ranking-aware gradient surrogates to FM optimization, enabling FM models to directly optimize NDCG-style ranking metrics rather than pointwise or pairwise losses.
- **Tags:** `#lambdarank` `#fm` `#ranking`

### Bootstrapping User and Item Representations for One-Class Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** Pan et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2018
- **Paper:** (search: Bootstrapping User Item Representations One-Class Collaborative Filtering Pan SIGIR 2018)
- **Summary:** Introduces a bootstrapping curriculum that gradually refines user and item representations under one-class (implicit) settings by treating high-confidence negatives as pseudo-positive updates, improving early convergence of MF models.
- **Tags:** `#implicit-feedback` `#bootstrapping` `#cf`
