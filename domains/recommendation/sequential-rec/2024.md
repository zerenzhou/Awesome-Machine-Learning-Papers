# 2024 — Sequential Recommendation Papers

State-space models challenge Transformers for efficiency (Mamba4Rec, LRURec, SSD4Rec); LLM-based sequential recommenders move from prototype to systematic evaluation; generative recommendation at production scale debuts from Meta.

## State-Space Models (SSM)

### Mamba4Rec: Towards Efficient Sequential Recommendation with Selective State Space Models
- **Authors:** Liu et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03900)
- **Summary:** Adapts the Mamba selective-SSM architecture to sequential recommendation; achieves SASRec-comparable accuracy with significantly lower inference FLOPs on long interaction sequences, establishing Mamba as a viable attention alternative for sequential rec.
- **Tags:** `#ssm` `#mamba` `#efficiency` `#sequential`

### Linear Recurrent Units for Sequential Recommendation (LRURec)
- **Authors:** Yue et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2024
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3616855.3635843)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/yueqirex/LRURec)
- **Summary:** Replaces self-attention with a diagonal linear recurrent unit (LRU), achieving O(L) inference cost with SASRec-comparable accuracy; systematic ablations show that recurrence structure is more important than complex gating for sequential rec.
- **Tags:** `#ssm` `#rnn` `#efficiency` `#sequential`

### SSD4Rec: A Structured State Space Duality Model for Efficient Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Ren et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01605)
- **Summary:** Leverages the structured state space duality (SSD) formulation from Mamba-2 for sequential recommendation, providing a unified attention–SSM view; achieves better long-sequence modeling than Mamba4Rec with improved hardware-parallel training.
- **Tags:** `#ssm` `#mamba` `#efficiency` `#long-sequence`

## Generative / Production-Scale

### Actions Speak Louder than Words: Trillion-Parameter Sequential Transducers for Generative Recommendation (HSTU)
- **Authors:** Zhai et al.
- **Venue:** ICML 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17152)
- **Summary:** Meta's production generative recommendation system replaces attention with hierarchical sequential transduction units (HSTU), achieving 12.4× FLOP efficiency at trillion-parameter scale; demonstrates that linear-time sequential architectures can surpass Transformer-based rankers in online A/B tests.
- **Tags:** `#ssm` `#industrial` `#generative` `#efficiency` `#long-sequence`

## LLM-augmented

### LLMRec: Large Language Models with Graph Augmentation for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wei et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2024
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3616855.3635853)
- **Summary:** Uses LLMs to augment user/item profiles and interaction graphs with generated textual descriptions, then feeds the enriched data to a sequential/graph recommender; achieves robust improvements on sparse and cold-start sequential benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#llm` `#gnn` `#data-augmentation` `#cold-start`

### TASTE: Text-Matching-based Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Liu et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2024
- **Paper:** (search: TASTE Text-Matching-based Sequential Recommendation WWW 2024)
- **Summary:** Encodes user behavior histories as natural-language prompts and retrieves the next item by matching against LLM-encoded item text descriptions; bridges ID-based and text-based sequential rec without expensive end-to-end fine-tuning.
- **Tags:** `#llm` `#text-matching` `#sequential` `#retrieval`
