# 2019 — Sequential Recommendation Papers

Transformer dominates: BERT4Rec applies bidirectional masked-item training, NextItNet shows dilated CNNs can match RNNs at scale, and HGN introduces gating for long-term preference. Industrial-scale systems at Alibaba complete the picture.

## Transformer-based

### BERT4Rec: Sequential Recommendation with Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformer
- **Authors:** Sun et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2019
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3357384.3357895)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/FeiSun/BERT4Rec)
- **Summary:** Adapts BERT's masked-language-model pre-training to sequential recommendation, randomly masking items in the history and training the model to predict them; the bidirectional context consistently outperforms unidirectional SASRec on several benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#transformer` `#bert` `#masked-modeling` `#ssl`

### Hierarchical Gating Networks for Sequential Recommendation (HGN)
- **Authors:** Ma et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2019
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3292500.3330984)
- **Summary:** Uses feature gating and instance gating at two levels — long-term interest via user/item gates, short-term interest via a recency window — to produce fine-grained sequential preference representations.
- **Tags:** `#gating` `#hierarchical` `#sequential`

## CNN-based

### A Simple Convolutional Generative Network for Next Item Recommendation (NextItNet)
- **Authors:** Yuan et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2019
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3289600.3290975)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/fajieyuan/nextitnet)
- **Summary:** Employs stacked dilated causal convolutions to grow the receptive field exponentially without recurrence, enabling parallel training and matching or exceeding GRU4Rec/SASRec on multiple benchmarks at higher throughput.
- **Tags:** `#cnn` `#dilated-conv` `#session-based`

## Industrial / Matching

### Sequential Deep Matching Model for Online Large-scale Recommender System (SDM)
- **Authors:** Lv et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2019
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3357384.3357818)
- **Summary:** Alibaba's production sequential matching system that fuses short-term session behavior via LSTM and long-term preference via multi-head attention, serving hundreds of millions of users in Taobao.
- **Tags:** `#industrial` `#matching` `#rnn` `#transformer`

## Graph-based

### Session-based Recommendation with Graph Neural Networks (SR-GNN)
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** AAAI 2019
- **Paper:** [AAAI Proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/3804)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/CRIPAC-DIG/SR-GNN)
- **Summary:** Constructs a directed item-transition graph from each session and applies gated graph neural networks to capture complex item transitions beyond simple chains, then uses a soft-attention readout to generate session representations.
- **Tags:** `#gnn` `#session-based` `#graph`

## Survey

### Sequential Recommender Systems: Challenges, Progress and Prospects
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** IJCAI 2019
- **Paper:** [IJCAI Proceedings](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2019/883)
- **Summary:** Comprehensive survey covering Markov-chain, RNN, attention, and memory-based sequential rec methods; defines evaluation protocols and open challenges; a standard reference for newcomers.
- **Tags:** `#survey` `#sequential`
