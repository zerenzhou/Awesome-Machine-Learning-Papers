# 2017 — Sequential Recommendation Papers

RNN architectures mature: attention is added on top of GRU, hierarchical designs bridge within-session and cross-session modeling, and neighborhood methods offer a strong challenge to deep models.

## RNN / Attention

### Neural Attentive Session-based Recommendation (NARM)
- **Authors:** Li et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2017
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3132847.3132926)
- **Summary:** Adds a dual-channel local and global attention mechanism over GRU hidden states to capture both the main user purpose and short-term sequential patterns within a session, outperforming vanilla GRU4Rec.
- **Tags:** `#rnn` `#attention` `#session-based`

### Improved Recurrent Neural Networks for Session-based Recommendations (GRU4Rec+)
- **Authors:** Hidasi & Karatzoglou
- **Venue:** CIKM 2017 (RecSys Workshop 2016 earlier version)
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3125486.3125494)
- **Summary:** Introduces BPR-max and TOP1-max loss functions along with data-augmentation strategies for GRU4Rec; substantially closes the gap between session-based RNNs and strong k-NN baselines.
- **Tags:** `#rnn` `#session-based` `#loss-function`

### Hierarchical RNN for Session-aware Recommendation (HRNN)
- **Authors:** Quadrana et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2017
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3109859.3109896)
- **Summary:** Stacks a session-level GRU over a user-level GRU to propagate latent preference states across sessions, jointly modeling within-session dynamics and long-term user evolution.
- **Tags:** `#rnn` `#hierarchical` `#cross-session`

## Neighborhood / Evaluation

### When Recurrent Neural Networks Meet the Neighborhood for Session-based Recommendation (SKNN study)
- **Authors:** Jannach & Ludewig
- **Venue:** RecSys 2017
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3109859.3109872)
- **Summary:** Shows that a carefully tuned k-NN session-based method (SKNN) matches or surpasses GRU4Rec on several benchmarks, sparking important discussion about proper evaluation protocols in sequential rec.
- **Tags:** `#knn` `#session-based` `#evaluation`

## Latent Factor

### Recurrent Recommender Networks (RRN)
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2017
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3018661.3018689)
- **Summary:** Models temporal dynamics of both users and items using LSTM, capturing how user tastes and item qualities evolve over time without requiring manual time-binning.
- **Tags:** `#rnn` `#temporal` `#latent-factor`

### Translation-based Recommendation (TransRec)
- **Authors:** He et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2017
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3109859.3109882)
- **Summary:** Embeds sequential transitions as vector translations (user + current item ≈ next item) in a shared latent space; lightweight yet competitive, and widely used as a sequential baseline.
- **Tags:** `#translation` `#embedding` `#sequential`
