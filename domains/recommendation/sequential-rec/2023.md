# 2023 — Sequential Recommendation Papers

Diffusion models enter sequential rec (DiffRec, DreamRec), LLMs are repurposed as recommenders (LLMRank, BIGRec), and the community debates whether ID-based or text-based representations will dominate.

## Diffusion-based

### Diffusion Recommender Model (DiffRec)
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2023
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3539618.3591663)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/YiyanXu/DiffRec)
- **Summary:** First work to apply denoising diffusion probabilistic models to collaborative filtering; adds noise to user interaction vectors and trains a Transformer-based denoiser; also introduces L-DiffRec that clusters users for scalability, achieving strong top-N accuracy on sparse data.
- **Tags:** `#diffusion` `#generative` `#sequential`

### Generate What You Prefer: Reshaping Sequential Recommendation via Guided Diffusion (DreamRec)
- **Authors:** Yang et al.
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04925)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/yangzhengyi98/dreamrec)
- **Summary:** Reformulates next-item prediction as a conditional generation problem: a diffusion model is guided by the user's historical sequence to directly generate a target item embedding, avoiding explicit negative sampling and achieving state-of-the-art on multiple benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#diffusion` `#generative` `#sequential` `#conditional-generation`

## LLM-augmented

### Is ChatGPT a Good Recommender? A Preliminary Study (LLMRank)
- **Authors:** Liu et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.10149)
- **Summary:** Systematically evaluates ChatGPT as a zero-shot and few-shot sequential recommender via ranking prompts; finds strong performance on popular items but struggles with tail items and long sequences, motivating future LLM-based rec research.
- **Tags:** `#llm` `#zero-shot` `#evaluation` `#sequential`

### BIGRec: Grounding Language Models for Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Bao et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09409)
- **Summary:** Fine-tunes LLaMA-style generative LLMs with a grounding step that maps generated item-description tokens back to actual item IDs in the catalog; achieves strong few-shot sequential rec without requiring item-ID-indexed training.
- **Tags:** `#llm` `#generative` `#sequential` `#grounding`

## Contrastive / Equivariant

### Equivariant Contrastive Learning for Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Zhou et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2023
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3604915.3608785)
- **Summary:** Observes that standard augmentations (crop, mask, reorder) change the sequential intent label and introduces equivariant contrastive objectives that match the transformation applied to the sequence, producing more label-consistent positive pairs.
- **Tags:** `#contrastive` `#ssl` `#equivariance` `#sequential`

## Time-aware / Attention

### Attention Mixtures for Time-Aware Sequential Recommendation (MOJITO)
- **Authors:** Lacombe et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2023
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3539618.3591951)
- **Summary:** Proposes a mixture-of-attention heads where each head specializes on a different temporal granularity, controlled by soft time-interval gates; combines the strengths of TiSASRec-style temporal encoding with multi-head diversity.
- **Tags:** `#transformer` `#time-aware` `#attention` `#sequential`

## Self-supervised Survey

### Self-Supervised Learning for Recommender Systems: A Survey
- **Authors:** Yu et al.
- **Venue:** TKDE 2023
- **Paper:** [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10144391)
- **Summary:** Comprehensive survey of SSL techniques applied to recommender systems, covering contrastive, generative, and predictive approaches across CF, session-based, and sequential settings; the authoritative reference for the SSL-in-RecSys literature.
- **Tags:** `#survey` `#ssl` `#contrastive` `#sequential`
