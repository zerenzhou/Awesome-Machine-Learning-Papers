# 2016 — Sequential Recommendation Papers

The year GRU4Rec (already in classic.md) launches the deep sequential rec era; follow-up work extends it with parallel architectures, richer item features, early CNN-based content encoding, and industry-scale session modeling at YouTube.

## RNN / Session-based

### Parallel Recurrent Neural Network Architectures for Feature-rich Session-based Recommendations (p-RNN)
- **Authors:** Hidasi et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2016
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2959100.2959167)
- **Summary:** Extends GRU4Rec with parallel RNN streams that separately encode rich item features (images, text) and interaction context, then merge at inference — substantially improving cold-start and feature-rich scenarios over the vanilla GRU baseline.
- **Tags:** `#rnn` `#session-based` `#side-information`

### Improved Session-based Recommendations Using Multi-View Data
- **Authors:** Tan et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2016
- **Paper:** (search: Improved Session-based Recommendations Multi-View Data Tan RecSys 2016)
- **Summary:** Introduces sequence-splitting data augmentation and leverages multi-view inputs (e.g., item content, context) on top of GRU4Rec; an early study of training-data strategies and input diversification for session-based RNNs.
- **Tags:** `#rnn` `#session-based` `#data-augmentation`

## Industrial / Large-scale

### Deep Neural Networks for YouTube Recommendations
- **Authors:** Covington et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2016
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2959100.2959190)
- **Summary:** YouTube's two-tower deep retrieval + ranking system that encodes watch history sequences as averaged embeddings; introduced the candidate-generation / ranking pipeline that became the industrial standard and directly inspired subsequent sequential rec research.
- **Tags:** `#industrial` `#sequential` `#two-tower` `#deep-learning`

## CNN / Content-based

### Convolutional Matrix Factorization for Document Context-Aware Recommendation (ConvMF)
- **Authors:** Kim et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2016
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2959100.2959165)
- **Summary:** Applies CNN to encode document text into latent factors for collaborative filtering; establishes CNN-based feature encoding for interaction modeling that later informs convolutional sequential models like Caser and NextItNet.
- **Tags:** `#cnn` `#content-based` `#document`

## Visual / Side-information

### VBPR: Visual Bayesian Personalized Ranking from Implicit Feedback
- **Authors:** He & McAuley
- **Venue:** AAAI 2016
- **Paper:** [AAAI Proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/9973)
- **Summary:** Augments BPR matrix factorization with visual CNN features extracted from product images; a foundational multi-modal sequential recommendation baseline widely used as the visual-features reference in subsequent session-based and sequential rec with side information.
- **Tags:** `#visual` `#multimodal` `#bpr` `#side-information`
