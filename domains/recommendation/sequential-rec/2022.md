# 2022 — Sequential Recommendation Papers

Contrastive learning consolidates as the dominant paradigm: CL4SRec, DuoRec, and ICLRec all publish formally; FMLP-Rec challenges attention with pure MLP; and the first LLM-text integration experiments (UniSRec) appear.

## Contrastive / Self-supervised

### Contrastive Learning for Sequential Recommendation (CL4SRec)
- **Authors:** Xie et al.
- **Venue:** ICDE 2022
- **Paper:** [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9835621)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/RUCAIBox/CL4SRec)
- **Summary:** The formal publication of CL4SRec: defines item crop, mask, and reorder augmentations and applies InfoNCE contrastive objectives to SASRec-style encoders, establishing the standard framework for contrastive sequential rec.
- **Tags:** `#contrastive` `#ssl` `#data-augmentation` `#transformer`

### DuoRec: Contrastive Learning for Representation Degeneration Problem in Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Qiu et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2022
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3488560.3498433)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Willy-Chap/DuoRec)
- **Summary:** Identifies representation degeneration (item embeddings collapsing to anisotropic clusters) in SASRec and proposes model-level augmentation via dropout-based positive pairs combined with a supervised contrastive objective, achieving better embedding uniformity.
- **Tags:** `#contrastive` `#ssl` `#representation-degeneration` `#transformer`

### Intent Contrastive Learning for Sequential Recommendation (ICLRec)
- **Authors:** Chen et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2022
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3485447.3512093)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/salesforce/ICLRec)
- **Summary:** Introduces latent intent variables via EM-based prototype clustering and uses intent-conditioned contrastive objectives to align sequence representations with latent user goals; improves over CL4SRec especially on noisy or mixed-intent histories.
- **Tags:** `#contrastive` `#ssl` `#intent` `#clustering`

## MLP / Alternative Architectures

### Filter-enhanced MLP is All You Need for Sequential Recommendation (FMLP-Rec)
- **Authors:** Zhou et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2022
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3485447.3512111)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Woeee/FMLP-Rec)
- **Summary:** Replaces self-attention entirely with learnable frequency-domain filters (FFT-based) applied in an MLP-Mixer-style all-MLP architecture; achieves competitive performance with SASRec/BERT4Rec while being faster to train, challenging the assumption that attention is necessary.
- **Tags:** `#mlp` `#frequency-domain` `#efficiency` `#sequential`

## Text / LLM Integration

### Towards Universal Sequence Representation Learning for Recommender Systems (UniSRec)
- **Authors:** Hou et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2022
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3534678.3539381)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/RUCAIBox/UniSRec)
- **Summary:** Encodes item textual descriptions with a pre-trained language model (BERT) and learns cross-domain sequential representations via MoE-enhanced adapters; enables transferable sequential rec across domains without item-ID overlap.
- **Tags:** `#transformer` `#pre-training` `#cross-domain` `#text`

## Evaluation / Reproducibility

### A Systematic Review and Replicability Study of BERT4Rec for Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** Petrov & Macdonald
- **Venue:** RecSys 2022
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3523227.3546788)
- **Summary:** Reproduces BERT4Rec under multiple preprocessing protocols; finds results vary substantially with data-split choices and that simpler models often match BERT4Rec under fair conditions — established stricter evaluation standards for the entire sequential rec community.
- **Tags:** `#reproducibility` `#evaluation` `#benchmark` `#transformer`
