# Classic — Sequential Recommendation

Foundational works that established sequential modeling for recommendation before the deep learning era, bridging Markov chains and latent factor models.

#### Pre-Deep Foundations

### Factorizing Personalized Markov Chains for Next-Basket Recommendation
- **Authors:** Rendle et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2010
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1772690.1772773)
- **Summary:** FPMC unifies matrix factorization and Markov chain transition modeling for next-basket recommendation, capturing both long-term preferences and short-term sequential dynamics.
- **Tags:** `#fpmc` `#markov` `#seminal`

### Learning Hierarchical Representation Model for Next-basket Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2015
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2766462.2767694)
- **Summary:** HRM captures both sequential behavior and user-item interaction patterns through a two-level hierarchical representation combining max-pooling and user latent factors.
- **Tags:** `#hrm` `#hierarchical`

### Fusing Similarity Models with Markov Chains for Sparse Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** He & McAuley
- **Venue:** ICDM 2016
- **Paper:** [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7837942)
- **Summary:** Fossil interpolates similarity-based CF with personalized Markov chains to handle sparse sequential data, substantially improving next-item prediction in sparse settings.
- **Tags:** `#fossil` `#sparse`
