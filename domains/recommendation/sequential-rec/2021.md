# 2021 — Sequential Recommendation Papers

Contrastive and data-augmentation strategies take center stage: CoSeRec and COTREC establish self-supervised baselines; ASReP addresses cold-start via reverse pre-training; LightSANs and Locker push efficient attention. The CL4SRec preprint (arXiv 2010.14395) circulates widely, setting up the 2022 contrastive wave.

## Contrastive / Self-supervised

### Contrastive Self-supervised Sequential Recommendation with Robust Augmentation (CoSeRec)
- **Authors:** Liu et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.06479)
- **Summary:** Introduces semantics-aware augmentation operators (substitute, insert) guided by item correlations to generate more informative contrastive views than random crop/mask, improving robustness on sparse sequences.
- **Tags:** `#contrastive` `#ssl` `#data-augmentation` `#sparse`

## Data Augmentation / Pre-training

### Augmenting Sequential Recommendation with Pseudo-Prior Items via Reversely Pre-training Transformer (ASReP)
- **Authors:** Liu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2021
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3404835.3462944)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/DyGRec/ASReP)
- **Summary:** Addresses cold-start short-sequence users by pre-training SASRec in reverse order to generate synthetic pseudo-prior items that are prepended to real histories, substantially improving next-item prediction for users with few interactions.
- **Tags:** `#pre-training` `#data-augmentation` `#cold-start` `#transformer`

## Efficient Attention

### Lighter and Better: Low-Rank Decomposed Self-Attention Networks for Next-Item Recommendation (LightSANs)
- **Authors:** Fan et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2021
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3404835.3462978)
- **Summary:** Replaces the full item–item self-attention matrix with a low-rank decomposition (item–latent-factor–item), reducing complexity from O(n²) to O(nk) while maintaining competitive accuracy; enables efficient sequential modeling for long interaction histories.
- **Tags:** `#transformer` `#efficiency` `#low-rank` `#sequential`

### Locker: Locally Constrained Self-Attentive Sequential Recommendation
- **Authors:** He et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2021
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3459637.3482136)
- **Summary:** Introduces a locally constrained attention mask that limits each position's attention span to its local neighborhood, improving locality inductive bias for sequential rec and reducing over-smoothing in long sequences.
- **Tags:** `#transformer` `#local-attention` `#sequential`

## Graph / Intent

### Self-Supervised Graph Co-training for Session-based Recommendation (COTREC)
- **Authors:** Xia et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2021
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3459637.3482388)
- **Summary:** Constructs item-transition and session-similarity graphs and applies cross-view contrastive co-training to simultaneously capture local item-transition patterns and global session correlations; strong performance on sparse session datasets.
- **Tags:** `#gnn` `#contrastive` `#session-based` `#ssl`
