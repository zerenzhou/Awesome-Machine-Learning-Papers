# 2021 — CTR Prediction & Ranking Papers

2021 brings ETA's hash-based efficient long-sequence targeting, STAR's multi-domain single-model architecture, and CAN's feature co-action network for fine-grained target-context interaction, as industrial systems push sequence length and domain coverage further.

## Long-sequence / Target-aware Interest

### Efficient Target-Aware Attention for Long-term User Behavior Sequence Modeling (ETA)
- **Authors:** Chen et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.04468)
- **Summary:** Replaces exact top-K search in SIM with SimHash-based approximation for target-aware retrieval from arbitrarily long behavior sequences, achieving sub-linear retrieval time while maintaining CTR accuracy; deployed in Alibaba's production ranking system.
- **Tags:** `#eta` `#long-sequence` `#simhash` `#alibaba`

### CAN: Feature Co-Action Network for Click-Through Rate Prediction
- **Authors:** Bian et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2022
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.05625)
- **Summary:** Introduces co-action units that learn explicit multi-order co-action patterns between target item features and each historical behavior feature, providing finer-grained interaction than DIN's single attention weight per behavior.
- **Tags:** `#can` `#co-action` `#target-aware` `#alibaba`

## Multi-domain / Multi-scenario

### STAR: One Model to Serve All
- **Authors:** Sheng et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.11427)
- **Summary:** Star-topology model with shared center network and domain-specific networks multiplied element-wise; outperforms per-domain models and parameter-sharing baselines across multiple business scenarios at Alibaba.
- **Tags:** `#star` `#multi-domain` `#multi-task`

## Bias Correction in Ranking

### Unbiased Learning-to-Rank with Biased Feedback (Revisited)
- **Authors:** Joachims et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2017 (seminal, widely adopted in 2021 production CTR stacks)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04468)
- **Summary:** Proves that propensity-weighted ranking losses yield unbiased estimates from click logs under position bias; the foundational theory behind bias-corrected CTR model training adopted in 2020–2021 production systems.
- **Tags:** `#position-bias` `#ips` `#unbiased-ltr`

## Contrastive Pre-training for CTR

### Self-supervised Graph Learning for Recommendation (applied to CTR)
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10783)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/wujcan/SGL-TensorFlow)
- **Summary:** SGL introduces stochastic graph augmentations (node/edge dropout, random walk) with InfoNCE contrastive loss on the user-item graph, learning more robust embeddings for downstream CTR and ranking; foundational for graph-contrastive rec.
- **Tags:** `#sgl` `#contrastive` `#ssl` `#graph`

## Lifelong Interest Modeling

### Deep Retrieval: Learning A Retrievable Structure for Large-Scale Recommendations
- **Authors:** Gao et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.07203)
- **Summary:** Learns a tree-structured index for large-scale retrieval where each path corresponds to a cluster of items; end-to-end beam-search-based retrieval in a single model replaces the standard ANN index in two-tower pipelines at ByteDance.
- **Tags:** `#deep-retrieval` `#tree-index` `#retrieval` `#bytedance`
