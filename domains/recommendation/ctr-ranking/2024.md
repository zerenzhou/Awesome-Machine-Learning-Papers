# 2024 — CTR Prediction & Ranking Papers

2024 sees production-scale generative recommendation (HSTU at Meta), Wukong's scaling law for CTR, and Pinterest's large-scale real-time ranking redesign. Sequence modeling surpasses feature-engineering as the dominant industrial CTR improvement axis.

## Generative Production Ranking

### Actions Speak Louder than Words: Trillion-Parameter Sequential Transducers for Generative Recommendation (HSTU)
- **Authors:** Zhai et al.
- **Venue:** ICML 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17152)
- **Summary:** Meta replaces Transformer-based rankers with hierarchical sequential transduction units (HSTU) that operate on user action sequences as the primary signal; achieves 12.4× FLOP efficiency at trillion-parameter scale and demonstrates in production A/B tests that generative approaches outperform discriminative CTR models.
- **Tags:** `#hstu` `#generative` `#sequential-transduction` `#industrial` `#meta` `#scaling`

## Scaling Laws for CTR

### Wukong: Towards a Scaling Law for Large-Scale Recommendation
- **Authors:** Zhang et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02545)
- **Summary:** Meta's Wukong stacks factorization machines and MLP blocks in a repeating pattern and systematically varies model size and training compute, discovering empirical power-law scaling for CTR AUC — the first published scaling law for recommendation models analogous to LLM scaling laws.
- **Tags:** `#wukong` `#scaling` `#fm` `#industrial` `#meta`

## Pinterest Real-time Ranking

### PinnerFormer: Sequence Modeling for User Representation at Pinterest
- **Authors:** Pancha et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2022 / extended production 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04507)
- **Summary:** Encodes a user's pin engagement history with a Transformer to produce dense real-time user representations updated on a rolling basis; these representations serve as cross-surface features across multiple Pinterest ranking tasks, reducing cold-start latency and improving fresh-user CTR.
- **Tags:** `#pinnerformer` `#user-representation` `#transformer` `#pinterest` `#industrial`
