# 2023 — CTR Prediction & Ranking Papers

2023 is a prolific year for industrial CTR: TransAct productionizes Transformer-based user-action modeling at Twitter/X, PEPNet injects personalized priors at Kuaishou, and sequence-length scaling continues with TWIN's two-stage lifelong interest modeling. The community also begins integrating LLM-derived features into CTR feature spaces.

## Industrial Transformer for Action Sequences

### TransAct: Transformer-based Realtime User Action Model for Twitter's Recommendation System
- **Authors:** Xia et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00248)
- **Summary:** Applies a Transformer directly over a user's recent real-time engagement sequence (tweet interactions) for tweet ranking; replaces aggregated feature statistics with full sequence encoding, capturing nuanced temporal engagement patterns; shows significant production engagement gains at Twitter/X.
- **Tags:** `#transact` `#transformer` `#real-time` `#twitter` `#industrial`

## Personalized Prior Injection

### PEPNet: Parameter and Embedding Personalized Network for Infusing with Personalized Prior Information
- **Authors:** Chang et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.01115)
- **Summary:** Injects personalized prior information (user demographics, scene context) through learnable gating units at both embedding and parameter levels, enabling a single multi-domain CTR model to adapt across 10+ business scenarios at Kuaishou with consistent per-scenario gains.
- **Tags:** `#pepnet` `#personalization` `#multi-domain` `#kuaishou` `#industrial`

## Lifelong Behavior Sequence

### TWIN: TWo-stage Interest Network for Lifelong User Behavior Modeling in CTR Prediction
- **Authors:** Chang et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2023
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02352)
- **Summary:** GSU (general search unit) uses lightweight matching to retrieve top-K behaviors from a user's full lifelong history (up to 100K items); ESU (exact search unit) then performs target-attention over this retrieved set, enabling practical full-history interest modeling at production scale at Kuaishou.
- **Tags:** `#twin` `#long-sequence` `#two-stage` `#target-attention` `#kuaishou`
