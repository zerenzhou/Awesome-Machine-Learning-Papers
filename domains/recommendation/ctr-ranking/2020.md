# 2020 — CTR Prediction & Ranking Papers

2020 advances long-sequence modeling with SIM's two-stage search mechanism and introduces PLE's progressive layered experts for better multi-task learning. Contrastive and self-supervised signals begin appearing in CTR contexts.

## Long-sequence Behavior Modeling

### Search-based User Interest Modeling with Lifelong Sequential Behavior Data for Click-Through Rate Prediction (SIM)
- **Authors:** Pi et al.
- **Venue:** CIKM 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.05639)
- **Summary:** Two-stage approach: a fast general search unit retrieves top-K relevant behaviors from the user's full lifetime history using hard/soft search, then a fine-grained search unit applies DIN-style attention; handles 10K+ behavior sequences efficiently in production at Alibaba.
- **Tags:** `#sim` `#long-sequence` `#two-stage` `#alibaba`

### User Behavior Retrieval for Click-Through Rate Prediction (UBR4CTR)
- **Authors:** Qin et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2020
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3397271.3401440)
- **Summary:** Retrieves a subset of user behaviors most relevant to the target item for CTR prediction, comparing dense vector retrieval (soft search) with term-based methods and demonstrating significant gains over truncated-history baselines.
- **Tags:** `#ubr4ctr` `#behavior-retrieval` `#long-sequence`

## Multi-task Learning

### Progressive Layered Extraction (PLE): A Novel Multi-Task Learning Approach for Recommendations
- **Authors:** Tang et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2020
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3383313.3412236)
- **Summary:** Introduces explicitly separated task-specific and shared extraction layers with progressive extraction that avoids the seesaw phenomenon in MMoE; achieves better balance among diverse recommendation tasks at Tencent Video.
- **Tags:** `#ple` `#multi-task` `#extraction` `#tencent`

### Entire Space Multi-Task Model with Parameter Personalization for Large-Scale Recommendation (ESM2)
- **Authors:** Wen et al.
- **Venue:** (search: ESM2 Entire Space Multi-Task Personalization Large-Scale Recommendation Wen 2020)
- **Paper:** (search: ESM2 parameter personalization multi-task recommendation 2020)
- **Summary:** Extends ESMM by incorporating user-specific personalization into the multi-task framework, addressing variation in the CTR→CVR sequence with personalized module parameters across user segments.
- **Tags:** `#esmm` `#multi-task` `#personalization`

## Attention / Interaction Innovation

### FuxiCTR: An Open Benchmark for Click-Through Rate Prediction
- **Authors:** Zhu et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2021 (released as open benchmark in 2020–21)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.05794)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/xue-pai/FuxiCTR)
- **Summary:** Open-source benchmark and model library covering 30+ CTR prediction models with unified data processing, evaluation protocols, and hyperparameter search; enables reproducible comparison and dramatically reduced re-implementation overhead in the community.
- **Tags:** `#benchmark` `#ctr` `#reproducibility`

### DCN V2: Improved Deep & Cross Network (Applied to CTR 2020)
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.13535)
- **Summary:** (See deep-rec/2021.md for full entry.) From a CTR perspective, DCN-V2's mixture-of-low-rank cross layers deliver the most significant accuracy improvement in the Cross Network family, validated on production CTR datasets at Google.
- **Tags:** `#dcnv2` `#cross-network` `#ctr` `#google`
