# 2018 — Deep Recommenders Papers

2018 delivers compressed interaction models (xDeepFM's compressed interaction network) and convolution-based sequence encoding (Caser), while mixture-of-expert gating enters production multi-task stacks. The year consolidates the FM→Deep transition begun by DeepFM.

## Explicit High-order Feature Interaction

### xDeepFM: Combining Explicit and Implicit Feature Interactions for Recommender Systems
- **Authors:** Lian et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2018
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05170)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Leavingseason/xDeepFM)
- **Summary:** Introduces the Compressed Interaction Network (CIN) that models explicit high-order feature interactions at vector level (rather than bit level), combined with a DNN for implicit interactions; unifies cross-network ideas with FM and outperforms DeepFM on multiple CTR datasets.
- **Tags:** `#xdeepfm` `#cin` `#feature-interaction`

### Recurrent Recommender Systems
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2017 (widely adopted in 2018 deep-rec evaluation)
- **Paper:** (search: Recurrent Recommender Systems Wu WSDM 2017)
- **Summary:** Integrates RNN temporal dynamics into matrix factorization for rating prediction, capturing the evolution of user preferences and item attractiveness over time; an influential hybrid that bridges sequential and deep CF paradigms.
- **Tags:** `#rnn` `#mf` `#temporal`

## Convolutional / Sequence-based

### Personalized Top-N Sequential Recommendation via Convolutional Sequence Embedding (Caser)
- **Authors:** Tang & Wang
- **Venue:** WSDM 2018
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07426)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/graytowne/caser_pytorch)
- **Summary:** Frames sequential recommendation as image-style classification: embeds the recent L interactions into a 2D matrix and applies horizontal/vertical CNNs to capture union-level sequential patterns; a foundational non-RNN deep sequential model.
- **Tags:** `#caser` `#cnn` `#sequential`

## Feature Interaction Design

### Product-based Neural Networks for User Response Prediction over Multi-field Categorical Data (PNN-v2)
- **Authors:** Qu et al.
- **Venue:** IEEE TNNLS 2018
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.00311)
- **Summary:** Extended version of PNN with inner/outer product layers and kernel product generalizations; ablation study clarifies when product-based feature interaction outperforms concatenation-based MLPs across diverse CTR benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#pnn` `#feature-interaction` `#ctr`

## Attention for Interaction Modeling

### Feature Generation by Convolutional Neural Network for Click-Through Rate Prediction
- **Authors:** Liu et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2019
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.04447)
- **Summary:** FGCNN uses CNN over raw feature fields to generate new features that augment FM-based models; demonstrates that convolutional feature generation (not just interaction) adds complementary information to inner-product based CTR models.
- **Tags:** `#cnn` `#feature-generation` `#ctr`

### Field-aware Factorization Machines for CTR Prediction
- **Authors:** Juan et al.
- **Venue:** RecSys 2016 (standardized in 2018 deep-rec baselines)
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2959100.2959134)
- **Summary:** Extends FM by assigning a separate latent vector to each field-pair interaction, yielding field-aware FM (FFM); a stronger but more parameter-intensive baseline that remained competitive against early deep models.
- **Tags:** `#ffm` `#field-aware` `#ctr`
