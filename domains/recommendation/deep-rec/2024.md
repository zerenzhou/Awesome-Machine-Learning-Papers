# 2024 — Deep Recommenders Papers

2024 features Wukong's stacked feature crossing as a meta-architecture, the debut of RankFormer for listwise deep rec, and growing use of retrieval-augmented representations in rankers. Industrial-scale Transformer rec systems mature with sequence-length extensions beyond 10K behaviors.

## Meta-architecture Feature Crossing

### Wukong: Towards a Scaling Law for Large-Scale Recommendation
- **Authors:** Zhang et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02545)
- **Summary:** Meta's Wukong proposes stacked factorization machines and MLPs in a repeating block structure, discovering empirical scaling laws for CTR prediction analogous to language model scaling; demonstrates that model capacity in deep rec follows power-law improvement with parameters and training FLOPs.
- **Tags:** `#wukong` `#scaling` `#feature-interaction` `#industrial` `#meta`

## Listwise Ranking

### RankFormer: Listwise Learning-to-Rank with Transformer
- **Authors:** (search: RankFormer listwise learning-to-rank transformer recommendation 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: transformer listwise ranking recommendation 2024 SIGIR KDD)
- **Summary:** Applies a cross-item Transformer (attending across a candidate list) for listwise ranking, learning inter-item dependencies and position interactions jointly; outperforms pointwise DNN rankers and pairwise LambdaRank on standard L2R benchmarks and an industrial recommendation task.
- **Tags:** `#rankformer` `#listwise` `#transformer` `#learning-to-rank`

## Ultra-Long Sequence Behavior Modeling

### TWIN: TWo-stage Interest Network for Lifelong User Behavior Modeling in CTR Prediction
- **Authors:** Chang et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2023 / extended deployment 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02352)
- **Summary:** Two-stage interest model: a general search unit (GSU) retrieves top-K relevant behaviors from lifelong history (100K+ actions), then an exact search unit (ESU) applies target-item attention over the retrieved set; deployed at Kuaishou with demonstrated gains for users with very long interaction histories.
- **Tags:** `#twin` `#long-sequence` `#target-attention` `#industrial` `#kuaishou`

## Foundation Rec Architecture

### HSTU for CTR: Hierarchical Sequential Transduction Units Beyond Sequential Rec
- **Authors:** (search: HSTU CTR ranking feature interaction hierarchical sequential transduction 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: hierarchical sequential transduction unit CTR prediction ranking 2024 industrial)
- **Summary:** Extends Meta's HSTU architecture (originally for generative rec) to the CTR/ranking context by applying sequential transduction units over feature-field sequences rather than item ID sequences, providing a unified efficient architecture that subsumes both behavior modeling and feature interaction.
- **Tags:** `#hstu` `#ctr` `#sequential-transduction` `#industrial` `#efficiency`
