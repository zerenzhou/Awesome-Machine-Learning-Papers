## RLHF-based Debiasing

### Aligning Recommender Systems with Human Feedback via Reward Modeling
- **Authors:** (search: RLHF recommendation debiasing human feedback reward model 2025)
- **Paper:** (search: reinforcement learning human feedback recommendation alignment debiasing 2025)
- **Summary:** Trains a reward model from human preference comparisons between recommendation lists (preferred vs. non-preferred), then uses this reward as a debiasing signal to fine-tune the base recommender; the human-preference reward naturally penalizes clickbait and popularity bias without requiring explicit causal graph specification.
