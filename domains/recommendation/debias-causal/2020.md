# 2020 — Debiasing / Fairness / Causal Recommendation Papers

2020 sees the emergence of fairness-aware recommendation (FairRec, FairGo), popularity debiasing via causal graphs, and improved doubly robust estimators with enhanced variance reduction. The community begins systematically distinguishing conformity bias from popularity bias.

## Fairness-aware Recommendation

### FairRec: Two-Sided Fairness for Personalized Recommendations in Two-Sided Platforms
- **Authors:** Patro et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10764)
- **Summary:** Proposes fairness constraints that ensure both user-side (envy-free recommendations) and provider-side (minimum exposure guarantees) fairness in two-sided recommendation platforms via an EF (envy-free) allocation framework.
- **Tags:** `#fairrec` `#fairness` `#two-sided` `#exposure`

### FairGo: Graph Neural Network-based Fair Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wu et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2021 (developed 2020)
- **Paper:** (search: FairGo graph neural network fair recommendation sensitive attribute SIGIR 2021)
- **Summary:** Applies adversarial filtering on GNN-based recommendation to remove sensitive attribute information from user representations, achieving individual fairness across demographic groups without sacrificing overall recommendation accuracy.
- **Tags:** `#fairgo` `#gnn` `#adversarial` `#fairness`

## Popularity Debiasing

### Causal Intervention for Leveraging Popularity Bias in Recommendation (PDA)
- **Authors:** Zhang et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06067)
- **Summary:** Uses causal graph interventions (do-calculus) to disentangle genuine user-item affinity from popularity-induced co-occurrence bias; introduces Popularity-bias Deconfounding and Adjusting (PDA) via backdoor adjustment to remove popularity confounders.
- **Tags:** `#pda` `#causal-graph` `#popularity-bias` `#do-calculus`

### Model-Agnostic Counterfactual Reasoning for Eliminating Popularity Bias in Recommender System (MACR)
- **Authors:** Wei et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2021
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.15363)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/weitianxin/MACR)
- **Summary:** Applies counterfactual reasoning to decompose the direct causal effect of popularity from the user-item matching score; MACR subtracts the popularity direct effect at inference time without retraining, making it model-agnostic.
- **Tags:** `#macr` `#counterfactual` `#popularity-bias` `#model-agnostic`

## Variance Reduction in DR

### Enhanced Doubly Robust Learning for Debiasing Post-Click Conversion Rate Estimation
- **Authors:** Li et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2022
- **Paper:** (search: enhanced doubly robust debiasing post-click conversion rate estimation Li SIGIR 2022)
- **Summary:** Extends the doubly robust estimator with importance weight clipping and self-normalized weighting to further reduce variance in the DR-JL framework, with applications to CVR debiasing where selection bias is severe.
- **Tags:** `#dr` `#variance-reduction` `#cvr` `#debiasing`
