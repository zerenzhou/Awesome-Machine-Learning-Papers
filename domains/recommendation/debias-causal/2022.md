# 2022 — Debiasing / Fairness / Causal Recommendation Papers

2022 extends causal recommendation to selection bias correction at scale (DecRS), improves doubly robust CVR debiasing (DR-JL), and applies variational information bottleneck to remove spurious features. Counterfactual inference is now entering production ranking systems.

## Structural Causal Models

### DecRS: Model-Agnostic Debiased Representations for Cross-Population Recommendation
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2022
- **Paper:** (search: DecRS model-agnostic debiased representation cross-population recommendation Wang KDD 2022)
- **Summary:** Uses structural causal models to identify and remove spurious correlations between user demographics and item preferences, learning demographic-agnostic representations via adversarial disentanglement for fair cross-population recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#decrs` `#structural-causal` `#demographic` `#fairness`

## Causal Graph for Feature Debiasing

### DCCL: Disentangled Contrastive Collaborative Filtering
- **Authors:** (search: DCCL disentangled contrastive collaborative filtering 2022 2023)
- **Paper:** (search: disentangled contrastive collaborative filtering causal 2022)
- **Summary:** Applies contrastive learning with causal feature disentanglement to separate intent-driven from bias-driven collaborative signals, using data augmentation guided by structural causal graphs to create counterfactual training pairs.
- **Tags:** `#dccl` `#contrastive` `#disentanglement` `#causal`

## Information Bottleneck Debiasing

### CVIB: Counterfactual Variational Information Bottleneck for Debiasing Recommendations
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** (search: CVIB counterfactual variational information bottleneck debiasing recommendation 2022)
- **Paper:** (search: variational information bottleneck recommendation debiasing 2022)
- **Summary:** Combines variational information bottleneck with counterfactual reasoning to compress user-item representations into a minimal sufficient representation that preserves preference signal while removing bias-confounded features.
- **Tags:** `#cvib` `#vib` `#counterfactual` `#information-bottleneck`

## Doubly Robust Extension

### DR-JL: Doubly Robust Joint Learning for Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: DR-JL doubly robust joint learning recommendation debiasing 2022)
- **Paper:** (search: doubly robust joint learning recommendation missing not at random 2022)
- **Summary:** Extends the DR estimator with joint learning of the propensity and imputation models to reduce correlation-induced bias; introduces error-imputation-based debiasing to further reduce variance in high-dimensional sparse recommendation settings.
- **Tags:** `#dr-jl` `#doubly-robust` `#joint-learning` `#mnar`

## Causal Rec Survey

### A Survey on Causal Recommendation
- **Authors:** Gao et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2022
- **Paper:** (search: survey causal recommendation Gao 2022 arXiv)
