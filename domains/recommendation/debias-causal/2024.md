# 2024 — Debiasing / Fairness / Causal Recommendation Papers

2024 brings debiasing into the LLM-augmented recommendation era: CausalLM4Rec applies LLM reasoning to identify confounders, and DIR-MRS introduces domain-invariant debiasing for multi-scenario systems. Fairness evaluation frameworks mature with provider-side metrics gaining traction.

## Domain-invariant Debiasing

### DIR-MRS: Domain-Invariant Representation for Multi-scenario Recommendation via Debiasing
- **Authors:** (search: DIR-MRS domain-invariant representation multi-scenario recommendation debiasing 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: domain-invariant debiasing multi-scenario recommendation 2024 KDD)
- **Summary:** Identifies domain-specific spurious correlations in multi-scenario recommenders (e.g., popularity varies across app surfaces) and learns invariant user-interest representations using cross-domain contrastive alignment, improving recommendation quality on domains with limited training data.

## Exposure Bias Survey

### Beyond Clicks: A Systematic Review of Evaluation Protocols for Debiased Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: evaluation protocol debiased recommendation survey beyond clicks 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: debiased recommendation evaluation survey 2024 ACM Computing Surveys TOIS)
- **Summary:** Reviews evaluation methodologies for debiased rec, arguing that click-based metrics systematically reward popularity-biased models; proposes a unified evaluation framework using propensity-stratified NDCG and intervention-based holdout sets, with a benchmark comparison of 15 debiasing methods under the new protocol.
