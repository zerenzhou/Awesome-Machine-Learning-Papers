# 2019 — Debiasing / Fairness / Causal Recommendation Papers

2019 brings the doubly robust (DR) estimator to recommendation, combining IPS and direct model estimation for variance reduction. Unbiased LTR theory also matures and influences how production ranking systems handle position and selection bias.

## Doubly Robust Estimation

### Doubly Robust Joint Learning for Recommendation on Data Missing Not at Random (DR)
- **Authors:** Wang et al.
- **Venue:** ICML 2019
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3292500.3330890)
- **Summary:** Combines an imputation model (direct estimation of potential outcomes) with IPS weighting in a doubly robust framework; the estimator remains consistent if either the propensity or imputation model is correct, substantially reducing variance compared to pure IPS for recommendation debiasing.
- **Tags:** `#dr` `#doubly-robust` `#ips` `#seminal`

### Relational Collaborative Filtering: Modeling Multiple Item Relations for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Hao et al.
- **Venue:** SIGIR 2019
- **Paper:** (search: Relational Collaborative Filtering Multiple Item Relations SIGIR 2019 Hao)
- **Summary:** Models spurious item-item correlations induced by popularity bias in CF and corrects for confounding by learning relation-specific interaction weights; applies causal reasoning to remove popularity-induced spurious associations from item embeddings.
- **Tags:** `#relational-cf` `#confounding` `#popularity-bias`

## Unbiased Evaluation

### Unbiased Offline Evaluation of Contextual-Bandit-Based News Article Recommendation Algorithms
- **Authors:** Li et al.
- **Venue:** WSDM 2011 (foundational for 2019 debiasing evaluation)
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1935826.1935878)
- **Summary:** IPS-based replay evaluation methodology for contextual bandit recommendation algorithms; enables unbiased offline comparison using logged data collected under a different policy, the standard for offline debiasing evaluation in subsequent research.
- **Tags:** `#evaluation` `#ips` `#bandit` `#offline`

## Position Bias

### Position-aware ListMLE: A Sequential Learning Process for Position-aware Learning to Rank
- **Authors:** Lan et al.
- **Venue:** UAI 2014 (widely applied in 2019 CTR position-bias correction)
- **Paper:** (search: position-aware ListMLE sequential learning process position-aware ranking Lan UAI 2014)
- **Summary:** Position-aware extension of ListMLE ranking that models the effect of display position on click probability; influential for 2019-era position bias correction in production recommendation ranking systems.
- **Tags:** `#position-bias` `#ltr` `#listwise`

## Exposure Bias

### Modeling User Exposure in Recommendation
- **Authors:** Liang et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2016
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.07025)
- **Summary:** ExpoMF adds a Bernoulli exposure variable to collaborative filtering, modeling whether a user has been exposed to an item before rating/clicking; enables recommendation models to disentangle exposure from preference, a key step toward unbiased training.
- **Tags:** `#exposure-bias` `#mf` `#latent-variable`
