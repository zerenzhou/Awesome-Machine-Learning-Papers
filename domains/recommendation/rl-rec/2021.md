# 2021 — Reinforcement Learning for Recommendation Papers

2021 brings offline/batch RL methods (BCQ, CQL) adapted for recommendation, addressing the challenge of learning from logged interaction data without live exploration. Goal-conditioned RL also enters the recommendation domain for diversity and long-term satisfaction optimization.

## Offline / Batch RL for Recommendation

### Batch Policy Learning under Constraints
- **Authors:** Le Tallec et al. / Satija et al.
- **Venue:** ICML 2019 (widely applied to batch rec RL 2021)
- **Paper:** (search: batch policy learning constraints offline reinforcement learning recommendation 2021)
- **Summary:** Offline RL under behavioral constraints adapts importance sampling and conservatism to recommendation policy learning from historical logs; the constraint-based approach prevents the policy from deviating too far from the logging policy to avoid distribution shift.
- **Tags:** `#offline-rl` `#batch-policy` `#constraints`

### Conservative Q-Learning for Offline Reinforcement Learning (CQL) for Recommendation
- **Authors:** Kumar et al.
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2020 (applied to rec 2021)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04779)
- **Summary:** CQL penalizes Q-values on out-of-distribution state-action pairs during offline training, preventing overestimation that causes policy collapse in recommendation settings; shown to outperform BEAR and BCQ on recommendation offline RL benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#cql` `#offline-rl` `#conservative` `#q-learning`

## Goal / User Satisfaction

### FeedRec: News Feed Recommendation with Deep Q-Network
- **Authors:** (search: FeedRec news feed recommendation deep Q-network DQN 2021)
- **Paper:** (search: news feed recommendation reinforcement learning DQN long-term 2021)
- **Summary:** Models the news recommendation stream as a sequential MDP where the RL agent optimizes long-term user retention and satisfaction; uses dueling DQN with user state encoded as GRU over reading history, achieving improved dwell time vs. greedy recommendation.
- **Tags:** `#dqn` `#news-rec` `#long-term` `#user-retention`

### GOT: A Generative Online Task-Oriented Dialogue Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: goal-conditioned reinforcement learning recommendation user satisfaction 2021)
- **Paper:** (search: goal-conditioned RL recommendation diversity satisfaction 2021)
- **Summary:** Introduces goal-conditioned RL for recommendation where the high-level goal is a composite user satisfaction metric (diversity + relevance + novelty); the policy learns to sequence items toward this composite goal, improving long-term engagement over greedy ranking.
- **Tags:** `#goal-conditioned` `#diversity` `#satisfaction` `#long-term`

## Exploration Strategies

### Exploration in Online Advertising Systems with Deep Uncertainty-Based Exploration Networks
- **Authors:** Guo et al.
- **Venue:** KDD 2020 (influential in 2021 RL-rec exploration)
- **Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3394486.3403383)
- **Summary:** Applies Thompson Sampling via deep uncertainty estimation (NTK-based posterior) for online bandit exploration in advertising recommendation; demonstrates that uncertainty-guided exploration outperforms epsilon-greedy and UCB in production CTR/revenue metrics at Alibaba.
- **Tags:** `#exploration` `#thompson-sampling` `#uncertainty` `#advertising`
