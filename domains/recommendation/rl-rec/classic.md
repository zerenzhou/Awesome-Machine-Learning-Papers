# Classic — Reinforcement Learning for Recommendation

Foundational bandit works (2010–2013) that established contextual bandits as the primary RL tool for online recommendation before full policy-gradient methods emerged.

#### Bandit Foundations

### A Contextual-Bandit Approach to Personalized News Article Recommendation
- **Authors:** Li et al.
- **Venue:** WWW 2010
- **Paper:** [ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.0146)
- **Summary:** LinUCB applies upper confidence bound exploration to contextual bandits for personalized news recommendation, providing a principled exploration-exploitation tradeoff and becoming the standard baseline for bandit-based recommenders.
- **Tags:** `#linucb` `#contextual-bandit` `#seminal`

### A Contextual-Bandit Algorithm for Mobile Context-Aware Recommender System
- **Authors:** Bouneffouf et al.
- **Venue:** ICONIP 2012
- **Paper:** (search: A Contextual-Bandit Algorithm for Mobile Context-Aware Recommender System Bouneffouf 2012)
- **Summary:** Applies contextual bandit learning with contextual exploration to mobile-specific recommendation scenarios, adapting UCB-style exploration to location- and time-aware context features.
- **Tags:** `#bandit` `#context-aware`

### Thompson Sampling for Contextual Bandits with Linear Payoffs
- **Authors:** Agrawal & Goyal
- **Venue:** ICML 2013
- **Paper:** [ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.3352)
- **Summary:** Provides theoretical guarantees for Thompson Sampling in linear contextual bandit settings, offering a Bayesian alternative to UCB-based methods with strong empirical performance across recommendation-style exploration tasks.
- **Tags:** `#thompson-sampling` `#bandit`
