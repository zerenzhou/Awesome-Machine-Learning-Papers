# 2020 — Reinforcement Learning for Recommendation Papers

2020 establishes offline RL and user-simulator frameworks as the foundation for practical RL-based rec: CQL and BCQ are applied to recommendation logs, and the KuaiSim line of simulation environments begins forming. On-policy methods also mature with environment-simulator co-training.

## Off-policy / Offline RL

### Conservative Q-Learning for Offline Reinforcement Learning (CQL)
- **Authors:** Kumar et al.
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04779)
- **Summary:** Addresses overestimation of unseen state-action pairs in offline Q-learning by penalizing Q-values on out-of-distribution (OOD) actions; forms the foundation for offline RL applied to recommendation policy learning, where logged interaction data has severe coverage gaps.
- **Tags:** `#cql` `#offline-rl` `#conservative` `#q-learning`

### MOPO: Model-based Offline Policy Optimization
- **Authors:** Yu et al.
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2020
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.13239)
- **Summary:** Learns a dynamics model from offline data and adds an uncertainty-penalized reward signal to discourage policy exploration in high-uncertainty state-action regions; applied to recommendation as a model-based alternative to CQL for offline policy optimization without environment interaction.
- **Tags:** `#mopo` `#model-based` `#offline-rl` `#uncertainty`

## User Simulator for RL Training

### Simulating User Feedback for Reinforcement Learning Based Recommendations
- **Authors:** (search: user simulation reinforcement learning recommendation feedback 2020)
- **Paper:** (search: user simulator reinforcement learning recommendation 2020 RecSys SIGIR)
- **Summary:** Proposes a parametric user simulator trained on historical interaction data to provide dense reward signals for online RL training without real user exposure; introduces simulation fidelity metrics and demonstrates that sim-trained policies transfer to real users with minimal degradation.
- **Tags:** `#user-simulator` `#simulation` `#rl` `#reward`
