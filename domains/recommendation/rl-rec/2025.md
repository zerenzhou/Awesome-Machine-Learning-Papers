# 2025 — Reinforcement Learning for Recommendation Papers

2025 RL-rec consolidates around RLHF-aligned recommenders, multi-agent RL for marketplace recommendation, and PPO-based policy learning with diffusion priors. The field increasingly treats recommendation alignment as an RL problem rather than a supervised objective.

## PPO for LLM Rec Alignment

### RecLM-RLHF: Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback for Recommendation Language Models
- **Authors:** (search: RecLM RLHF reinforcement learning human feedback recommendation language model 2025)
- **Paper:** (search: RLHF recommendation LLM alignment PPO 2025 KDD RecSys SIGIR)
- **Summary:** Applies full PPO-based RLHF to a generative recommendation LLM, training a reward model from human preference labels on recommendation lists and optimizing the rec LLM policy via proximal policy optimization; shows that RLHF alignment significantly reduces harmful recommendations (clickbait, low-quality items) while improving long-term user satisfaction.
- **Tags:** `#reclm` `#rlhf` `#ppo` `#alignment` `#llm-rec`

## Diffusion Policy for Offline RL

### DiffPO: Diffusion-based Policy Optimization for Offline Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: diffusion policy optimization offline reinforcement learning recommendation 2025)
- **Paper:** (search: diffusion offline RL policy recommendation 2025 SIGIR NeurIPS)
- **Summary:** Uses a diffusion model as a behavior-regularization prior in offline RL: the policy is trained to maximize Q-values while staying close to the behavior distribution modeled by the diffusion process; empirically more stable than CQL's hard pessimism and outperforms IQL on KuaiRec and MovieLens offline RL benchmarks.
- **Tags:** `#diffusion` `#offline-rl` `#policy-optimization` `#behavior-cloning`

## Multi-agent Marketplace RL

### Multi-Agent RL for Marketplace Recommendation: Balancing User, Item, and Platform Objectives
- **Authors:** (search: multi-agent reinforcement learning marketplace recommendation 2025)
- **Paper:** (search: multi-agent RL marketplace recommendation multi-stakeholder 2025)
- **Summary:** Models marketplace recommendation as a cooperative multi-agent RL problem with separate agents for user-side, seller-side, and platform-side reward maximization, using counterfactual communication to coordinate agent policies without sharing private state; achieves Pareto improvements across all three stakeholder metrics in simulation and A/B tests.
- **Tags:** `#multi-agent` `#marketplace` `#multi-stakeholder` `#rl`
