# 2024 — Generative Recommendation Papers

2024 scales generative recommendation to industry: Meta's HSTU and OneRec demonstrate billion-scale deployment; COBRA introduces codebook-based ID compression; and scaling law studies reveal predictable performance improvement with model and data scaling in recommendation.

## Industrial-scale Generative Rec

### OneRec: Unifying Retrieve and Rank with Generative Recommender and Iterative Preference Alignment
- **Authors:** Dang et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2024
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18232)
- **Summary:** Kuaishou's production generative recommender that unifies candidate retrieval and ranking into a single generative model via iterative preference alignment (DPO-style), eliminating the retrieve-then-rank pipeline and achieving online metric gains at billion-user scale.
- **Tags:** `#onerec` `#industrial` `#kuaishou` `#preference-alignment`

### Scaling Laws for Recommendation (Meta)
- **Authors:** Zhai et al.
- **Venue:** ICML 2024 (companion to HSTU paper)
- **Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17152)
- **Summary:** Demonstrates that HSTU-based generative recommenders exhibit power-law scaling with both model size and training data, consistent with LLM scaling laws; validates the foundation model paradigm for recommendation at trillion-interaction scale.
- **Tags:** `#scaling-laws` `#foundation-model` `#meta` `#industrial`

## Semantic Code Compression

### COBRA: Towards Fast and Accurate Recommendation via Codebook-based Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: COBRA codebook-based recommendation generative fast accurate 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: COBRA codebook recommendation generative item ID 2024)
- **Summary:** Introduces a codebook-based item representation that compresses RQ-VAE codes for faster beam-search decoding in generative recommendation; achieves order-of-magnitude decoding speedup over TIGER while maintaining competitive accuracy.
- **Tags:** `#cobra` `#codebook` `#efficiency` `#decoding`

## Preference Alignment for Rec

### Direct Preference Optimization for Recommendation
- **Authors:** (search: DPO direct preference optimization recommendation alignment 2024)
- **Paper:** (search: direct preference optimization generative recommendation RLHF 2024)
- **Summary:** Applies DPO (Direct Preference Optimization) to align generative recommenders with user preferences derived from implicit feedback, enabling preference-aware generation without reward model training and improving beyond standard cross-entropy fine-tuning.
