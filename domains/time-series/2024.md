# Time Series Forecasting — 2024

- **iTransformer: Inverted Transformers Are Effective for Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2024 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06625)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/iTransformer)]
  Inverted the Transformer to apply attention on the variate dimension instead of the temporal dimension.

- **TimesFM: A Decoder-Only Foundation Model for Time-Series Forecasting** — ICML 2024 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10688)] [[Code](https://github.com/google-research/timesfm)]
  Pretrained a decoder-only patched-Transformer foundation model on 100B real-world time points for zero-shot forecasting across diverse domains.

- **Timer: Generative Pre-trained Transformers Are Large Time Series Models** — ICML 2024 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02368)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/Large-Time-Series-Model)]
  Pre-trained a GPT-style model on heterogeneous time series with unified single-series sequence format for general forecasting.

- **Time-LLM: Time Series Forecasting by Reprogramming Large Language Models** — ICLR 2024 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01728)] [[Code](https://github.com/KimMeen/Time-LLM)]
  Reprogrammed frozen LLMs for time series forecasting via input reprogramming and prompt-as-prefix, without modifying LLM weights.

- **Unified Training of Universal Time Series Forecasting Transformers (MOIRAI)** — ICML 2024 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02592)] [[Code](https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/uni2ts)]
  Proposed a universal forecasting Transformer trained on the Large-scale Open Time Series Archive with any-variate attention.

- **FITS: Modeling Time Series with 10k Parameters** — ICLR 2024 [`Frequency`] [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03756)] [[Code](https://github.com/VEWOXIC/FITS)]
  Achieved competitive forecasting with only 10k parameters by operating entirely in the frequency domain via complex-valued interpolation.

- **ModernTCN: A Modern Pure Convolution Structure for General Time Series Analysis** — ICLR 2024 [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03820)] [[Code](https://github.com/luodhhh/ModernTCN)]
  Modernized TCN with large kernels, DWConv, and ConvFFN blocks, demonstrating pure convolution can match Transformer performance.

- **TimeMachine: A Time Series is Worth 4 Mambas for Long-term Forecasting** — arXiv 2024 [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09898)] [[Code](https://github.com/Atik-Ahamed/TimeMachine)]
  Applied multi-scale Mamba blocks with integrated channel mixing for efficient long-term time series forecasting.

- **S-Mamba: Is Mamba Effective for Time Series Forecasting?** — arXiv 2024 [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11144)] [[Code](https://github.com/wzhwzhwzh0921/S-D-Mamba)]
  Explored Mamba's effectiveness for time series forecasting by treating variates as tokens with bidirectional Mamba blocks.

- **SAN: Towards Non-stationary Time Series Forecasting with Adaptive Normalization** — ICLR 2024 [`Normalization`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BCN2qUOFVv)] [[Code](https://github.com/icantnamemyself/SAN)]
  Proposed slice-and-normalize with adaptive normalization statistics for handling complex non-stationarity patterns.

- **Lag-Llama: Towards Foundation Models for Probabilistic Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv 2024 [`Foundation Model`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08278)] [[Code](https://github.com/time-series-foundation-models/lag-llama)]
  Pretrained a decoder-only Transformer on diverse time series with lag features for zero-shot probabilistic forecasting.

- **CARD: Channel Aligned Robust Blend Transformer for Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2024 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12095)] [[Code](https://github.com/wxie9/CARD)]
  Designed channel-aligned attention with token blend to effectively capture cross-channel dependencies.

- **SparseTSF: Modeling Long-term Time Series Forecasting with 1k Parameters** — ICML 2024 Oral [`MLP`] [`Frequency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00946)] [[Code](https://github.com/lss-1138/SparseTSF)]
  Achieved ultra-lightweight forecasting with cross-period sparse forecasting exploiting periodicity in time series.

- **Chronos: Learning the Language of Time Series** — ICML 2024 [`Foundation Model`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07815)] [[Code](https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting)]
  Tokenized time series values into discrete bins and trained language model architectures for probabilistic forecasting.

- **TimeMixer: Decomposable Multiscale Mixing for Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2024 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=7oLshfEIC2)] [[Code](https://github.com/kwuking/TimeMixer)]
  Proposed Past-Decomposable-Mixing and Future-Multipredictor-Mixing for multiscale temporal pattern extraction.

- **Pathformer: Multi-scale Transformers with Adaptive Pathways for Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2024 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05956)] [[Code](https://github.com/decisionintelligence/pathformer)]
  Designed adaptive multi-scale pathways that dynamically select temporal resolutions for different time series patterns.

- **MOMENT: A Family of Open Time-Series Foundation Models** — ICML 2024 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03885)] [[Code](https://github.com/moment-timeseries-foundation-model/moment)]
  Built an open-source time series foundation model with a patch-based masked pre-training pipeline on diverse public datasets.

- **TimeXer: Empowering Transformers for Time Series Forecasting with Exogenous Variables** — NeurIPS 2024 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19072)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/TimeXer)]
  Proposed endogenous-exogenous cross-attention to effectively incorporate external covariates into Transformer forecasting.

- **AutoTimes: Autoregressive Time Series Forecasters via Large Language Models** — NeurIPS 2024 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02370)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/AutoTimes)]
  Repurposed LLMs as autoregressive time series forecasters via in-context token-wise prompting without modifying model weights.

- **PDF: Periodicity Decoupling Framework for Long-term Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2024 [`Frequency`] [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=dp27P5HBBt)] [[Code](https://github.com/Hank0626/PDF)]
  Decoupled periodic and non-periodic components via 2D periodic representations for long-term forecasting.

- **MambaTS: Improved Selective State Space Models for Long-term Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv 2024 [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16440)] [[Code](https://github.com/XiudingCai/MambaTS-pytorch)]
  Enhanced Mamba with variable-aware scanning and temporal information fusion for multivariate long-term forecasting.

- **CycleNet: Enhancing Time Series Forecasting through Modeling Periodic Patterns** — NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight [`MLP`] [`Frequency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18479)] [[Code](https://github.com/ACAT-SCUT/CycleNet)]
  Introduced Residual Cycle Forecasting with learnable recurrent cycles to model periodic patterns, reducing parameters by over 90% while achieving SOTA accuracy.

- **FilterNet: Harnessing Frequency Filters for Time Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2024 [`Frequency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01623)] [[Code](https://github.com/aikunyi/FilterNet)]
  Built upon learnable plain and contextual frequency filters to selectively pass or attenuate time series signal components, surrogating linear and attention mappings.

- **SOFTS: Efficient Multivariate Time Series Forecasting with Series-Core Fusion** — NeurIPS 2024 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14197)] [[Code](https://github.com/Secilia-Cxy/SOFTS)]
  Proposed an MLP-based model with STar Aggregate-Redistribute (STAR) module that reduces cross-variate modeling from quadratic to linear complexity.

- **UniTS: A Unified Multi-Task Time Series Model** — NeurIPS 2024 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00131)] [[Code](https://github.com/mims-harvard/UniTS)]
  Unified forecasting, classification, imputation, and anomaly detection into a single model via task tokenization, with zero-shot and few-shot transfer across 38 multi-domain datasets.
