# Time Series Forecasting — 2020

- **Spectral Temporal Graph Neural Network for Multivariate Time-series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2020 [`GNN`] [`Frequency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.07719)] [[Code](https://github.com/microsoft/StemGNN)]
  Proposed StemGNN combining graph Fourier transform and discrete Fourier transform to capture inter-series and temporal patterns jointly.

- **Connecting the Dots: Multivariate Time Series Forecasting with Graph Neural Networks** — KDD 2020 [`GNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11650)] [[Code](https://github.com/nnzhan/MTGNN)]
  Proposed MTGNN with adaptive graph learning and mix-hop propagation for multivariate time series without predefined graph structure.

- **Adversarial Sparse Transformer for Time Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2020 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/c6b8c8d762f707772aca9122be4e3cee-Abstract.html)]
  Introduced adversarial training with sparse Transformer to improve generalization in time series forecasting.

- **Deep Transformer Models for Time Series Forecasting: The Influenza Prevalence Case** — arXiv 2020 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08317)]
  Applied deep Transformer architecture to influenza-like illness forecasting, demonstrating Transformer effectiveness on public health time series.
