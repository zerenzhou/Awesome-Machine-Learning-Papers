# Time Series Forecasting — 2022

- **FEDformer: Frequency Enhanced Decomposed Transformer for Long-term Series Forecasting** — ICML 2022 [`Transformer`] [`Frequency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12740)] [[Code](https://github.com/MAZiqing/FEDformer)]
  Applied frequency-domain attention with seasonal-trend decomposition for long-term time series forecasting.

- **SCINet: Time Series Modeling and Forecasting with Sample Convolution and Interaction** — NeurIPS 2022 [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09305)] [[Code](https://github.com/cure-lab/SCINet)]
  Introduced a recursive downsampling-convolution-interaction architecture for temporal modeling.

- **Non-stationary Transformers: Exploring the Stationarity in Time Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2022 [`Transformer`] [`Normalization`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14415)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/Nonstationary_Transformers)]
  Proposed Series Stationarization and De-stationary Attention to address over-stationarization in non-stationary time series.

- **FiLM: Frequency improved Legendre Memory Model for Long-term Time Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2022 [`Frequency`] [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08897)] [[Code](https://github.com/tianzhou2011/FiLM)]
  Combined Legendre polynomial projections with frequency-domain processing for efficient long-term forecasting.

- **ETSformer: Exponential Smoothing Transformers for Time-series Forecasting** — arXiv 2022 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01381)] [[Code](https://github.com/salesforce/ETSformer)]
  Replaced vanilla attention with exponential smoothing and frequency attention inspired by classical ETS methods.

- **Triformer: Triangular, Variable-Specific Attentions for Long Sequence Multivariate Time Series Forecasting** — IJCAI 2022 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13767)]
  Proposed variable-specific patch attention with triangular structure for efficient multivariate long-sequence forecasting.

- **Reversible Instance Normalization for Accurate Time-Series Forecasting against Distribution Shift** — ICLR 2022 [`Normalization`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=cGDAkQo1C0p)] [[Code](https://github.com/ts-kim/RevIN)]
  Proposed RevIN as a plug-in normalization to handle distribution shift in time series, widely adopted across subsequent models.

- **Pyraformer: Low-Complexity Pyramidal Attention for Long-Range Time Series Modeling and Forecasting** — ICLR 2022 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=0EXmFzUn5I)] [[Code](https://github.com/ant-research/Pyraformer)]
  Introduced pyramidal attention with inter-scale and intra-scale connections for linear complexity long-range temporal modeling.

- **S4: Efficiently Modeling Long Sequences with Structured State Spaces** — ICLR 2022 [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00396)] [[Code](https://github.com/state-spaces/s4)]
  Introduced the Structured State Space model with HiPPO initialization, enabling efficient long-range sequence modeling foundational to later SSM/Mamba works.

- **TS2Vec: Towards Universal Representation of Time Series** — AAAI 2022 [`Representation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.10466)] [[Code](https://github.com/yuezhihan/ts2vec)]
  Proposed hierarchical contrastive learning over augmented time series for universal representations at both instance and temporal levels.

- **CoST: Contrastive Learning of Disentangled Seasonal-Trend Representations for Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2022 [`Representation`] [`Frequency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01575)] [[Code](https://github.com/salesforce/CoST)]
  Disentangled seasonal and trend representations via contrastive learning in both time and frequency domains.

- **Depts: Deep Expansion Learning for Periodic Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2022 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=AJAR-JgNw__)] [[Code](https://github.com/weifantt/DEPTS)]
  Explicitly modeled periodicity via periodic expansion and residual forecasting for time series with strong periodic patterns.

- **LOCA: Learning Operators with Coupled Attention** — JMLR 2022 [`Neural Operator`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01032)] [[Code](https://github.com/PredictiveIntelligenceLab/LOCA)]
  Coupled attention weights with an integral transform to approximate nonlinear operators even with few training output functions; first operator-learning method explicitly evaluated on black-box climate forecasting, establishing the operator-theoretic route for spatio-temporal prediction.
