# Time Series Forecasting — 2021

- **Informer: Beyond Efficient Transformer for Long Sequence Time-Series Forecasting** — AAAI 2021 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07436)] [[Code](https://github.com/zhouhaoyi/Informer2020)]
  Introduced ProbSparse self-attention and distilling operation for efficient long-sequence time-series forecasting.

- **Temporal Fusion Transformers for Interpretable Multi-horizon Time Series Forecasting** — International Journal of Forecasting 2021 [`Transformer`] [`RNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09363)] [[Code](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/tft)]
  Proposed TFT combining LSTM encoding with multi-head attention and variable selection for interpretable multi-horizon forecasting.

- **Autoregressive Denoising Diffusion Models for Multivariate Probabilistic Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2021 [`Diffusion`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.12072)] [[Code](https://github.com/zalandoresearch/pytorch-ts)]
  Proposed TimeGrad applying denoising diffusion probabilistic models to time series for high-quality probabilistic forecasts.

- **Discrete Graph Structure Learning for Forecasting Multiple Time Series** — ICLR 2021 [`GNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06861)] [[Code](https://github.com/chaoshangcs/GTS)]
  Proposed GTS learning discrete graph structures simultaneously with a GNN forecaster for multivariate time series.

- **Autoformer: Decomposition Transformers with Auto-Correlation for Long-Term Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2021 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.13008)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/Autoformer)]
  Introduced deep decomposition architecture with auto-correlation mechanism, establishing the decomposition paradigm for long-term time series forecasting.

- **CSDI: Conditional Score-based Diffusion Models for Probabilistic Time Series Imputation** — NeurIPS 2021 [`Diffusion`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03502)] [[Code](https://github.com/ermongroup/CSDI)]
  Applied score-based diffusion with conditional training for probabilistic time series imputation and forecasting.
