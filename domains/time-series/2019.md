# Time Series Forecasting — 2019

- **Enhancing the Locality and Breaking the Memory Bottleneck of Transformer on Time Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2019 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.00235)] [[Code](https://github.com/mlpotter/Transformer_Time_Series)]
  Proposed LogTrans with convolutional self-attention and LogSparse design, one of the earliest Transformers applied to TSF.

- **Shape and Time Distortion Loss for Training Deep Time Series Forecasting Models** — NeurIPS 2019 [`Loss`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09020)] [[Code](https://github.com/vincent-leguen/DILATE)]
  Introduced DILATE loss combining shape and temporal distortion penalties for sharper, more realistic forecasts.

- **Think Globally, Act Locally: A Deep Neural Network Approach to High-Dimensional Time Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2019 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03806)]
  Proposed DeepGLO combining global matrix factorization with local temporal networks for high-dimensional time series.
