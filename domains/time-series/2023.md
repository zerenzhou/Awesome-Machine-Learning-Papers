# Time Series Forecasting — 2023

- **Are Transformers Effective for Time Series Forecasting?** — AAAI 2023 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13504)] [[Code](https://github.com/cure-lab/LTSF-Linear)]
  Challenged the necessity of Transformers for TSF, showing simple linear models (DLinear/NLinear) can outperform complex architectures.

- **PatchTST: A Time Series is Worth 64 Words: Long-term Forecasting with Transformers** — ICLR 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14730)] [[Code](https://github.com/yuqinie98/PatchTST)]
  Applied patching and channel-independent design to Transformers for multivariate long-term forecasting.

- **TimesNet: Temporal 2D-Variation Modeling for General Time Series Analysis** — ICLR 2023 [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02186)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/TimesNet)]
  Transformed 1D time series into 2D tensors to capture intra- and inter-period variations via 2D convolution.

- **Crossformer: Transformer Utilizing Cross-Dimension Dependency for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=vSVLM2j9eie)] [[Code](https://github.com/Thinklab-SJTU/Crossformer)]
  Introduced Two-Stage Attention to capture both cross-time and cross-dimension dependencies in multivariate forecasting.

- **N-HiTS: Neural Hierarchical Interpolation for Time Series Forecasting** — AAAI 2023 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12886)] [[Code](https://github.com/Nixtla/neuralforecast)]
  Extended N-BEATS with hierarchical interpolation and multi-rate sampling for improved long-horizon forecasting efficiency.

- **TiDE: Long-term Forecasting with TIme-series Dense Encoder** — TMLR 2023 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08424)] [[Code](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/tide)]
  Proposed a simple MLP encoder-decoder architecture competitive with Transformers at a fraction of the compute.

- **TSMixer: An All-MLP Architecture for Time Series Forecasting** — TMLR 2023 [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06053)] [[Code](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/tsmixer)]
  Designed an all-MLP architecture with time-mixing and feature-mixing for multivariate forecasting.

- **FreTS: Frequency-domain MLPs are More Effective Learners in Time Series Forecasting** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Frequency`] [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06184)] [[Code](https://github.com/aikunyi/FreTS)]
  Applied MLP learning directly in the frequency domain to capture both channel and temporal dependencies.

- **Dish-TS: A General Paradigm for Alleviating Distribution Shift in Time Series Forecasting** — AAAI 2023 [`Normalization`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.14829)] [[Code](https://github.com/weifantt/Dish-TS)]
  Proposed learnable distribution coefficients to handle diverse distribution shift patterns beyond simple normalization.

- **One Fits All: Power General Time Series Analysis by Pretrained LM** — NeurIPS 2023 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.11939)] [[Code](https://github.com/DAMO-DI-ML/NeurIPS2023-One-Fits-All)]
  Demonstrated frozen pretrained GPT-2 with lightweight adaptation can serve as a general-purpose time series backbone.

- **Large Language Models Are Zero-Shot Time Series Forecasters** — NeurIPS 2023 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07820)] [[Code](https://github.com/ngruver/llmtime)]
  Showed that LLMs can perform time series forecasting by encoding numerical values as text tokens without any training.

- **MICN: Multi-scale Local and Global Context Modeling for Long-term Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2023 [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=zt53IDUR1U)] [[Code](https://github.com/wanghq21/MICN)]
  Designed multi-scale isometric convolution to capture local and global temporal patterns for long-term forecasting.

- **Scaleformer: Iterative Multi-scale Refining Transformers for Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04038)] [[Code](https://github.com/BorealisAI/scaleformer)]
  Proposed iterative multi-scale refinement framework that improves existing Transformer forecasters via coarse-to-fine prediction.

- **Client: Cross-variable Linear Integrated Enhanced Transformer for Multivariate Long-Term Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18838)]
  Integrated cross-variable linear components with Transformer for enhanced multivariate long-term forecasting.

- **Koopa: Learning Non-stationary Time Series Dynamics with Koopman Predictors** — NeurIPS 2023 [`MLP`] [`Theory`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18803)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/Koopa)]
  Applied Koopman theory to decompose non-stationary dynamics into time-variant and time-invariant components for linear forecasting.

- **SegRNN: Segment Recurrent Neural Network for Long-Term Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv 2023 [`RNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11200)] [[Code](https://github.com/lss-1138/SegRNN)]
  Revived RNNs for long-term forecasting by segment-wise processing with parallel decoding, achieving competitive results with far fewer parameters.

- **ClimaX: A Foundation Model for Weather and Climate** — ICML 2023 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10343)] [[Code](https://github.com/microsoft/ClimaX)]
  Pre-trained a ViT-based model on heterogeneous climate data with variable tokenization for weather and climate forecasting.

- **Leddam: A Lite Decomposition-based Dual Attention Model for Time Series Forecasting** — KDD 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01068)]
  Proposed lightweight dual attention combining seasonal and trend decomposed features for efficient long-term forecasting.

- **Adaptive Normalization for Non-stationary Time Series Forecasting: A Temporal Slice Perspective** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Normalization`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.01479)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/Adaptive-Normalization)]
  Analyzed non-stationarity from a temporal slice perspective and proposed slice-level adaptive normalization.

- **WPMixer: Wavelet Patch Mixer for Long-term Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv 2023 [`Frequency`] [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07558)]
  Combined wavelet decomposition with patch-based MLP mixing for multi-resolution time series forecasting.
