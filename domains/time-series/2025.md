# Time Series Forecasting — 2025

- **Sundial: A Family of Highly Capable Time Series Foundation Models** — ICML 2025 Oral [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00816)] [[Code](https://github.com/thuml/Sundial)]
  Proposed TimeFlow Loss based on flow-matching for native pre-training on continuous-valued time series, pre-trained on TimeBench (1 trillion time points) to achieve SOTA on point and probabilistic forecasting.

- **Time-MoE: Billion-Scale Time Series Foundation Models with Mixture of Experts** — ICLR 2025 Spotlight [`Foundation Model`] [`MoE`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16040)] [[Code](https://github.com/Time-MoE/Time-MoE)]
  Scaled time series foundation models to 2.4B parameters with sparse MoE, trained on Time-300B (300 billion time points), validating scaling laws for time series.

- **TimeMixer++: A General Time Series Pattern Machine for Universal Predictive Analysis** — ICLR 2025 Oral [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16032)] [[Code](https://github.com/kwuking/TimeMixer)]
  Extended TimeMixer with multi-resolution time imaging and dual-axis decomposition, achieving SOTA across 8 diverse time series tasks including forecasting, classification, and anomaly detection.

- **LinOSS: Linear Oscillatory State-Space Models** — ICLR 2025 Oral [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03943)]
  Proposed state-space models based on forced harmonic oscillators with stable dynamics and universal approximation, outperforming Mamba on sequences up to 50k length.

- **Moirai-MoE: Empowering Time Series Foundation Models with Sparse Mixture of Experts** — ICML 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`MoE`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10469)] [[Code](https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/uni2ts)]
  Introduced token-level MoE specialization for time series foundation models, delivering up to 17% improvement over Moirai with 65x fewer activated parameters.

- **TimeBase: The Power of Minimalism in Efficient Long-term Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2025 Spotlight [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=GhTdNOMfOD)] [[Code](https://github.com/hqh0728/TimeBase)]
  An ultra-lightweight model with fewer than 0.4k parameters that extracts core basis temporal components via orthogonality constraints, reducing MACs by 35x over standard models.

- **TimeBridge: Non-Stationarity Matters for Long-term Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2025 [`Transformer`] [`Normalization`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04442)] [[Code](https://github.com/Hank0626/TimeBridge)]
  Bridged non-stationarity and dependency modeling via Integrated Attention (mitigating short-term non-stationarity) and Cointegrated Attention (preserving long-term cross-variate relationships).

- **AdaPTS: Adapting Univariate Foundation Models to Probabilistic Multivariate Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10235)] [[Code](https://github.com/abenechehab/adapts)]
  Introduced adapters that project multivariate inputs into latent spaces for independent univariate FM processing, enabling probabilistic multivariate forecasting from univariate foundation models.

- **TS-RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation based Time Series Foundation Models are Stronger Zero-Shot Forecaster** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`RAG`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07649)] [[Code](https://github.com/UConn-DSIS/TS-RAG)]
  Enhanced zero-shot forecasting by retrieving semantically similar segments from a knowledge base and fusing them via an Adaptive Retrieval Mixer, improving TSFMs by up to 6.84%.

- **MoFo: Empowering Long-term Time Series Forecasting with Periodic Pattern Modeling** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=MoFo)]
  Designed period-structured patches via discrete sampling to directly model periodic correlations, achieving 14x memory efficiency gain and 10x faster training.

- **TimePFN: Effective Multivariate Time Series Forecasting with Synthetic Data** — AAAI 2025 [`Foundation Model`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16294)] [[Code](https://github.com/egetaga/TimePFN)]
  Trained a Prior-data Fitted Network on synthetic multivariate data from Gaussian processes and linear coregionalization, achieving SOTA in zero-shot and few-shot settings.

- **TimesBERT: A BERT-Style Foundation Model for Time Series Understanding** — ACM MM 2025 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21245)]
  Adapted BERT-style masked modeling with functional token prediction for time series, pre-trained on 260B time points for understanding tasks including classification and anomaly detection.

- **Chronos-2: From Univariate to Universal Forecasting** — arXiv 2025 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15821)] [[Code](https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting)]
  Extended Chronos to support univariate, multivariate, and covariate-informed forecasting via group attention, delivering 300+ forecasts per second on a single A10G GPU.

- **HDT: Hierarchical Discrete Transformer for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting** — AAAI 2025 [`Transformer`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08302)] [[Code](https://github.com/hdtkk/HDT)]
  Modeled time series into discrete token representations via vector quantization, generating forecasts hierarchically from coarse long-term trends to fine-grained details.

- **xPatch: Dual-Stream Time Series Forecasting with Exponential Seasonal-Trend Decomposition** — AAAI 2025 [`MLP`] [`CNN`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17323)] [[Code](https://github.com/stitsyuk/xPatch)]
  Proposed EMA-based exponential decomposition with a dual-stream architecture (MLP linear + CNN non-linear) and arctangent loss for robust forecasting.

- **ARMD: Auto-Regressive Moving Diffusion Models for Time Series Forecasting** — AAAI 2025 [`Diffusion`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09328)] [[Code](https://github.com/daxin007/ARMD)]
  Proposed chain-based diffusion with ARMA-inspired priors for continuous sequential forecasting, outperforming existing diffusion-based TSF models.

- **K²VAE: A Koopman-Kalman Enhanced Variational AutoEncoder for Probabilistic Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2025 Spotlight [`Probabilistic`] [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23017)] [[Code](https://github.com/decisionintelligence/K2VAE)]
  Leveraged Koopman operators to linearize nonlinear dynamics and Kalman filtering to refine predictions with uncertainty quantification, reducing error accumulation.

- **NsDiff: Non-stationary Diffusion for Probabilistic Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2025 [`Diffusion`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04278)] [[Code](https://github.com/wwy155/NsDiff)]
  Introduced Location-Scale Noise Model to capture non-stationary uncertainty, with adaptive noise schedules improving probabilistic forecasting by up to 66% over baselines.

- **TimeFilter: Patch-Specific Spatial-Temporal Graph Filtration for Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2025 [`GNN`] [`Frequency`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13041)] [[Code](https://github.com/TROUBADOUR000/TimeFilter)]
  Applied MoE-style dynamic routing on spatial-temporal graphs to filter irrelevant patch-level dependencies while preserving critical correlations.

- **CNDiff: Conditional Diffusion Model with Nonlinear Data Transformation for Time Series Forecasting** — ICML 2025 [`Diffusion`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=kcUNMKqrCg)] [[Code](https://github.com/quest-lab-iisc/CNDiff)]
  Employed nonlinear data transformations and learnable conditions in the forward diffusion process, outperforming leading models on nine real-world datasets.

- **Time-o1: Time-Series Forecasting Needs Transformed Label Alignment** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17847)]
  Proposed transformation-augmented learning that decorrelates label sequences and aligns the most significant components, achieving SOTA across diverse models.

- **Moirai 2.0: When Less Is More for Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv 2025 [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11698)] [[Code](https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/uni2ts)]
  Simplified Moirai with a decoder-only architecture, single patch, and quantile loss, achieving 2x speed and 30x smaller model size while improving accuracy.
