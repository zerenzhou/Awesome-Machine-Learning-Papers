# Time Series Forecasting — 2026

- **Aurora: Towards Universal Generative Multimodal Time Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2026 [`Foundation Model`] [`Multimodal`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22295)] [[Code](https://github.com/decisionintelligence/Aurora)]
  First multimodal time series foundation model that fuses text/image context with temporal data via flow-matching generative decoding, achieving 27% MSE reduction over Sundial.

- **ms-Mamba: Multi-scale Mamba for Time-Series Forecasting** — ICLR 2026 [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07654)]
  Incorporated multiple temporal scales via parallel Mamba blocks with different sampling rates, outperforming Transformer and Mamba baselines with fewer parameters and less memory.

- **SE-LLM: Semantic-Enhanced Time-Series Forecasting via Large Language Models** — ICLR 2026 [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07697)]
  Bridged the modality gap between LLM linguistic knowledge and time series patterns via Temporal-Semantic Cross-Correlation and Time-Adapter modules while freezing the LLM backbone.

- **CoRA: Covariate-Aware Adaptation of Time Series Foundation Models** — ICLR 2026 [`Foundation Model`] [`Multimodal`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12681)]
  General covariate-aware adaptation framework for TSFMs that fuses exogenous signals across modalities (time series, text, image) into a frozen foundation backbone, achieving 31.1% MSE reduction over SOTA covariate-aware deep forecasters.

- **EMAformer: Enhancing Transformer through Embedding Armor for Time Series Forecasting** — AAAI 2026 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08396)] [[Code](https://github.com/PlanckChang/EMAformer)]
  Enhanced Transformers with channel, phase, and joint channel-phase embeddings, reducing forecasting errors by 2.73% MSE and 5.15% MAE on 12 benchmarks.

- **APN: Rethinking Irregular Time Series Forecasting: A Simple yet Effective Baseline** — AAAI 2026 Oral [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11250)] [[Code](https://github.com/decisionintelligence/APN)]
  Proposed Time-Aware Patch Aggregation for adaptive patching with learnable boundaries, transforming irregular sequences into regularized representations.

- **OccamVTS: Distilling Vision Models to 1% Parameters for Time Series Forecasting** — AAAI 2026 [`Foundation Model`] [`MLP`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01727)]
  Distilled large vision models into lightweight networks retaining only 1% of parameters via pyramid-style feature alignment, excelling in few-shot and zero-shot scenarios.

- **NeuralOM: Neural Ocean Model for Subseasonal-to-Seasonal Simulation** — AAAI 2026 [`Graph`] [`Probabilistic`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21020)] [[Code](https://github.com/YuanGao-YG/NeuralOM)]
  Neural operator for slow-changing physical dynamics combining progressive residual correction with a physics-guided graph network, achieving 13.3% RMSE reduction at 60-day lead time on global ocean S2S forecasting.

- **GTR: Enhancing Multivariate Time Series Forecasting with Global Temporal Retrieval** — arXiv 2026 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10847)] [[Code](https://github.com/macovaseas/GTR)]
  A plug-and-play module that maintains adaptive global temporal embeddings and dynamically retrieves relevant segments to extend any model's temporal awareness beyond the input window.

- **ROSE: Forecasting with Regularities on Sparse Embeddings** — AAAI 2026 [`MLP`] [`Representation Learning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11590)] [[Code](https://github.com/OrigamiSL/ROSE)]
  Captured dominant temporal regularities through sparse frequency-domain embeddings, surpassing Transformer and MLP baselines with negligible parameter overhead on LTSF benchmarks.

- **Spatio-temporal Modeling and Forecasting with Fourier Neural Operators** — arXiv 2026 [`Neural Operator`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01813)]
  Recast FNOs as a statistical dynamical spatio-temporal model: the FNO approximates the (possibly unknown) PDE solution operator, and forecasts are produced without an explicit PDE specification — benchmarked against state-of-the-art statistical spatio-temporal methods on simulations from a nonlinear PDE, positioning neural operators as a drop-in replacement for classical geostatistical forecasting.

- **PGMNO: A Physics-Guided Mamba Neural Operator Framework for Partial Differential Equations** — Neural Networks 2026 [`Neural Operator`] [`SSM`]
  [[Paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0893608026003072)]
  Coupled Mamba's latent evolution with an implicit BDF multistep integration scheme plus a causality-aware learning strategy, turning classical linear-multistep stability criteria into a physics-consistent inductive bias — improves long-horizon prediction accuracy, data efficiency, and resolution-invariant generalization over prior neural operators.

- **Integrated Spatio-temporal Modeling with Hybrid Graph Convolutions and the Graph Fourier Neural Operator for Traffic Prediction** — Scientific Reports 2026 [`Neural Operator`] [`Graph`]
  [[Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-38563-y)]
  Combined GCN-based local spatial aggregation with a graph Fourier neural operator for unified global temporal modeling on non-Euclidean road networks, addressing the decoupled spatial-temporal learning and scalability limits of RNN/Transformer/SSM traffic forecasters in long-horizon settings.
