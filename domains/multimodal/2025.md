# Multimodal Learning — 2025

- **Qwen2-VL: Enhancing Vision-Language Model's Perception of the World at Any Resolution** — arXiv 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12191)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen2-VL)]
  Introduced dynamic resolution processing with naive dynamic resolution and multimodal rotary position embedding, achieving GPT-4o level performance on multimodal benchmarks.

- **Qwen2.5-VL Technical Report** — arXiv 2025 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13923)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen2.5-VL)]
  Extended Qwen2-VL with dynamic resolution and absolute time encoding for hour-long videos, enabling second-level event localization and agentic visual interaction.

- **InternVL 2.5 / InternVL3: Exploring Advanced Training and Test-Time Recipes for Open-Source Multimodal Models** — arXiv 2025 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10479)] [[Code](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVL)]
  Scaled InternVL to competitive performance with GPT-4o and Qwen2.5-VL through advanced training recipes and test-time strategies.

- **LLaVA-OneVision: Easy Visual Task Transfer** — arXiv 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03326)] [[Code](https://github.com/LLaVA-VL/LLaVA-NeXT)]
  First single model to push performance boundaries across single-image, multi-image, and video scenarios via task transfer.

- **Molmo and PixMo: Open Weights and Open Data for State-of-the-Art Multimodal Models** — CVPR 2025 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17146)] [[Code](https://github.com/allenai/molmo)]
  Fully open (weights and data) VLM family trained on the novel PixMo dataset, surpassing proprietary models on academic benchmarks.

- **DeepSeek-VL2: Mixture-of-Experts Vision-Language Models for Advanced Multimodal Understanding** — arXiv 2024 [`VLM`] [`MoE`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10302)] [[Code](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-VL2)]
  Combined dynamic tiling vision encoding with DeepSeek-MoE and multi-head latent attention, achieving competitive performance with only 4.5B activated parameters.

- **Phi-4-Mini Technical Report: Compact yet Powerful Multimodal Language Models via Mixture-of-LoRAs** — arXiv 2025 [`VLM`] [`Efficient`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01743)] [[Code](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct)]
  Introduced mixture-of-LoRAs to integrate text, vision, and speech modalities into a compact 3.8B model while keeping the base LLM frozen.

- **Janus: Decoupling Visual Encoding for Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation** — CVPR 2025 [`Unified`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13848)] [[Code](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/Janus)]
  Decoupled visual encoding into separate understanding and generation pathways within a single unified transformer architecture.

- **Janus-Pro: Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation with Data and Model Scaling** — arXiv 2025 [`Unified`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17811)] [[Code](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/Janus)]
  Scaled up Janus with optimized training strategies and expanded data, improving text-to-image instruction following and multimodal understanding.

- **Show-o: One Single Transformer to Unify Multimodal Understanding and Generation** — ICLR 2025 [`Unified`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12528)] [[Code](https://github.com/showlab/Show-o)]
  Unified autoregressive and discrete diffusion modeling in a single transformer for both multimodal understanding and image generation.

- **Transfusion: Predict the Next Token and Diffuse Images with One Multi-Modal Model** — ICLR 2025 [`Unified`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11039)] [[Code](https://github.com/lucidrains/transfusion-pytorch)]
  Combined next-token prediction for text and diffusion for images in one transformer, establishing scaling laws for mixed-modality generation.

- **VITA-1.5: Towards GPT-4o Level Real-Time Vision and Speech Interaction** — NeurIPS 2025 [`Unified`] [`Video`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01957)] [[Code](https://github.com/VITA-MLLM/VITA)]
  Achieved real-time vision and speech interaction through three-stage progressive training, reducing end-to-end speech latency to 1.5 seconds.
