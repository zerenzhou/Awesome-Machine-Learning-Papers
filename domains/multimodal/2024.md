# Multimodal Learning — 2024

- **LLaVA-NeXT: Improved Reasoning, OCR, and World Knowledge** — CVPR 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06209)] [[Code](https://github.com/LLaVA-VL/LLaVA-NeXT)]
  Scaled LLaVA to higher-resolution inputs and stronger LLM backbones, significantly improving OCR, reasoning, and world knowledge.

- **InternVL: Scaling up Vision Foundation Models and Aligning for Generic Visual-Linguistic Tasks** — CVPR 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14238)] [[Code](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVL)]
  Scaled a vision-language foundation model to 6B vision parameters and aligned it with LLMs for strong multimodal performance.

- **Cambrian-1: A Fully Open, Vision-Centric Exploration of Multimodal LLMs** — NeurIPS 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16860)] [[Code](https://github.com/cambrian-mllm/cambrian)]
  Systematically studied vision encoder design for multimodal LLMs, proposing a dynamic visual token routing strategy.

- **Idefics2: An 8B Foundation Vision-Language Model** — arXiv 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02246)] [[Code](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceM4/idefics2-8b)]
  Built an efficient 8B open vision-language model with native resolution handling and improved OCR through simplified architecture.

- **LLaVA-Video: Video Instruction Tuning with Synthetic Data** — arXiv 2024 [`Video`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02713)] [[Code](https://github.com/LLaVA-VL/LLaVA-NeXT)]
  Extended LLaVA-NeXT to video understanding through length-generalized video representation and high-quality synthetic video instruction data.

- **Emu2: Generative Multimodal Models are In-Context Learners** — CVPR 2024 [`Unified`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.13286)] [[Code](https://github.com/baaivision/Emu)]
  Demonstrated that a large-scale generative multimodal model can perform strong in-context learning across vision-language tasks.

- **Unified-IO 2: Scaling Autoregressive Multimodal Models with Vision, Language, Audio, and Action** — CVPR 2024 [`Unified`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.17172)] [[Code](https://github.com/allenai/unified-io-2)]
  Trained a single autoregressive model that understands and generates across images, text, audio, and actions.

- **4M: Massively Multimodal Masked Modeling** — NeurIPS 2024 [`Unified`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06647)] [[Code](https://github.com/apple/ml-4m)]
  Proposed a single transformer trained with masked modeling across diverse modalities and tasks, enabling any-to-any prediction.

- **OneLLM: One Framework to Align All Modalities with Language** — CVPR 2024 [`Unified`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03700)] [[Code](https://github.com/csuhan/OneLLM)]
  Unified eight modalities into a single LLM framework using a universal projection module and progressive multimodal alignment.

- **NExT-GPT: Any-to-Any Multimodal Large Language Model** — ICML 2024 [`Unified`] [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05519)] [[Code](https://github.com/NExT-GPT/NExT-GPT)]
  Connected an LLM with multimodal encoders and decoders to enable arbitrary input-to-output modality generation via lightweight alignment.
