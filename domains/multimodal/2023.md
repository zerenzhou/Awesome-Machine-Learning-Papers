# Multimodal Learning — 2023

- **BLIP-2: Bootstrapping Language-Image Pre-training with Frozen Image Encoders and Large Language Models** — ICML 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.12597)] [[Code](https://github.com/salesforce/LAVIS)]
  Bridged frozen image encoders and LLMs with a lightweight Querying Transformer for efficient vision-language pre-training.

- **LLaVA: Visual Instruction Tuning** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08485)] [[Code](https://github.com/haotian-liu/LLaVA)]
  Connected a vision encoder to an LLM via visual instruction tuning for multimodal chat and reasoning.

- **ImageBind: One Embedding Space to Bind Them All** — CVPR 2023 [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05665)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ImageBind)]
  Learned a joint embedding space across six modalities (image, text, audio, depth, thermal, IMU) using image-paired data.

- **Improved Baselines with Visual Instruction Tuning (LLaVA-1.5)** — CVPR 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03744)] [[Code](https://github.com/haotian-liu/LLaVA)]
  Strengthened LLaVA with an MLP vision-language connector and academic-task-oriented data, achieving strong performance with simple modifications.

- **InstructBLIP: Towards General-purpose Vision-Language Models with Instruction Tuning** — NeurIPS 2023 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06500)] [[Code](https://github.com/salesforce/LAVIS)]
  Extended BLIP-2 with vision-language instruction tuning, enabling a general-purpose model that follows diverse multimodal instructions.

- **MiniGPT-4: Enhancing Vision-Language Understanding with Advanced Large Language Models** — ICLR 2024 [`VLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.10592)] [[Code](https://github.com/Vision-CAIR/MiniGPT-4)]
  Aligned a frozen visual encoder with Vicuna using a single projection layer and two-stage training for multimodal dialogue.

- **Qwen-VL: A Versatile Vision-Language Model for Understanding, Localization, Text Reading, and Beyond** — arXiv 2023 [`VLM`] [`Grounding`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12966)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-VL)]
  Introduced a vision-language model supporting multi-image input, grounding, and text reading via multi-task pre-training.

- **Video-LLaMA: An Instruction-tuned Audio-Visual Language Model for Video Understanding** — EMNLP 2023 Demo [`Video`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02858)] [[Code](https://github.com/DAMO-NLP-SG/Video-LLaMA)]
  Empowered LLMs to understand both visual and auditory content in videos via Video Q-Former and Audio Q-Former modules.

- **VideoChat: Chat-Centric Video Understanding** — CVPR 2024 [`Video`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06355)] [[Code](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/Ask-Anything)]
  Combined a video foundation model with an LLM for spatiotemporal reasoning and chat-centric video understanding.

- **DALL-E 3** — arXiv 2023 [`Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/dall-e-3.pdf)]
  Improved text-to-image generation fidelity and prompt following by training on highly descriptive synthetic captions.

- **Kosmos-2: Grounding Multimodal Large Language Models to the World** — ICLR 2024 [`Grounding`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14824)] [[Code](https://github.com/microsoft/unilm/tree/master/kosmos-2)]
  Enabled a multimodal LLM to perceive object descriptions and ground language to visual regions via a linking representation.

- **MMBench: Is Your Multi-modal Model an All-around Player?** — ECCV 2024 [`Benchmark`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06281)] [[Code](https://github.com/open-compass/MMBench)]
  Proposed a systematically designed benchmark covering 20 fine-grained ability dimensions for evaluating vision-language models.
