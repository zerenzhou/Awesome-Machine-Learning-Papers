# Speech & Audio — 2021

- **HuBERT: Self-Supervised Speech Representation Learning by Masked Prediction of Hidden Units** — TASLP 2021 [`ASR`] [`Self-Supervised`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07447)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairseq)]
  Applied masked prediction on offline cluster assignments for self-supervised speech representation learning.

- **VITS: Conditional Variational Autoencoder with Adversarial Learning for End-to-End Text-to-Speech** — ICML 2021 [`TTS`] [`VAE`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06103)] [[Code](https://github.com/jaywalnut310/vits)]
  Combined VAE, normalizing flows, and adversarial training in a single-stage end-to-end TTS model for natural and diverse speech synthesis.

- **DiffWave: A Versatile Diffusion Model for Audio Synthesis** — ICLR 2021 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09761)] [[Code](https://github.com/lmnt-com/diffwave)]
  Applied diffusion probabilistic models to raw audio waveform synthesis with high fidelity.

- **FastSpeech 2: Fast and High-Quality End-to-End Text to Speech** — ICLR 2021 [`TTS`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04558)] [[Code](https://github.com/ming024/FastSpeech2)]
  Extended FastSpeech with direct mel spectrogram training and variance adaptors for pitch, energy, and duration, improving quality and simplicity.

- **Grad-TTS: A Diffusion Probabilistic Model for Text-to-Speech** — ICML 2021 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06337)] [[Code](https://github.com/huawei-noah/Speech-Backbones/tree/main/Grad-TTS)]
  Formulated TTS as a score-based diffusion process from encoder-predicted noise to mel spectrograms with monotonic alignment.

- **SoundStream: An End-to-End Neural Audio Codec** — TASLP 2022 [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03312)]
  Proposed a fully neural audio codec with residual vector quantization for scalable speech and music compression at low bitrates.
