# Speech & Audio — 2022

- **EnCodec: High Fidelity Neural Audio Compression** — arXiv 2022 [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13438)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/encodec)]
  Proposed a streaming neural audio codec using residual vector quantization and end-to-end training for real-time high-fidelity audio compression.

- **AudioLM: a Language Modeling Approach to Audio Generation** — arXiv 2022 [`Audio Generation`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03143)]
  Generated coherent audio by hierarchically modeling semantic and acoustic tokens with language models, without any text supervision.

- **NaturalSpeech: End-to-End Text to Speech Synthesis with Human-Level Quality** — arXiv 2022 [`TTS`] [`VAE`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04421)]
  Achieved human-level speech quality on LJSpeech via a VAE-based system with phoneme pre-training, differentiable duration, and bidirectional prior/posterior modeling.
