# Speech & Audio — 2017

- **Tacotron: Towards End-to-End Speech Synthesis** — INTERSPEECH 2017 [`TTS`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.10135)]
  Proposed an end-to-end sequence-to-sequence model generating mel spectrograms from characters, simplifying the traditional TTS pipeline.

- **SampleRNN: An Unconditional End-to-End Neural Audio Generation Model** — ICLR 2017 [`Audio Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07837)] [[Code](https://github.com/deepsound-project/samplernn-pytorch)]
  Modeled raw audio waveforms at multiple temporal resolutions with hierarchical RNNs for unconditional audio generation.
