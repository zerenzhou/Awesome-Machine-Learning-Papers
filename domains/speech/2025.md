# Speech & Audio — 2025

- **MaskGCT: Zero-Shot Text-to-Speech with Masked Generative Codec Transformer** — ICLR 2025 [`TTS`] [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00750)] [[Code](https://github.com/open-mmlab/Amphion/tree/main/models/tts/maskgct)]
  Fully non-autoregressive TTS using mask-and-predict learning over semantic and acoustic tokens, eliminating explicit text-speech alignment.

- **WavTokenizer: An Efficient Acoustic Discrete Codec Tokenizer for Audio Language Models** — ICLR 2025 [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16532)] [[Code](https://github.com/jishengpeng/WavTokenizer)]
  Designed a single-codebook codec tokenizer achieving competitive audio reconstruction quality with significantly fewer tokens per second.

- **DiTTo-TTS: Diffusion Transformers for Scalable Text-to-Speech without Domain-Specific Factors** — ICLR 2025 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11427)]
  Applied a Diffusion Transformer to scalable zero-shot TTS without speech domain-specific factors, achieving 4.6x faster inference than comparable autoregressive models.

- **HALL-E: Hierarchical Neural Codec Language Model for Minute-Long Zero-Shot Text-to-Speech Synthesis** — ICLR 2025 [`TTS`] [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04380)]
  Introduced Multi-Resolution Requantization to reduce codec frame rates to 8 Hz, enabling stable minute-long zero-shot speech synthesis in a single step.

- **Presto! Distilling Steps and Layers for Accelerating Music Generation** — ICLR 2025 Spotlight [`Music`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05167)]
  Combined score-based distribution matching distillation with layer distillation to accelerate music generation 10-18x, producing 32s of 44.1kHz stereo in under 0.5s.

- **DiTAR: Diffusion Transformer Autoregressive Modeling for Speech Generation** — ICML 2025 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03930)]
  A patch-based autoregressive framework combining a language model with a diffusion transformer for state-of-the-art zero-shot speech generation.

- **Spark-TTS: An Efficient LLM-Based Text-to-Speech Model with Single-Stream Decoupled Speech Tokens** — arXiv 2025 [`TTS`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01710)] [[Code](https://github.com/SparkAudio/Spark-TTS)]
  Introduced BiCodec to decouple speech into low-bitrate semantic and fixed-length global tokens, enabling controllable TTS via chain-of-thought LLM decoding.

- **Step-Audio: Unified Understanding and Generation in Intelligent Speech Interaction** — arXiv 2025 [`LLM`] [`Speech Interaction`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11946)] [[Code](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step-Audio)]
  A 130B-parameter unified speech-text model combining comprehension and generation with instruction-driven control over dialects, emotions, and singing.

- **Qwen2.5-Omni: End-to-End Multimodal Model for Text, Audio, Vision, and Video** — arXiv 2025 [`LLM`] [`Multimodal`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20215)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen2.5-Omni)]
  Proposed a Thinker-Talker architecture for concurrent text and speech generation with streaming multimodal input via TMRoPE.
