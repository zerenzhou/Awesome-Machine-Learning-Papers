# Speech & Audio — 2023

- **Whisper: Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision** — ICML 2023 [`ASR`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04356)] [[Code](https://github.com/openai/whisper)]
  Trained a large-scale Transformer on 680K hours of weakly supervised audio for robust multilingual speech recognition.

- **VALL-E: Neural Codec Language Models are Zero-Shot Text to Speech Synthesizers** — arXiv 2023 [`TTS`] [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02111)]
  Treated TTS as a language modeling task over discrete audio codec codes, enabling zero-shot voice cloning from a 3-second prompt.

- **Voicebox: Text-Guided Multilingual Universal Speech Generation at Scale** — NeurIPS 2023 [`TTS`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15687)]
  Proposed a non-autoregressive flow-matching model for large-scale speech generation supporting style transfer, editing, and denoising.

- **NaturalSpeech 2: Latent Diffusion Models are Natural and Zero-Shot Speech and Singing Synthesizers** — ICLR 2024 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09116)]
  Combined a neural audio codec with latent diffusion to achieve zero-shot speech and singing synthesis with high naturalness.

- **MusicLM: Generating Music From Text** — arXiv 2023 [`Music`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.11325)]
  Generated high-fidelity music from text descriptions by hierarchically modeling audio tokens from MuLan and SoundStream representations.

- **MusicGen: Simple and Controllable Music Generation** — NeurIPS 2023 [`Music`] [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05284)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft)]
  Introduced an efficient single-stage Transformer-based music generation model with codebook interleaving patterns over EnCodec tokens.

- **AudioPaLM: A Large Language Model That Can Speak and Listen** — arXiv 2023 [`LLM`] [`ASR`] [`TTS`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12925)]
  Fused PaLM-2 text and AudioLM speech capabilities into a unified multimodal LLM for speech-to-speech translation and ASR.

- **Pengi: An Audio Language Model for Audio Tasks** — NeurIPS 2023 [`LLM`] [`Audio Understanding`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11834)]
  Framed all audio tasks as text-generation problems by combining an audio encoder with a text decoder for open-ended audio reasoning.
