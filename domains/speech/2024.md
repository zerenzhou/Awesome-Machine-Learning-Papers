# Speech & Audio — 2024

- **NaturalSpeech 2: Latent Diffusion Models are Natural and Zero-Shot Speech and Singing Synthesizers** — ICLR 2024 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09116)]
  Combined a neural audio codec with latent diffusion to achieve zero-shot speech and singing synthesis with high naturalness.

- **NaturalSpeech 3: Zero-Shot Speech Synthesis with Factorized Codec and Diffusion Models** — ICML 2024 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03100)]
  Disentangled speech into content, prosody, timbre, and acoustic subspaces via factorized vector quantization, generating each with dedicated diffusion models.

- **CosyVoice: A Scalable Multilingual Zero-Shot Text-to-Speech Synthesizer** — arXiv 2024 [`TTS`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05407)] [[Code](https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice)]
  Combined supervised semantic tokens with a flow-matching generative model for scalable multilingual zero-shot TTS with fine-grained control.

- **CosyVoice 2: Scalable Streaming Speech Synthesis with Large Language Models** — arXiv 2024 [`TTS`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10117)] [[Code](https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice)]
  Replaced vector quantization with finite-scalar quantization and introduced chunk-aware causal flow matching for human-parity streaming TTS.

- **F5-TTS: A Fairytaler that Fakes Fluent and Faithful Speech with Flow Matching** — arXiv 2024 [`TTS`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06885)] [[Code](https://github.com/SWivid/F5-TTS)]
  Proposed a fully non-autoregressive, text-guided flow-matching TTS system that achieves natural prosody without explicit duration modeling.

- **Seed-TTS: A Family of High-Quality Versatile Speech Generation Models** — arXiv 2024 [`TTS`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02430)]
  Large-scale autoregressive TTS achieving human-parity naturalness and speaker similarity, with a diffusion variant for speech editing.

- **Fish-Speech: Leveraging Large Language Models for Advanced Multilingual Text-to-Speech Synthesis** — arXiv 2024 [`TTS`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01156)] [[Code](https://github.com/fishaudio/fish-speech)]
  Dual autoregressive architecture with grouped finite scalar vector quantization, using LLMs for linguistic features without grapheme-to-phoneme conversion.

- **VALL-E 2: Neural Codec Language Models are Human Parity Zero-Shot Text to Speech Synthesizers** — arXiv 2024 [`TTS`] [`Codec`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05370)]
  Achieved human-parity zero-shot TTS via repetition-aware sampling and grouped code modeling to improve robustness and shorten codec sequences.

- **SpeechTokenizer: Unified Speech Tokenizer for Speech Language Models** — ICLR 2024 [`Codec`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16692)] [[Code](https://github.com/ZhangXInFD/SpeechTokenizer)]
  Disentangled semantic and acoustic information into hierarchical RVQ layers, unifying content and speaker tokens for speech language models.

- **Moshi: a Speech-Text Foundation Model for Real-Time Dialogue** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`] [`Speech Interaction`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00037)] [[Code](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/moshi)]
  Full-duplex speech-text model with parallel streams for speech and inner monologue text, enabling real-time spoken dialogue with 160ms theoretical latency.

- **SALMONN: Towards Generic Hearing Abilities for Large Language Models** — ICLR 2024 [`LLM`] [`Audio Understanding`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13289)] [[Code](https://github.com/bytedance/SALMONN)]
  Equipped an LLM with dual auditory encoders (Whisper + BEATs) and a window-level Q-Former for general-purpose audio and speech understanding.

- **Qwen-Audio: Advancing Universal Audio Understanding via Unified Large-Scale Audio-Language Models** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`] [`Audio Understanding`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07919)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-Audio)]
  Scaled audio-language pre-training across 30+ tasks and diverse audio types to build a versatile audio understanding foundation model.

- **Qwen2-Audio: Advancing Universal Audio Understanding** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`] [`Audio Understanding`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10759)] [[Code](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen2-Audio)]
  Scaled audio-language pre-training with natural language prompts across diverse audio types, supporting voice chat and audio analysis modes.

- **GLM-4-Voice: Towards Intelligent and Human-Like End-to-End Spoken Chatbot** — arXiv 2024 [`LLM`] [`Speech Interaction`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02612)] [[Code](https://github.com/THUDM/GLM-4-Voice)]
  End-to-end spoken chatbot using an ultra-low bitrate single-codebook tokenizer and flow-matching decoder, pre-trained on 1T interleaved speech-text tokens.

- **Stable Audio Open** — arXiv 2024 [`Music`] [`Diffusion`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14358)] [[Code](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-tools)]
  Applied latent diffusion with a timing-conditioned U-Net to generate variable-length stereo audio from text prompts.
