# Speech & Audio — 2018

- **Natural TTS Synthesis by Conditioning WaveNet on Mel Spectrogram Predictions** — ICASSP 2018 [`TTS`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.05884)]
  Proposed Tacotron 2, combining a sequence-to-sequence mel spectrogram predictor with a modified WaveNet vocoder for near-human speech quality.

- **Parallel WaveNet: Fast High-Fidelity Speech Synthesis** — ICML 2018 [`TTS`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10433)]
  Distilled autoregressive WaveNet into a parallel feed-forward model via probability density distillation for real-time high-fidelity synthesis.

- **Transfer Learning from Speaker Verification to Multispeaker Text-to-Speech Synthesis** — NeurIPS 2018 [`TTS`] [`Voice Cloning`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.04558)] [[Code](https://github.com/CorentinJ/Real-Time-Voice-Cloning)]
  Enabled multispeaker TTS by conditioning a Tacotron 2 model on speaker embeddings from a separately trained speaker verification network.
