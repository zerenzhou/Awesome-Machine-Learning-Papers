# Speech & Audio — 2020

- **Conformer: Convolution-augmented Transformer for Speech Recognition** — INTERSPEECH 2020 [`ASR`] [`Transformer`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08100)]
  Combined Transformer self-attention with convolution modules to capture both global and local audio features.

- **Glow-TTS: A Generative Flow for Text-to-Speech via Monotonic Alignment Search** — NeurIPS 2020 [`TTS`] [`Flow`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11129)] [[Code](https://github.com/jaywalnut310/glow-tts)]
  Proposed a flow-based TTS model with monotonic alignment search for parallel synthesis, enabling diverse and controllable speech generation.
