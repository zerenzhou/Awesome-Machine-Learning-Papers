# Speech & Audio — 2026

- **Latent Speech-Text Transformer** — ICLR 2026 Oral [`ASR`] [`LLM`] [`Foundation Model`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06195)] [[Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/lst)]
  Aggregated discrete speech tokens into latent speech patches aligned with textual units, improving compute efficiency of speech-text pre-training while enabling cross-modal knowledge transfer.

- **CALM: Continuous Audio Language Models** — ICLR 2026 [`TTS`] [`Audio Generation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06926)] [[Code](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts)]
  Replaced discrete audio tokens with continuous VAE frames generated via consistency modeling, achieving higher fidelity at lower cost for speech and music generation.

- **TASTE: Text-Aligned Speech Tokenization and Embedding for Spoken Language Modeling** — ICLR 2026 [`Codec`] [`LLM`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07053)] [[Code](https://github.com/mtkresearch/TASTE-SpokenLM)]
  Aligned speech tokens with text transcriptions via attention-based aggregation, reducing sequence length while preserving paralinguistic information.

- **Echo: Towards Advanced Audio Comprehension via Audio-Interleaved Reasoning** — ICLR 2026 [`LLM`] [`Audio Understanding`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11909)] [[Code](https://github.com/wdqqdw/Echo)]
  Proposed audio-interleaved reasoning for large audio language models, treating audio as an active reasoning component with sustained engagement.
