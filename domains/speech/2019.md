# Speech & Audio — 2019

- **FastSpeech: Fast, Robust and Controllable Text to Speech** — NeurIPS 2019 [`TTS`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.09263)]
  Introduced a non-autoregressive feed-forward Transformer for TTS with explicit duration prediction, achieving 270x speedup over autoregressive models.

- **SpecAugment: A Simple Data Augmentation Method for Automatic Speech Recognition** — INTERSPEECH 2019 [`ASR`] [`Data Augmentation`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08779)]
  Applied simple spectrogram masking and warping as data augmentation, significantly improving ASR performance without extra data.

- **wav2vec: Unsupervised Pre-training for Speech Recognition** — INTERSPEECH 2019 [`ASR`] [`Self-Supervised`]
  [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05862)]
  Pioneered unsupervised pre-training of speech representations via contrastive learning on raw audio, establishing the wav2vec framework.
